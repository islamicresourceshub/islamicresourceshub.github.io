Tallafin Hajji: Kafin a sanya al'ummar Musulmi a matsayin wadanda ake zargi

പി.വി.എ പ്രിംറോസ്‌ · 2017-01-28 · Opinion & Analysis

Akwai wata kalma a harshen Ingilishi da ake kira 'Weeping Boy' (yaro mai kuka). A zahiri, wadannan yaran ba su ne wadanda suka aikata laifin da ya sa aka doke su ba. Wannan kalmar ta samo asali ne daga wani mummunan al'ada da aka yi a karni na 16, inda ake amfani da wasu yara marasa galihu a matsayin wadanda za a yi wa horo a madadin 'ya'yan sarakuna idan sun yi sakaci a karatunsu. Wasu matakan da gwamnatoci ke dauka a yau suna tunatar da mu wadannan 'Weeping Boys', inda ake sanya wasu a matsayin wadanda za a dora wa laifin da ba nasu ba.

Sabon takaddamar tallafin Hajji

Abin da muke magana a kai shi ne sabon takaddamar tallafin Hajji. A kowace shekara yayin lokacin Hajji, kanun labarai guda biyu ne suke yawaita a kafafen yada labarai: daya shi ne 'Maraba da Mahajjata', dayan kuma shi ne 'A dakatar da tallafin Hajji'. Wadanda ke kiran a dakatar da tallafin suna sauyawa bisa ga yanayi; wani lokaci kungiyoyin Sang Parivar ne, wani lokaci kuma umarni ne daga wani ministan tarayya ko alkalin kotun koli. Kwanan nan, ministan kananan hukumomi da walwalar tsiraru na jihar Kerala ne ya sake tayar da wannan maganar a wani taron manema labarai a Delhi.

Ra'ayin ministan ba na kashin kansa ba ne. Ya dogara ne ga shawarar kwamitin mutane shida da gwamnatin tarayya ta kafa, biyo bayan hukuncin kotun koli na shekarar 2012 da alkalan kotun koli Justice Aftab Alam da Justice Ranjana P. Desai suka yanke, wanda ya bukaci a rage tallafin Hajji a hankali har sai an dakatar da shi gaba daya nan da shekarar 2022.

"Idan mutum bai amince ba, kada a yi amfani da kudin da ya hada da nasa wajen tallafa wa aikin Hajji. Hajji wajibi ne ga wadanda suke da kudi da lafiya. Ba aikin da za a yi da kudin wani ba ne."

Ko da yake ba za a yi shakkar kyakkyawar manufar ministan ba, amma abin mamaki ne yadda yake saurin mayar da martani a kowane batun da ya shafi sanya al'ummar Musulmi a matsayin wadanda ake zargi, kamar batun fitila, ziyarar Sabarimala, gemun 'yan sanda, ko sunayen gine-ginen gwamnati.

Tarihin tallafin Hajji

Gwamnatin Saudiyya tana samar da hanyoyi uku na zuwa aikin Hajji: ta ruwa, ta sama, da kuma ta kasa. A Indiya, tafiye-tafiyen ta sama da ta ruwa ne aka fi amfani da su. Bayan shekarar 1995, gwamnati ta dakatar da zirga-zirgar jiragen ruwa kuma ta mayar da hankali kan zirga-zirgar jiragen sama.

Tallafin Hajji ya samo asali ne tun zamanin mulkin mallaka. A shekarar 1932, tare da kafa dokar The Port Haj Committee Act, aka fara ba da rangwame ga mahajjata ta tashoshin jiragen ruwa na Bombay da Calcutta. Bayan samun 'yancin kai a shekarar 1959, gwamnati ta ci gaba da wannan tsarin ba tare da wani matsin lamba ko nuna fifiko ga Musulmi ba.

Menene tallafin? Shin kowane mahajjaci yana samun tallafi? A'a. Kananan kashi ne kawai na mahajjata suke samun damar tafiya ta tsarin gwamnati, kuma zabinsu yana da wahala. Wannan tallafin yana iyakance ne kawai ga kudin jirgin sama. Sauran kudaden abinci, masauki, da sauransu, mahajjata ne suke biya da kansu. Tambayar ita ce: shin tallafin ga mahajjata ne ko ga kamfanin jirgin sama?

Tallafin Hajji a matsayin hanyar tallafa wa Air India

Idan aka binciki tallafin Hajji, za a ga cewa wata hanya ce ta ceto kamfanin Air India daga faduwa. Gwamnatin Indiya ta ba wa Air India damar gudanar da zirga-zirgar mahajjata ba tare da yin gasa ba. Idan aka duba yadda ake tsara kudaden jirgin, za a ga ana amfani da lokacin Hajji wajen kara kudin jirgi fiye da kima. Misali, kudin jirgi daga Karipur zuwa Jeddah a watan Fabrairun 2017 ya kai 14,126 rupees, amma a lokacin Hajji, kudin yana hawa sama da 47,000 ko 54,000 rupees. Tallafin da gwamnati ke bayarwa yana komawa ne ga Air India. A takaice, gwamnati tana kawai daukar kudi daga aljihun hagu tana sakawa a aljihun dama!

Fashi da rana a sunan tallafi

Abin da muka karanta a sama shi ne gaskiyar lamarin tallafin Hajji. Zargin cewa ana amfani da kudin harajin sauran addinai wajen tallafa wa Musulmi ba gaskiya ba ne. Maimakon yin shiru, ya kamata a mayar da martani yadda ya kamata. Shawarar dan majalisa Asaduddin Owaisi na cewa a dakatar da tallafin a kuma ba da kudin don ilimin 'yan matan Musulmi, shawara ce mai ma'ana.

Karya ce a ce Musulmi ne kawai suke samun tallafi. Gwamnati tana ba da tallafi ga kowane irin tafiye-tafiyen addini zuwa kasashen waje. Misali, ana ba da tallafi ga mahajjatan Hindu zuwa Manasarovar a Tibet da kuma Kiristoci zuwa Jerusalem. A shekarar 2014, sama da mahajjata 1,000 na Hindu ne suka samu tallafin gwamnati zuwa Manasarovar, kamar yadda dan siyasar gurguzu Comrade P.K. Premnath ya tabbatar da takardu.

Shawarwari kan tallafin Hajji

Tun da gwamnati ta kafa kwamiti don nazarin yadda za a dakatar da tallafin Hajji nan da 2022, ga wasu shawarwari:

  1. Inganta tsarin madadin: Idan za a dakatar da tallafin, a tabbatar an tura kudin zuwa tallafin karatu ga dalibai kamar yadda aka yi alkawari, ba wai a bar shi a matsayin maganar baka ba.
  2. Dawo da zirga-zirgar jiragen ruwa: Ya kamata a sake bude hanyar jiragen ruwa don rage kudin tafiya.
  3. Bada damar yin gasa ta duniya: Kada a takaita zirga-zirgar mahajjata ga Air India kawai. A bude kofa ga sauran kamfanonin jiragen sama don rage kudi.
  4. Bayyana kudaden a fili: Akwai zargin cewa akwai babban bambanci tsakanin kudin da mahajjata suke biya da kuma kudaden da ake kashewa. Ya kamata a bayyana wadannan kudaden a fili don gaskiya.
  5. Dakatar da 'yawon shakatawa' na VIP: Akwai kujeru 500 da ake warewa kowace shekara don mutanen VIP. Wannan ya kamata a duba shi, domin yawancin wadanda suke tafiya a karkashin wannan tsarin ba su da wata alaka da hidimar mahajjata.

Kammalawa

Lokaci ya yi da za a daina amfani da al'ummar Musulmi a matsayin wadanda ake zargi a kowane lokaci. Ba a bukatar 'yan siyasa masu nuna kamar suna son addini a gaban sauran addinai, a'a, ana bukatar shugabanni masu kishin gaskiya da adalci ga kowa. Ta haka ne kawai za a iya gina kasa mai hadin kai a cikin bambance-bambance.
