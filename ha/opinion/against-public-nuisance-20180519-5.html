Game da Matsalar Jama'a

മുസാഫിര്‍ · 2018-05-19 · Opinion & Analysis

Matsalar Jama'a: Ma'anar Doka

A cikin Kundin Dokar Indiya, an bayyana matsalar jama'a a matsayin 'wani aiki ko gazawa wajen yin aiki wanda ke haifar da haɗari ko damuwa ga jama'a baki ɗaya'. Zuba guba a cikin ruwan da jama'a ke amfani da shi don sha ko wanka ana ɗaukarsa a matsayin matsalar jama'a. Haka nan, gurbata iskar da muke shaka shima matsala ne. Haka zalika, kwararar ruwan datti zuwa hanyoyin da mutane ke bi ko kan titi shima matsala ne ga jama'a. Idan mutum ko cibiyar da ke da nauyin gudanar da wani aiki bisa doka ta gaza yin hakan, ana kiran hakan da gazawa wajen sauke nauyi. Tsaftace birni yana ɗaya daga cikin muhimman ayyukan da suka rataya a wuyan hukumar karamar hukuma bisa doka.

Shari'ar Karamar Hukumar Ratlam

An samu matsala irin wadda aka ambata a sama a karamar hukumar birnin Ratlam da ke jihar Madhya Pradesh. Saboda rashin kammala aikin magudanan ruwa a gefen titi, ruwan datti ya rika ambaliya yana taruwa a wurare daban-daban kan titi. Mutanen da ke zaune a bukka ba su da bandakunan jama'a, don haka suke amfani da gefen titi don biyan bukata. Haka kuma, ana zubar da shara daga gidaje da otal-otal a kan titi. Karamar hukumar ba ta ɗauki wani mataki ba don hana hakan ko kuma kwashe sharan a kan lokaci.

Matakan Doka Kan Matsalar Jama'a

Akwai wani sashe (133) a cikin Kundin Dokar Shari'ar Laifuka wanda ke ba Alkalin Gunduma ikon ɗaukar mataki kan matsalar jama'a. Idan aka karɓi korafi game da matsalar jama'a, alkali yana da ikon aika sammaci ga wanda ake ƙara, ya saurari shaidu, kuma idan ya gamsu da gaskiyar korafin, yana da ikon ba da umarnin a kawar da matsalar. Akwai sashe a cikin Kundin Dokar Indiya da ke ba da damar ɗaukar mataki idan aka gaza bin umarnin. Wannan shi ne taƙaitaccen bayani kan tanade-tanaden dokar shari'ar laifuka dangane da matsalar jama'a.

Hukuncin Kotun Koli

Alkalin ya gamsu cewa korafin da aka yi kan karamar hukumar Ratlam gaskiya ne. Ya ba da umarnin a tsaftace magudanan ruwa, a kammala aikin magudanan, kuma a gina bandakunan jama'a don amfanin talakawa. Babban uzurin karamar hukumar shi ne rashin kuɗi. Don guje wa hukunci, karamar hukumar ta ɗaukaka ƙara zuwa kotun sauraron ƙararrakin laifuka (Sessions Court) don kalubalantar umarnin alkali. Daga nan batun ya kai ga Babban Kotun Jiha, har ma ya kai ga Kotun Koli. A lokacin da aka kai ga wannan mataki, shekaru bakwai sun riga sun shude! A cikin hukuncin da ya yanke, Mai Shari'a V.R. Krishna Iyer ya bayyana cewa babban nauyin karamar hukuma shi ne tsaftace birni, kuma karamar hukumar da ta gaza yin hakan ba ta cancanci sunan ba. Ya ba da umarnin cewa a sanya gwamnatin jihar cikin shari'ar don ta ba da tallafin kuɗi da karamar hukumar ke bukata. Haka kuma ya yi gargaɗin cewa idan ba a kawar da matsalar ba a cikin lokacin da aka kayyade, za a ɗauki matakin hukunci.
