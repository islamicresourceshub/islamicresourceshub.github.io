Musulmin Rohingya: Wadanda aka zalunta da kyamar launin fata

ഡോ. ശബീൽ പി.എൻ · 2017-01-28 · Opinion & Analysis

Musulmin Rohingya: 1

Wannan ita ce Myanmar. Wata kasa da ta samu yanci daga mulkin mallaka na Birtaniya a shekarar 1948. Daga baya, ta zama kasa inda mulkin soja ya danne dimokuradiyya da hakkin dan adam. Lokacin da Aung San Suu Kyi da jam’iyyarta ta National League for Democracy suka karbi mulki a zaben shekarar 2015, duniya ta yi imanin cewa kasar ta dawo kan turbar dimokuradiyya. An yi tsammanin za a kare hakkin dan adam, amma abin da ke faruwa a can ya saba wa hakan gaba daya.

Daga can ne ake fitar da hotuna da labarai masu ban tsoro na kisan kiyashi. Mabiyan addinin Buddha da shugabanninsu da kuma malaman addininsu, wadanda aka sani da koyarwar zaman lafiya, sun hada kai wajen kawar da Musulmin Rohingya a wani yanayi mai taba zuciya. Majalisar Dinkin Duniya ta ayyana su a hukumance a matsayin al’ummar da aka fi zalunta a duniya; kungiyoyin kare hakkin dan adam kuma sun bayyana su a matsayin mutanen da aka fi kai wa hari kuma aka yi watsi da su a fadin duniya.

Wannan al’umma mai dimbin tarihi da kuma harshenta na kanta, ba ta da kasa. Ta hanyar kisan gilla, yawan mutanensu ya ragu matuka. Sun gudu zuwa kasashe daban-daban a matsayin ‘yan gudun hijira. Da yawa ba su ba su mafaka ba; ba su bar su sun taka kafa a kasashensu ba. Ba su da inda za su koma. Abin da ke jiran su shi ne karnukan farauta masu jiran gado. Dubbai sun mutu a tsakiyar teku! Wannan shi ne zahirin kisan kiyashi da aka tsara kuma aka aiwatar da shi.

Dan kadan daga tarihi

Dangane da alkaluman kungiyoyin kare hakkin dan adam, yawan mutanen ya kai miliyan 1.5 zuwa miliyan 2. Tun daga shekarar 1400 ne wasu daga cikinsu suka zauna a yankin Rakhine na Burma (Myanmar). Harshen Rohingya shi ne harshen da suke magana da shi.

Mutanen Bengal da aka kawo yankin don aikin gona da sauran ayyuka a lokacin da sarakunan Bengal da na Burma suke raba iyakoki, su ma sun zama wani bangare na wannan al’umma. Bayan Birtaniya ta kwace ikon Burma, kamfanin East India Company ya kawo mutane da yawa daga Bengal zuwa yankin Arakan mai albarka don aikin gona. A farkon karni na 19, mutane da yawa sun yi kaura daga Chittagong na Bengal zuwa yankin Arakan (Rakhine) don neman aiki. Lokacin da Burma ta samu yanci a 1948, akwai wadanda suka yi kaura zuwa Burma. Akwai kabilu kusan 135 a Myanmar. Kabilun Chin, Koman, da Cami duk bakin haure ne daga kasashe daban-daban.

Tushen matsalolin

Tsawon daruruwan shekaru, mabiya addinin Buddha da Musulmin Rohingya da suke zaune a yankin Arakan sun kasance cikin aminci da mu’amala. Wadanda suke zaune a bangarorin biyu na kogin Naf (iyakar Burma da Bangladesh) suna da alakar kasuwanci da zamantakewa.

A shekarar 1942, **Burmese Independence Army** da aka kafa karkashin jagorancin Janar Ne Win ta fara shuka kyamar Rohingya a tsakanin mabiya addinin Buddha. Sun yi nasarar yada ra’ayin cewa Musulmin Rohingya ma’aikatan kamfanin East India Company ne kuma karuwar yawan mutanensu barazana ce ga rayuwarsu.

A lokacin yakin duniya na biyu, sojojin Japan sun shiga Burma. Lokacin da Japan da dakarun Buddha na yankin suka yi kokarin kwace iko, Rohingya sun tare su a arewacin Arakan tare da taimakon Birtaniya. Duk da haka, a sauran yankuna, an kai wa tsiraru hari, ciki har da bakin haure ‘yan China, Kiristoci, da Musulmin Rohingya. Da yawa daga cikinsu sun gudu zuwa Bengal.

Lokacin da aka ayyana Pakistan a matsayin kasa mai yanci a 1947, wani bangare na Rohingya ya so su shiga kasar. Amma Muhammad Ali Jinnah ya ki shiga cikin harkokin cikin gidan Burma. Wani babban bangare kuma ya so su ci gaba da zama a Burma. A 1948, aka ayyana Burma a matsayin kasa mai yanci, inda tun farko aka samu hadin kai a cikin bambance-bambance tare da kabilu da addinai 135.

Amma yanayin ya sauya lokacin da Janar Ne Win ya kwace mulki daga hannun farar hula a 1962. Don ya samu goyon bayan mafi yawan al’ummar Buddha, tare da taimakon soja, ya fara kisan gilla ga tsirarun Rohingya, cin zarafin mata, da kuma korarsu daga kasarsu. Kungiyar ta’addanci ta mabiya addinin Buddha mai suna Arakanese Independent Movement ta kai wa Rohingya hare-hare da dama. Rikicin ya ci gaba. Gaskiyar mabiyan Buddha, wadanda suka koyi zaman lafiya a matsayin manufa, sun soki wadannan ayyukan. Har ma ‘yan ta’addan sun kashe malaman addinin Buddha da suka nuna adawa. Ai fasisanci ba ya son duk wanda ya saba masa.

(Ba a kammala ba)
