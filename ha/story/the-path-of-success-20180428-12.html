Hanyar Nasara

അബ്ദുൽ ഗഫൂര്‍ പുതുപ്പണം · 2018-04-28 · Stories & Literature

Idan ka kiyaye salloli biyar Lallai za ka samu nasarori da yawa Idan ka tuna Allah a cikin zuciyarka Za ka iya tafiya ba tare da tuntube ba

Domin samun tsarkin zuciya Addu'a ita ce magani mafi kyau Idan tsoron Allah yana cikin zuciya Za ka aikata ayyukan alheri da yawa

Idan ka aikata ayyukan alheri da yawa Ubangiji zai ba ka Aljanna Idan ba ka aikata wani mugun aiki ba Za ka iya samun yardar Ubangiji

Lokacin da kake aikata ayyukan kwarai Wane irin kuzari ne a cikin zuciya Domin mu so sauran halittu Ya kamata mu kasance masu tausayi

Idan muna tuna duk abin da muka koya Za mu iya hawan matakan nasara daya bayan daya Idan muna da tawali'u To lallai nasara tabbatacciya ce
