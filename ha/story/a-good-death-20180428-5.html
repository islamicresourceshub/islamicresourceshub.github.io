Kyakkyawar Mutuwa

വെള്ളില പി. അബ്ദുല്ല · 2018-04-28 · Stories & Literature

'Afkarun Ukhrawiyya' (Tunanin Lahira): 6

Mutumin da ya riga ya mutu

Mutumin da ya riga ya mutu Kana ganin shi waliyyi ne a tunaninka Amma kana barin koyarwar Manzon Allah Kuma kana shiga hurumin hukuncin Ubangiji Bai kamata ba masoyi, kada ka fadi haka Abin da Allah ya boye, babu wani wanda ya sani

Sahabi Uthman bin Maz'un

Uthman bin Maz'un shi ne sahabi Sahabin da ya fara mutuwa a Madina Bawan Allah da ya samu damar ganin Manzo Jarumin da ya tsaya tsayin daka a tafarkin Musulunci

Raba gado tsakanin Ansar da Muhajir

Lokacin da aka raba Muhajirai ga Ansar Suka sauka a gidan Ummul Ala A can ne ya cika da mutuwa Manzon Allah ya zo don ya duba shi

Ummul Ala tana cewa

'Allah ya girmama ka Kuma ni shaida ce akan hakan'

Ta ce Uthman ya yi nasara Sai Manzon Allah ya yi fushi ya ce:

'Waye ya ba ki wannan labarin?'

'Ya Manzon Allah, shin bai cancanta ba? Idan bai cancanta ba, wa kuma ya cancanci girmamawa?'

Nan take Mustafa Annabi ya ce:

'Ni ma Annabi ne, ban san abin da zai faru ba Ina fatan alheri gare shi Abin da Allah ya kaddara shi ne zai faru.'

Bayan haka, ban sake cewa komai ba

Bayan haka, ban sake cewa komai ba game da wani mutum Wannan shi ne maganar wannan matar Bayanan da aka samo daga littattafai da dama Har a cikin Bukhari za ka ga hakan Aljanna da wuta Allah ne yake bayarwa Ba mu san wa zai shiga ba Kowa zai isa inda aka kaddara masa Ko dai a Aljanna, ko kuma a wuta Akwai wadanda suke mutuwa suna fadin Kalima Amma mutuwa tana da kyau idan akwai fata Haka ma shahidi Idan niyyarsa ta gaskiya ce, Aljanna tabbatacciya ce gare shi

Wasu da suke mutuwa ba a yaki ba

  • Wasu da suke mutuwa ba a yaki ba, Annabi ya ce su ma shahidai ne
  • Mutumin da ya mutu sanadiyyar cutar kwalara, shi ma shahidi ne
  • Idan karshen mutum ya kasance nutsewa a ruwa, shi ma shahidi ne
  • Idan mutum ya mutu sanadiyyar shanyewar jiki, shahada ce gare shi
  • Mutumin da ya mutu sanadiyyar cutar ciki, shi ma shahidi ne kamar yadda Manzo ya fada
  • Mutumin da ya mutu a karkashin baraguzan gini, shi ma yana cikin su
  • Mace da ta mutu sanadiyyar nakuda, ita ma tana cikin jerin shahidai
  • Wadanda suka mutu sanadiyyar konewa, su ma suna cikin wannan rukuni

Wannan hadisi yana cikin littafin Muwatta Malik ne marubucin wannan littafi

  • Mutumin da ya mutu yana aikata alheri, wannan mutuwa ma tana da kyau
  • Wanda ya yi yaki don kare mutuncin iyalinsa
  • Wanda ya yi yaki don kare dukiyarsa
  • Wanda ya yi yaki don kare addininsa

Dukkan su sun shiga jerin shahidai

Don haka kada ka yanke hukunci akan kowa

Don haka dukkan wadannan mutuwa suna da kyau Za mu iya tunanin hakan saboda wadannan dalilai Amma hakan ba hujja ba ce Kada ka yanke hukunci cewa shi dan Aljanna ne Kuma idan ba a ga wadannan alamun ba Ba hujja ba ce cewa shi dan wuta ne! Don haka kada ka yanke hukunci akan kowa Ilimin hakan yana hannun Ubangiji ne kawai

Mutuwa a tafarkin addini

Ya Allah, ka sa in mutu a tafarkin addininka Kai ne kadai mataimaki na, ya Allah Ka ba ni haske a cikin kabari, ya Allah Ka ba ni Jannatul Firdausi, ya Allah
