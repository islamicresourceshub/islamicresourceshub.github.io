Hukuncin Babri

Shekaru ashirin da biyar sun shude tun lokacin da aka ruguza masallacin Babri, wanda yake alamar dimokuradiyyar Indiya, sakamakon ayyukan wasu gungun masu tsattsauran ra'ayin addini. Advani, Murli Manohar Joshi, Kalyan Singh, da Uma Bharti, wadanda suka kasance kwakwalwar da ke bayan ruguza masallacin, ga wani bincike kan lamarin.

Shekaru ashirin da biyar sun shude tun lokacin da aka ruguza masallacin Babri, wanda yake alamar dimokuradiyyar Indiya, sakamakon ayyukan wasu gungun masu tsattsauran ra'ayin addini. A daidai lokacin da Kotun Koli ta shirya sake sauraron shari'ar makirci da ake yi wa L. K. Advani, Murli Manohar Joshi, Kalyan Singh, da Uma Bharti, wadanda suka kasance kwakwalwar da ke bayan ruguza masallacin, ga wani bincike kan lamarin. Baya ga wadannan, akwai wasu shugabannin jam'iyyar BJP guda 13 da ake tuhuma. Kotun sauraron kararrakin Raebareli ta wanke su, hukuncin da Kotun Koli ta Lucknow ta tabbatar. Kotun ta yi wadannan lura ne yayin da take nazarin korafe-korafen da CBI ta shigar a shekarar 2011 da kuma wanda wani mai sa kai, Haji Mehboob Ahmad, ya shigar a shekarar 2015.

Akwai shari'o'i guda biyu da suka shafi ruguza masallacin Babri. Shari'a ta farko (197/1992) ta shafi kai tsaye ruguza masallacin. Wadanda ake tuhuma a wannan shari'ar su ne dubban mutane da ba a san ko su waye ba wadanda suka shiga aikin ruguzawar. Wannan shari'a ce da za ta iya kai ga hukuncin daurin shekaru goma a gidan yari. Wannan shari'ar ita ce aka fara saurara a kotun Lucknow. Shari'a ta biyu (198/1992) ta shafi makircin ruguza masallacin. Wannan shari'ar ta shafi Advani, Kalyan Singh, Joshi, Uma Bharti, Vinay Katiyar, da Ashok Singhal, wadanda suka kasance a kusa da wurin kuma suka shiga cikin makircin yayin da ake ruguza masallacin. Babban tuhumar da ake yi musu, wacce za ta iya kai ga hukuncin daurin shekaru biyar, ita ce kokarin haddasa kiyayya tsakanin al'ummomin addinai daban-daban ta yadda hakan zai lalata hadin kan kasa. Wannan shari'ar ana saurarenta ne a kotun Raebareli. A ranar 5 ga Oktoba, 1993, CBI ta karbi shari'o'in biyu kuma ta shigar da su a matsayin takarda daya. CBI ta sanar da kotun cewa wadanda aka ambata a takardar sun gudanar da taron sirri a gidan Vinay Katiyar kuma suka yanke shawarar ruguza masallacin. Amma kotun Allahabad ta Lucknow ta ci gaba da jinkirta shari'ar ba tare da wani dalili ba. A watan Fabrairun 2001, kotun ta rage gudu wajen gudanar da shari'ar saboda wani kuskuren fasaha. Kotun ta yi ikirarin cewa lokacin da CBI ta karbi shari'ar makircin daga kotun Raebareli, ba ta tuntubi kotun Allahabad ba. A lokaci guda, kotun ba ta fitar da wata sanarwa da za ta nuna cewa tana kare wadanda ake tuhuma ba. Amma CBI ko gwamnatocin jihohi da suka biyo baya ba su dauki matakan da suka dace don magance wannan kuskuren fasaha na kotun ba. Sakamakon haka, shari'a mai lamba 1980 da ke da alaka da makircin ta koma kotun Raebareli.

Hoton alama dangane da gina haikalin Ram a Ayodhya.

Shari'a mai lamba 1970 ce kawai aka ci gaba da saurara a kotun Lucknow. Wannan shari'ar ta shafi wadanda suka shiga ruguza masallacin ne kawai. Babu shari'ar shugabannin BJP a ciki. Da wannan hujjar, a ranar 24 ga Mayu, 2001, alkalin kotun Lucknow, Srikant Shukla, ya janye shari'ar da ake yi wa Advani da sauransu, yana mai cewa shari'ar ba ta da tushe. Ko da yake CBI ta shigar da karar daukaka kara, a ranar 20 ga Mayu, 2010, Kotun Allahabad ta amince da hukuncin Shukla kuma ta yi watsi da karar CBI. Bayan haka ne a ranar 11 ga Fabrairu, 2011, CBI ta dauki mataki mai karfi a Kotun Koli. CBI ta yi jayayya a Kotun Koli cewa Srikant Shukla ya yi wani bambanci na bogi (artificial distinction) wajen raba shari'o'in da suka shafi ruguza masallacin Babri. Sun yi ikirarin cewa Shukla ya yi kokarin kare su daga tuhumar makirci ta hanyar cewa shugabannin BJP ba su da hannu kai tsaye wajen ruguza masallacin. Sun kuma bukaci Kotun Koli da ta samar da yanayin da za a iya ci gaba da shari'o'in biyu tare kamar yadda aka yi a baya. 'Tunda kwarin gwiwa ga aikata laifi, aiwatar da laifin, ruguza masallacin, da kuma ci gaba da kai hare-hare kan 'yan jarida duk abubuwa ne da ke da alaka da juna, babu wani cikas na shigar da su tare a karkashin sashe na 120 B na dokar hukunta manyan laifuka ta Indiya. Ya kamata a saurari shari'o'in tare saboda an aikata laifukan a jere ta hanyar keta dokoki da dama. Ba za a iya kai shaidu zuwa kotuna biyu daban-daban ba,' in ji CBI a gaban kotun.

A ranar 6 ga Maris din da ya gabata ne Kotun Koli ta yi magana kan karar CBI. Kotun Koli ta bayyana cewa matakin da kotun sauraron kararrakin ta dauka na soke shari'ar makircin saboda dalilai na fasaha ba zai iya tsayawa ba. Kotun da ta kunshi Alkalai R. S. Nariman da P. C. Ghose ne suka ba CBI umarnin shigar da sabon takardar tuhuma a kan dukkan wadanda ake tuhuma. Ko da yake an dage sauraron karar zuwa 22 ga Maris, an sake dage ta zuwa 6 ga Afrilu saboda rashin kasancewar Nariman.

Lauyan shugabannin BJP, K. K. Venugopal, ya yi jayayya sosai kan kawo sabon takardar tuhuma, amma Alkali Nariman bai amince ba. 'Ba za mu iya amincewa da wanke wadanda ake zargi ba a kan cewa laifin bai tabbata ba a farkon kallo. A wane tushe aka wanke su? Dalilai na fasaha ba su taba zama hujjar wanke mutum ba,' in ji Alkali Nariman. Neeraj Kishan Kaul, mataimakin babban lauyan gwamnati, ne ya wakilci CBI.

A halin yanzu, Uma Bharti ministar tarayya ce, kuma Kalyan Singh gwamnan Rajasthan ne. Murli Manohar Joshi kuwa yana shirin zama shugaban kasa bayan Pranab Mukherjee. Joshi ne na farko a jerin sunayen shugaban kasa da BJP ta shirya. Idan hukuncin ya saba musu, burin Joshi na zama shugaban kasa ba zai cika ba. Haka kuma, Uma Bharti da Kalyan Singh za su iya rasa mukamansu.

Yayin da ake wannan jayayya, shugaban BJP Subramanian Swamy ya tunkari Kotun Koli yana neman izinin gina haikalin Ram. Alkali J. S. Khehar na Kotun Koli ya ba da shawarar cewa za a iya magance matsalar ta hanyar sulhu a wajen kotu. 'Kada ku dauke shi a matsayin umarnin kotu, ku dauke shi a matsayin shawara kawai. Tunda batun yana da alaka da sulhu da kuma motsin rai, za mu iya zama tare da dukkan bangarorin don neman mafita,' in ji Alkali Khehar.

Dangane da shari'ar ruguza masallacin Babri, wanda ya bata sunan Indiya a idon duniya, Uma Bharti da ake tuhuma a halin yanzu ministar tarayya ce, kuma Kalyan Singh gwamnan Rajasthan ne. Murli Manohar Joshi kuwa yana shirin zama shugaban kasa bayan Pranab Mukherjee. Joshi ne na farko a jerin sunayen shugaban kasa da BJP ta shirya. Idan hukuncin ya saba musu, burin Joshi na zama shugaban kasa ba zai cika ba. Haka kuma, Uma Bharti da Kalyan Singh za su iya rasa mukamansu.

Kungiyoyin masu ra'ayin dimokuradiyya sun yi watsi da shawarar Khehar gaba daya. Shugabannin kamar mai gudanarwa na kwamitin ayyuka na masallacin Babri Dr. S.Q.R. Ilyas, babban sakataren kungiyar Muslim League na Indiya E. T. Mohammed Basheer MP, mamba a hukumar kula da shari'ar musulunci Maulana Khalid Rashid, da kuma Sunni Waqf Board sun yi watsi da shawarar kotun. Shugabannin sun ce ya kamata kotun ta yanke hukunci bisa shaidun da ke gabanta dangane da mallakar filin. Babban sakataren jam'iyyar CPI (M) Sitaram Yechury shi ma ya ce ana bukatar hukuncin kotu ne a kan takaddamar shari'a.

Kotun Koli ta shigo da shawarar sulhu ne bayan Kotun Allahabad ta yanke wani muhimmin hukunci a ranar 30 ga Satumba, 2010, dangane da mallakar wurin da masallacin Babri yake. Kotun Allahabad ta yanke hukuncin raba wurin da ake takaddama a kai gida uku daidai wa daida ga kungiyoyin Hindu Mahasabha, Sunni Waqf Board, da kuma kungiyar Vaishnav ta Nirmohi Akhara. Amma wannan hukuncin bai gamsar da Hindu Mahasabha da Sunni Waqf Board ba. Sun tunkari Kotun Koli. A ranar 9 ga Mayu, 2011, Kotun Koli ta dakatar da hukuncin Kotun Allahabad kuma ta ba da umarnin a ci gaba da zama a yadda ake. Kotun Koli ta lura cewa abin mamaki ne yadda Kotun Allahabad ta yanke wannan hukuncin alhali babu daya daga cikin bangarorin da ya nemi hakan.

Advani ya gudanar da tafiyar ratha (tafiyar keken doki) wacce ta ratsa zuciyar kasar. Ko da yake Lalu Prasad da Mulayam Singh, wadanda su ne jaruman Bihar da Uttar Pradesh, sun yi kokarin dakatar da tafiyar, Advani ya yi nasarar tayar da motsin zuciyar Hindu ta hanyar tafiyar. A wata hira da ya yi da India Today kafin aikin kar seva, Advani ya ce kar seva ba kawai waƙoƙin addini ba ne; a'a, za a yi amfani da tubali da sauran kayan aiki. Vinay Katiyar, shugaban Bajrang Dal wanda ke jiran hukunci tare da Advani, ya ce a lokacin, 'Ko doka ta hana, ko akwai hukuncin kotu, kar seva za ta faru.' Maganar wani 'wanda ake tuhuma', shugaban VHP Ashok Singhal, ta fi wannan muni. 'Idan aka dakatar da kar sevaks, za a ga lahira a nan,' in ji Singhal.

Muhimman sauraron kararrakin da hukunce-hukuncen da suka shafi masallacin Babri za su zo ne a makonni masu zuwa. A halin da ake ciki inda gwamnati ke ci gaba da farautar tsiraru, hukunci mai adalci daga bangaren shari'a ne kawai zai ba tsirarun bege. 6 ga Disamba rana ce mai duhu a tarihin duniya. Rana ce da Indiya ta ji kunya a gaban duniya. Wannan lokaci ne da za a iya dawo da martabar dimokuradiyyar kasarmu da ta dushe. Ba za a iya ba da hujjar cewa wani bangare ya kwace wurin bautar wani bangare ta hanyar sanya gumaka a cikin duhun dare ba tare da wata hujja ta tarihi ba, sannan daga baya ya ruguza wurin bautar ya kuma haddasa rikici a fadin kasar. Dole ne a yaki halin da ake ciki inda hargitsi ya maye gurbin mulkin doka, ba tare da la'akari da kabilanci, addini, ko siyasa ba. Dole ne a dawo da martabar kasar da ta bata, ta yadda za ta iya tsayawa kai tsaye a gaban kasashen duniya.

Dole ne a hukunta wadanda suka aikata laifin. Tafiyar ratha ta L. K. Advani ta ratsa zuciyar kasar. Ko da yake Lalu Prasad da Mulayam Singh, wadanda su ne jaruman Bihar da Uttar Pradesh, sun yi kokarin dakatar da tafiyar, Advani ya yi nasarar tayar da motsin zuciyar Hindu ta hanyar tafiyar. A wata hira da ya yi da India Today kafin aikin kar seva, Advani ya ce kar seva ba kawai waƙoƙin addini ba ne; a'a, za a yi amfani da tubali da sauran kayan aiki.

Subramanian Swamy ya tunkari kotun yana neman izinin gina haikalin bisa lura da cewa yanayin da zai ba da damar gina haikalin Ram ya riga ya kasance tunda BJP ta karbi mulki a UP. Kotun Koli ta bukaci Swamy da ya tattauna da dukkan bangarorin kuma ya sanar da kotun a ranar 31 ga Maris.
