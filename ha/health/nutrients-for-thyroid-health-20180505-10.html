Abubuwan gina jiki don lafiyar garkuwar thyroid

ഡോ. മുഹമ്മദ് കുട്ടി കണ്ണിയന്‍ · 2018-05-05 · Health & Medicine

Kamar yadda yake ga kowane aikin jiki, tsarin abinci yana taka muhimmiyar rawa wajen taimakawa ko kawo cikas ga aikin garkuwar thyroid. Ga muhimman abubuwan gina jiki da ke taimakawa lafiyar garkuwar thyroid:

Iodine

Iodine shi ne sinadarin da garkuwar thyroid kawai take sha a jikin dan adam. Garkuwar thyroid tana amfani da kusan kashi biyu bisa uku na dukkan adadin iodine da ke jikinmu. Tun da karancin iodine ko kuma yawan amfani da shi na iya shafar aikin garkuwar, dole ne a kula da yadda ake amfani da wannan sinadari.

Manyan hanyoyin samun sa su ne abincin teku, ganyayyaki, kayan lambu, da hatsi. Ko da yake ruwan teku shi ne babban tushen sa, gishirin da ake samu ta hanyar busar da shi ba shi da kyau sosai. Gishirin da aka kara wa iodine yana tabbatar da cewa kana samun adadin da ya dace a cikin abincinka.

Selenium

Yawancin sinadarin selenium da ke jikin dan adam ana samun sa ne a cikin garkuwar thyroid. Wannan sinadari yana da matukar muhimmanci wajen samarwa da kuma sarrafa sinadarin hormone na T3. Baya ga haka, yana da mahimmanci wajen daidaita matakin hormone na T3 a sauran gabobin jiki da kuma cikin jini. Selenium yana daya daga cikin muhimman abubuwan da ke samar da sinadaran enzyme masu yakar guba, kuma yana da matukar muhimmanci ga enzymes da garkuwar thyroid ke bukata don gudanar da aikinta. A matsayinsa na mai yakar guba, yana da ikon hana cutar daji da kuma kara karfin garkuwar jiki, basira, da kuma karfin haihuwa. Ana samun selenium a cikin abincin teku kamar kaguwa, kifin tuna, nau'ikan namomin kaza, da hanta.

Iron, Zinc, da Copper

Waɗannan su ne sauran muhimman abubuwa guda uku da ake bukata don lafiyar thyroid. An gano cewa karancin zinc yana rage matakin TCH, karancin iron yana rage karfin aikin garkuwar thyroid, yayin da copper kuma yake shafar samar da sinadaran thyroid.

Ya kamata a hada hanta, ganyayyaki, nau'ikan namomin kaza, goro, wake, da abincin teku a cikin abinci gwargwadon adadin da ya dace.

Bitamin

Ko da yake dukkan bitamin suna da mahimmanci ga lafiyar thyroid, bitamin D, bitamin B12, da bitamin masu yakar guba kamar beta-carotene, C, da E sun fi kowanne muhimmanci.

Bitamin D: Karancin bitamin D yana haifar da raunin kashi wanda ke kara tsananta a lokacin da mutum ke fama da cutar hyperthyroidism. Baya ga kifin mai, madara, kwai, da nau'ikan namomin kaza, ana iya samun sa ta hanyar hasken rana. Bitamin masu yakar guba kamar C, E, da beta-carotene suma suna taka muhimmiyar rawa wajen lafiyar thyroid. Ganyayyaki, kayan lambu masu launi, 'ya'yan itatuwa, da man shuka su ne mafi kyawun hanyoyin samun wadannan.

Omega-3 Fatty Acids

Daga cikin nau'ikan kitse, wadannan su ne suka fi kowanne mahimmanci ga lafiyar thyroid. Ana samun su da yawa a cikin kifi, man kifi, iri na flax, da man kwakwa.
