Matsalolin Thyroid

ഡോ. മുഹമ്മദ് കുട്ടി കണ്ണിയന്‍ · 2018-04-28 · Health & Medicine

Thyroid gland ita ce gland din da ke sarrafa yanayin jiki. Tana taka rawa wajen gudanar da ayyukan jiki da dama, ciki har da sarrafa sinadaran jiki. Tana nan a wuyanka, tana da girman kusan inci biyu, kuma tana da siffar malam-buɗe-littafi mai buɗe fiffikinta.

Thyroid gland tana samar da manyan hormones guda biyu: Triiodothyronine wanda aka fi sani da T3, da kuma Thyroxine wanda aka fi sani da T4. Ana samar da wadannan ne lokacin da gland din pituitary ta fitar da hormone mai motsa thyroid wanda ake kira TSH domin ta motsa gland din thyroid. Hormone din T4 yana canzawa a cikin kyallen jikin mutum zuwa hormone din T3 wanda yake da matukar tasiri.

Ga muhimman ayyukan hormone din thyroid: Yana kara amfani da iskar oxygen a jiki wanda hakan ke samar da karin kuzari. Yana taimakawa wajen kiyaye zafin jiki. Yana kara bugun zuciya. Yana hanzarta watsa sakonni a jijiyoyin jiki. Yana taka muhimmiyar rawa wajen bunkasar kwakwalwa a lokacin daukar ciki da kuma ga yara. Yana rage matakin cholesterol a cikin jini. Yana kara sha da narkewar sinadaran abinci. Yana taimakawa wajen samar da madara a lokacin shayarwa. Ga mata, yana taimakawa wajen samun lokacin al'ada daidai da kuma karfin haihuwa.

Ya fi shafar mata

Cututtukan thyroid sun fi yawa a tsakanin mata. Matsalolin thyroid na iya bayyana a kowane mataki na rayuwa, tun daga lokacin daukar ciki. Duk da haka, bincike ya nuna cewa kusan daya daga cikin mata biyar na iya fuskantar matsalolin thyroid a wani lokaci a rayuwarsu.

Matsalolin Thyroid

Cututtukan thyroid iri-iri ne. Wani lokaci gland din ba ta iya samar da hormone yadda ya kamata ba. Wani lokacin kuma tana iya samar da shi da yawa. A wasu mutane, ana samar da hormone din amma ba ya canzawa zuwa T3 mai tasiri. Ko kuma, ko da sauran ayyukan suna tafiya daidai, tsarin garkuwar jiki na iya kasa daidaita su.

Manyan matsalolin thyroid su ne Hypothyroidism wato raguwar aikin thyroid, da Hyperthyroidism wato wuce gona da iri wajen aiki. Babban dalilin karuwar cututtukan thyroid a zamanin yau shi ne rashin daidaito a sinadaran abinci da kuma salon rayuwa mara kyau. Iodine shi ne sinadarin da ya fi kowane muhimmanci ga aikin thyroid. Baya ga haka, rashin wasu kananan sinadarai kamar Zinc da Selenium na iya haifar da cututtukan thyroid. Jiyya da sinadarin Radioactive Iodine, matsalolin garkuwar jiki, da kuma kwayoyin halitta suma na iya zama sanadi. Wasu bincike da aka gudanar kwanan nan sun nuna cewa sinadarai da ake sakawa a abinci da kuma abubuwan dandano na wucin gadi na haifar da irin wadannan matsalolin.

Hypothyroidism shi ne matsalolin thyroid da aka fi gani. Manyan alamunsa su ne gajiya, kasala, karuwar nauyin jiki ba tare da wani dalili ba, bushewar fata, zubar gashi, rashin jure sanyi, maƙarƙashiya, damuwa, zubar jini mai yawa lokacin al'ada, karuwar matakin cholesterol a jini, da kumburin fuska da gabobi. Manyan alamun Hyperthyroidism su ne raguwar nauyin jiki ba tare da dalili ba, yawan zufa, bugun zuciya da sauri, fargaba, fushi, gajiya, rawar hannu, gudawa, da rashin barci.
