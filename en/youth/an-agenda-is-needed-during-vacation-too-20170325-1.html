An Agenda is Needed; Even During Vacation

ഡോ. സി.എം സാബിര്‍ നവാസ് · 2017-03-25 · Youth & Children

Making Effective Use of Vacation

Another academic year has come to an end, and the vacation is upon us. The hustle and bustle of children is shifting from the classroom to the home courtyard (or the apartment terrace, for those in urban areas). With proper preparation, the vacation can be utilized much more effectively. Some parents, fearing that their children might slack off in their studies, try to turn them into full-time bookworms. If they do not realize that there is value in play, it will seriously affect the child's mental and physical balance.

How can we teach the basics of environmental protection to a generation that cannot interact with the soil and nature, get to know living creatures, or play with birds? Children must be allowed to play, not just during the holidays, but at all times.

Visiting Relatives

Since moving from joint families to nuclear families, the domestic atmosphere in many places has become volatile. Malayalis are living amidst the inconveniences created by modern transport and communication facilities. It would be wise to plan visits to relatives' homes and meetings with elders during the vacation period. Such visits are highly beneficial for instilling the importance of relationships in children's minds and helping them cultivate good virtues.

Family Trips

It is a good idea for parents and children to organize suitable trips together. At a time when school excursions often lead to bad habits, including alcohol consumption, family trips that adhere to Islamic etiquette are of great significance.

Discovering Creative Talents

If parents do not take care to discover and encourage their children's creative talents and join in their laughter and play, there is no doubt that our children's adolescence will wither away.

Only if parents are willing to spend more time with their children can they be raised properly. If we can organize children's academic and extracurricular activities and help them prepare for the next academic year in advance, it will be very beneficial.

The Importance of Moral Education

Religious education plays a huge role in ensuring discipline and order in children's lives. There is no doubt that in the rush to provide material education to our children, moral education is being marginalized.

During the vacation, we must open opportunities for our children to learn the Holy Quran and the Arabic language.

Organizing a suitable syllabus and conducting vacation Moral Schools at selected centers across the state is a highly exemplary initiative.

Allah says: "O you who have believed, protect yourselves and your families from a Fire whose fuel is people and stones..." (Quran 66:6).

Things to Keep in Mind

In short, before we blindly send our children into the dazzling, musical world of motors, media, games, and animated cartoons, there are many things we need to pay attention to during the vacation.
