> "I do not desire a life of rest. The rest that Allah has ordained for a person is after life; that is, after death. Every day when I wake up, I pray to Allah to provide me with the opportunity to help at least two people."

These are the words shared with journalists by the historic figure **E. Ahamed** during a life journey that spanned eight decades. This 79-year-old man departed from this world having illustrated the noble message of human service through his own life.

## The Voice of the Voiceless

There is no doubt that this man from Kannur, who traveled the world as the voice of the voiceless and a pillar of support for the helpless, will remain an indelible face in the hearts of the Indian people. The respect and love for E. Ahamed were reflected in the faces of the tens of thousands who arrived at the Karipur Hajj House and the Kozhikode beach to catch a final glimpse of his lifeless body.

## Lessons for the New Generation

Starting his journey as a student, moving through the Legislative Assembly and Parliament, and roaring for justice within the halls of the United Nations, this leader stood tall on the world stage. The new generation must be prepared to learn the basic lessons of public life from the life of this leader. While many in the new generation dream of easy success, avoiding hard work, living in air-conditioned homes, traveling in climate-controlled vehicles, and obsessing over parliamentary status in crisp, unwrinkled clothes, they have much to learn from this hardworking humanitarian. Until his final breath, Ahamed Sahib ran tirelessly for the sake of the destitute.

## Political Mentors and Training

The affection received from **Qaid-e-Millath**, **Bafakhy Thangal**, and **Pookoya Thangal**, along with the training provided by **Seethi Sahib** and **C.H. Mohammed Koya**, helped Ahamed Sahib learn the discipline of political life from a young age. One who learned his craft in the arena of such political masters, viewing public work as religious duty and service as a virtue, can proudly say that he never faltered in his steps.

## A List of Achievements

E. Ahamed, the great banyan tree of politics, fell while leaving behind a list of achievements that turned everything he touched into gold for the next generation. His ability to remain prominent in Kerala politics as an MLA and Minister, and in national politics as an MP and Union Minister of State, was not due to arrogance or ego, but solely because of his untainted service to humanity.

## A Pillar of Support for Expatriates

In many expatriate homes across different regions of India, people cannot eat a meal or lay their heads down at night without remembering Ahamed Sahib. How could the hundreds of lives that swam back to the shores of life from the brink of the death penalty, thanks to Ahamed’s flawless diplomatic maneuvers, ever forget him?

This public servant personally intervened to rescue many who had to spend long, unjust years in the prisons of Arab nations. There is no doubt that prayers for Ahamed Sahib’s salvation in the hereafter will rise from the depths of the hearts of those individuals and their families. The Indians who were able to perform their holy pilgrimage due to the services he rendered regarding the Hajj journey now carry a deep, burning pain in their hearts knowing that there is no longer an Ahamed Sahib to guide them.

It is undoubtedly an act of ingratitude that this good man, who worked his entire life for the Indian people, did not receive the justice and consideration he deserved from the administration in his final moments.
