The Erosion of Values Leading the Younger Generation Astray

പത്രാധിപർ · 2018-05-05 · Youth & Children

Children Rule Every Home

Many parents today are raising their children without imparting any moral or ethical culture. Children are now the ones ruling every home. Their desires have become the parents' priorities. Children decide what to eat, what to wear, where to go for vacations, and which vehicle to buy. Parents fulfill these demands even if it means taking out loans from their provident funds or banks. This applies to everything, whether it is the latest motorcycle, a cell phone, or even securing an engineering or medical seat for a child who lacks the necessary marks.

Children growing up this way see the world only as a collection of opportunities laid out for them. They view roads as tracks for reckless speeding and girls as targets to be ensnared. Parents are afraid to guide their children toward the right path. They have begun to view their children merely as financial investments for the future.

Moral Consciousness or God-Consciousness

There is no doubt that if parents performed their roles properly, their children would be able to swim against the tide of evil. Currently, parents grant their children absolute freedom and fulfill every whim, yet they fail to provide what is truly essential. Providing this is what makes them human: moral consciousness or God-consciousness. To be able to distinguish between right and wrong, truth and falsehood, to stand up for righteousness, to view classmates as siblings, to love and respect parents, and to refrain from lusting after teachers—children must be taught from a young age that there is a world where good and evil deeds receive their due rewards and punishments.

Those who do not hesitate to plunge daggers into the chests of classmates in the name of politics, kill others with their vehicles, or gouge out the eyes of junior students under the euphemism of ragging are living without reflection. In the midst of their 'cool' lives, they forget that the phenomenon of death will eventually reach them too. Or perhaps, they operate under the mindset that they must 'celebrate' life to the fullest until they die. If parents had tried to instill in them the teaching that death is not the end of life, they would not be engaging in such 'devilish games.'

The Truth of the Afterlife

The notion that life ends with death is a misconception. The afterlife is a truth and the pursuit of the wise. We cannot fully administer rewards and punishments for good and evil in this world. Whether a person kills one individual or a hundred, the punishments available to earthly courts are limited. The precise venue for true justice is the hereafter. Consider what the Quran says:

"Every soul will taste death, and you will only be given your [full] compensation on the Day of Resurrection. So he who is drawn away from the Fire and admitted to Paradise has attained [his] desire. And what is the life of this world except the enjoyment of delusion" (3:185).

Ideologies That Foster Atheism

We are witnessing the tragic consequences of parents failing to provide direction to students who are growing up amidst ideologies that foster atheism, as well as films, arts, and literature that reject moral values.
