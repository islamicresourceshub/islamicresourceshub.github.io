## **Shareefa** is a clever girl

She studies well. She has a spacious room where she plays, sleeps, and studies.

## The room must always be clean

Shareefa insists that her room should always be beautiful and clean. Because of this, she never scribbles on the walls or anywhere else in her room.

## Jamal and Jumana came to play

One day, Shareefa's aunt's children, Jamal and Jumana, came over to play. Even though Shareefa suggested they go outside to play, they didn't listen. They played with a ball inside the room. The room became a mess. Leftovers from ice cream and snacks were scattered everywhere. Even though she asked them not to make the room dirty, they didn't listen. Since they were guests in her home, and the **Prophet** (peace be upon him) taught us to treat guests with kindness, Shareefa did not speak harshly to them.

## What to do with all this?

After Jamal and Jumana finished playing and left, Shareefa started cleaning her room. She put the fallen items back in their places and swept up all the trash.

What should she do with all this? Without thinking much, she began throwing the trash out through the open window. It would fall right onto the road. Just then, Shareefa's father came by.

"My dear, what are you doing? Throwing waste outside?" he asked.

"Yes, Father, what is wrong with that? Am I not cleaning my room?" Shareefa asked innocently.

"My dear, think about it. Is this not your room?"

"Yes, Father!! That is why I prepared to remove the waste from my room."

"Think again. Isn't that our road? If everyone throws waste from their homes onto the road like this, what will the condition of the road be?"

"Father, that is true! I didn't think that far. I also want our road to be clean. Otherwise, it would be very difficult for travelers."

"My dear, all the roads and paths in our town should be clean. It will only happen if everyone puts their mind to it."

"Then Father, shall we throw the waste into the nearby river? The current will carry it somewhere far away."

"No, my dear, the river is also ours. If the river is polluted, the fish in it will get sick. They will die in large numbers."

"That is true, Father! A few days ago, there was news in the paper about fish dying in a river because poisonous water was released from some factory, and people who bathed in it got sick."

"Clever girl, you remember all that. Very good. Remember that many families use the water from this river for drinking."

"Then I have an idea...!"

"What is it, my dear?"

"We can burn this trash, including the plastic and paper. That is what **Nafisatha** in the house next door does."

"Don't do that, my dear! Do not burn it! Nafisatha is polluting the air. Do you like the pungent smell and smoke that comes from burning plastic and other things? Polluted gases cause many diseases, my dear."

"That is true. **Khadeeja Teacher** told us about that at school. Polluted gases negatively affect even our crops. It even causes trouble for birds."

"My dear, you are thinking well now. My daughter is truly clever."

"Thank you, Father..! Now, what shall we do with this trash?"

"Child, before you throw away any waste material, you must think. You should ask yourself two questions."

"What are those two questions, Father?"

1. Can anything useful be made from this item? That is one question.
2. Can it be of any use to others? That is the second one.

"Are you talking about turning waste materials and trash into something useful in another way, Father?"

"Yes, exactly!"

"Now I understand. But how can we do all this?"

"We should deposit the waste in the boxes placed by the **Panchayats** and municipalities for collecting waste."

"What happens to these things we discard later, Father?"

"Tomorrow, we can go to see the **Waste Treatment Plant**. Then you will understand everything clearly."

## A visit to the Waste Treatment Plant

The next day, Shareefa visited the waste treatment plant with her father.

- How **organic fertilizer** is made from organic waste
- How plastic and other materials are separated, purified, and recycled into useful items

They saw and understood these processes. 

Shareefa was overjoyed when her father bought her a beautiful bag and a flower pot made from waste materials. Shareefa returned home with her father, eager to tell all her friends about what she had learned.
