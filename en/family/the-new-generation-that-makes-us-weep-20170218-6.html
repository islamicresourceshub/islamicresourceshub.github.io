The New Generation That Makes Us Weep

ഇബ്‌നു അലി എടത്തനാട്ടുകര · 2017-02-18 · Family & Society

A Tear-Stained Office Visit

In my office, the man sitting in the chair before me sobbed uncontrollably. Tears streamed down his cheeks. Though he wiped them away with embarrassment, they continued to flow with even greater intensity. He was a retired official who had held a respectable position in the state government.

His problem was his son. The son had been running a business, purchasing and distributing medical equipment from another state. However, he failed to submit the necessary documents to the tax department or pay his taxes. He did not respond to the warning notices sent by officers, nor did he file the required returns or pay the dues, even after delays. It was only when the department proceeded with legal action and penalties that this elderly man climbed three flights of stairs to reach my office, sobbing as he spoke.

The Pitfalls Behind Fame

The son had developed a bit of a political fever. Some cunning friends and politicians took advantage of him. By presenting himself as a kind, compassionate, and selfless person in front of others, the son fell into the trap of fame and media attention.

He gave away machinery worth lakhs that he had purchased at a cost. He began donating lakhs in charity. When his checks bounced, it turned into a legal case.

A Shattered Family Life

The father lost all peace of mind due to the constant stream of people coming to collect money or seeking further favors. He spent his entire pension savings to pay off his son's debts. When that was not enough, he sold his land to settle the dues. Yet, the debt remained.

Because the land set aside for his daughter's wedding expenses had to be sold to pay off the debts, her marriage was called off. He lamented, asking why God had given him such a son. The father declared that he had no relationship with his son anymore and that no one should come to see him regarding anything related to the boy. But after all, he is the son he raised; how could he simply abandon him?

I sent him off with the promise that I would help as much as possible within the limitations of the law and that I would inform him before the final orders were issued.

While those without children suffer in their sorrow, how many are there who have lost their peace, family life, and dignity precisely because they have children!

Where Did the Mistake Lie?

Where did that father go wrong? - Was it in giving too much love and money? - Was it in rejoicing when he saw his son becoming famous? - Was it in losing himself when he saw the big business and setup? - Or was it in raising his son without instilling faith in God and a sense of moral consciousness?

To ensure that such wayward sons do not continue to make their parents and families weep, there must be conscious efforts to shape the new generation into living lives grounded in moral values.
