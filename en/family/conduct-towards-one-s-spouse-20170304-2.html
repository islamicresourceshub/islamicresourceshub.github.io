Conduct Towards One's Spouse

അബൂ റാഷിദ · 2017-03-04 · Family & Society

Narrated by Abu Hurairah (may Allah be pleased with him): The Prophet (peace be upon him) said: "The most perfect of believers in faith are those who are best in character, and the best of you are those who are best to their wives." (Tirmidhi)

Challenges in Married Life

There is no one who does not desire a happy married life. Yet, for many, married life becomes unbearable and filled with conflict. News of husbands killing their wives and wives killing their husbands appears frequently in newspapers. The number of wives suffering from mental and physical abuse is increasing day by day.

The Islamic Perspective

Mutual cooperation and trust between spouses are the foundation of a good family. In their absence, problems naturally arise. How can one be a good husband? How can one be a good wife? Islam provides very clear guidance in this area. The Prophetic tradition mentioned above makes it clear that to become a good human being, one must first become a good husband.

Many who are known as respectable and good people in society are cruel and inhumane when they are with their own wives. They wear a mask of respectability. What is the value of respectability and a good reputation in society for someone who cannot treat his own wife with dignity?

Advice to Husbands

The Quranic command to husbands, "Live with them in kindness" (4:129), and the Prophetic tradition beginning with, "Woman is like a rib; if you try to straighten her by force, you will break her," are points that all believing husbands should heed.

Although a woman is very patient, she may naturally exhibit impatience, quick temper, carelessness, and other minor flaws in various matters. It is a mere delusion to think that one can completely change all of these and turn his wife into someone who possesses no traits he dislikes. If one attempts this, the result will only be the destruction of the relationship. The Prophet (peace be upon him) said:

"A believing man should not hate a believing woman. If he dislikes one of her traits, he may be pleased with another."

Instructions from the Farewell Sermon

In his historic Farewell Sermon, the Prophet (peace be upon him) said regarding women:

"Know that you must treat women with kindness, for they are entrusted to you (as a trust). You have no other authority over them, unless they commit a clear act of immorality. If they do that, then (without resorting to divorce) forsake them in their beds and strike them without causing injury. If they obey you, then do not seek any other way against them. Know that you have rights over your wives, and your wives have rights over you. Your right over them is that they should not allow anyone you dislike to tread on your bedding, and they should not allow anyone you dislike to enter your homes. Know that their right over you is that you treat them with kindness in matters of clothing and food." (Tirmidhi 1163)
