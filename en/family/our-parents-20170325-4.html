Our Parents

കെ.സഫ്‌വാന്‍ മുഹമ്മദ്, ആമയൂര്‍ · 2017-03-25 · Family & Society

Those Blessed by Allah

We are all recipients of Allah's immense blessings. The very fact that we were born as humans is a great blessing in itself. Where were we before this? We existed in a time and a world that was void, beyond our imagination. For over nine months, we lived comfortably in our mother's womb. Allah nurtured us there in complete safety.

Our Mother's Struggle

Yet, during that time, our mother was enduring hardship. Why? For our sake! For our very lives! Allah says:

"His mother carried him with difficulty upon difficulty" (Surah Luqman: 14).

This verse is very clear. While pregnant, our mother had to be extremely cautious and endure great difficulty with every movement. She could not even rest her head comfortably. And what about when she gave birth? It was a pain akin to the agony of death. Science tells us that the pain of childbirth is as intense as having 28 bones broken simultaneously! For whom did she endure all this? Remember, for us alone!

A Question to Umar (RA)

Once, a man came to the Caliph Umar (RA) and asked:

"My mother has become very old. She cannot do anything for herself. Because of this, I carry her on my shoulders to fulfill many of her needs. She relies on me for everything. Has this repaid the things she did for me when I was young?"

Umar (RA) replied:

"Not at all! She raised and cared for you with great hope. She watched every milestone of your growth with joy. But when you care for her, you are wishing for her life to end quickly."

Who Deserves the Best Treatment?

No matter how much good we do or how much we help them, it will never be a full repayment for their deeds. Once, a man came to the Prophet (PBUH) and asked who he should treat with the best companionship. The Prophet (PBUH) said:

"Your mother."

He asked: "Then who?" The Prophet (PBUH) said:

"Your mother."

He repeated this again. When he asked for the fourth time, the Prophet (PBUH) said:

"Your father."

Three Things the Father Cannot Do

The Prophet (PBUH) gave the mother the first three positions over the father. The mother has endured three things that the father cannot:

  • Carrying the pregnancy
  • Giving birth
  • Breastfeeding and nurturing

These three things are beyond the father's capacity. That is why the mother is given the first three positions. We must grant them these positions. We must obey them and treat them with kindness.

Do Not Say 'Uff' to Them

Yet, we are often quick to snap and get angry at the very mother who never gets angry at us and loves us dearly. Allah said:

"...If one or both of them reach old age with you, do not say to them 'uff' (a word of irritation), and do not repel them, but speak to them a noble word." (Surah Al-Isra: 23).

In the commentary of this verse, scholars have noted that one should treat parents with the same fear and caution that a servant shows before a cruel ruler. Therefore, we must never do anything that displeases our parents. If our mother ever raises her hands against us in prayer, Allah will surely accept it. We will undoubtedly face the consequences in this world. There is no doubt about it.

Jurayj, the Scholar of the Children of Israel

Jurayj, a scholar among the Children of Israel, was praying in his place of worship when his mother called him. Confused, he prioritized his worship and continued praying. His mother left. This happened again the next day. The third time, receiving no answer, his mother was heartbroken and raised her hands against Jurayj right there. That prayer was accepted, and Jurayj had to face the consequences.

A Great Lesson for Us

There is a great lesson here. A great scholar could not fulfill his mother's need because he was in worship. Yet, Allah accepted the mother's prayer against her son. If our mother prays against us, what will be the result? Allah will accept it. Therefore, we must obey our parents in everything that does not contradict Islam. We need our parents to pray for us. To earn that, we must win their pleasure.

Three 'Amins' on the Minbar

Once, while ascending the Minbar, the Prophet (PBUH) said 'Amin' three times. When his companions asked why, the Messenger (PBUH) said:

"Jibril (AS) made three supplications, and I said Amin to them. One of them was: 'May he be ruined, the one who has elderly parents and does not attain Paradise through them.'"

Remember!

Remember! The one who prayed was Jibril (AS), and the one who said Amin was the Prophet (PBUH). Therefore, let us be careful and strive not to be among those who are cursed. We must understand that elderly parents are a path to attaining Paradise.

What Do We See Today?

But what do we see today? Old age homes are increasing. The number of elderly people abandoned on the streets is rising. As education and career pressures grow, the mother who bore the pain of birth and the father who grew weary working for their children become a burden! In doing so, children shut the doors to their own Paradise.

Praying for Them After Death

We must pray for our parents. Even after their death, prayer is something we can do for them. Allah said:

"...And lower to them the wing of humility out of mercy and say, 'My Lord, have mercy upon them as they brought me up [when I was] small.'" (Surah Al-Isra: 24).

May Allah include us all among the righteous children who obey their parents, pray for them, care for them, earn their pleasure, and attain Paradise through them!
