Water and Life

Water Scarcity: A Warning

As water bodies dry up and drinking water becomes increasingly scarce, Kerala is heading toward a severe drought. Our state, which generally enjoys a balanced climate, has reached a point of critical water shortage this time. If government systems, the general public, and voluntary organizations act proactively and in unison, we can overcome the impending drought-related hardships to a significant extent.

Priority must be given to drinking water, which serves the fundamental need of sustaining the lives of humans, birds, and animals. We must be careful to limit usage when water is required for other purposes during these critical times.

Moderation in Water Usage

In many places, we see a trend where increased comfort in life is treated as a license to waste resources. Even for religious acts like the «wudu» - the ritual ablution, the religion mandates using only the necessary amount of water. Leaving taps running carelessly and allowing water to leak from damaged supply systems are common sights in our land.

Sharing Water and Social Responsibility

If the fresh water in our wells remains after meeting the needs of ourselves and our families, we must recognize that it is also the right of our neighbors and the local community. Let this drought be a catalyst for fostering a culture of sharing water. The situation where water is turned into a commodity and monopolized must not be encouraged under any circumstances. We must not allow any large corporation to thrive by draining the lifeblood of the common man.

Popular Interventions for Water Conservation

In Kerala, which was water-abundant in any season, the time when fresh water becomes a rare commodity is not far off. Voluntary organizations must come forward to formulate and implement grassroots-level projects to reclaim natural public water reservoirs such as rivers, streams, ponds, and backwaters.

Awareness campaigns are essential to encourage the construction of new public ponds and wells in drought-affected areas. Effective systems for free water distribution need to be implemented during times of drought.

Causes and Solutions for Drought

Global warming and the significant decrease in annual rainfall are being pointed out as the reasons for the early onset of drought this year.

Our society must realize that Allah is the only one who sends down rain, and there is no other way but to pray to Him. Wherever possible, congregational prayers for rain can also be organized.

The impending water crisis offers us some realizations.

Water serves as the fundamental element of life. In a sense, the Earth is habitable only because of the presence of water. Every time it rains, it demonstrates the harmony of the laws established by the Lord of the Universe.

Allah says:

"And We made from water every living thing. Then will they not believe?" (Holy Quran 21:30)

The question posed by the Quran to those who live in arrogance and ignorance, forgetting God, is very relevant:

"Say, 'Have you considered: if your water was to become sunken [into the earth], then who could bring you flowing water?'" (67:30)
