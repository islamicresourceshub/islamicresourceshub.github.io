Vacation Time and Family Visits

അബൂഫായിദ · 2018-04-28 · Family & Society

As their father prepared to leave the house, Najwa ran up to him and asked, "Father, may I come with you?"

Smiling, the father replied, "Of course. I am just heading to the market."

Hearing this, Faisal came running from inside and said, "Father, isn't this vacation time? Why don't we go on a trip somewhere?"

"Since school and madrasa are both closed, we could go visit some relatives," their mother suggested.

Let us visit Uncle's house

"In that case, children, let us visit your uncle's house today," the father proposed.

"We want to go on a trip, Father. My friend Fayaz told me there is a flower show happening in Ooty. He said it is beautiful to see," Faisal said, expressing his wish once again.

"My children, those trips cost a lot of money. I am suggesting something that does not cost much but earns us rewards from Allah. You already go on trips with your school, don't you?" the father explained.

"Father, you visit our relatives quite often. Why do you always go there?" Najwa asked, expressing her curiosity.

Smiling, the father replied, "What is wrong with that? Don't you know that visiting relatives is something commanded by the religion of Allah?"

"We are commanded to visit our close relatives?"

We are commanded to maintain family ties

"Yes, my dear. We are commanded to maintain family ties. We must visit them regularly. That is how we ensure the bond between them and us remains unbroken," the father explained.

"Then from now on, whenever you go, I will come with you," Najwa said.

"I will come too," Faisal added, not wanting to be left out.

Setting off for Uncle's house

And so, they set off for their uncle's house. Their uncle and his family welcomed them with joy. As soon as they arrived, they were served delicious juice. Their uncle's daughter gave Najwa a box of chocolates and said:

"These are delicious chocolates. A gift for you and Faisal for coming to visit us."

Najwa handed it to Faisal. Opening the box and popping a piece of chocolate into her mouth, Najwa said, "Aunt, I will be coming here often with Father from now on. Father has explained to me the importance of maintaining family ties."

"What a good girl. You should visit all the family homes. Maintaining ties between close relatives increases mutual love. Allah will maintain ties with those who maintain them, and Allah will sever ties with those who sever them," the aunt said.

As they returned home after lunch, Najwa and Faisal were very happy. Their joy grew even more when their father promised to take them to all their relatives' homes before the vacation ended.
