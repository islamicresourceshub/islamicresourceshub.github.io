**Part: 1**

![](/assets/islamic-parenting-significance-and-goals-part-1-20170114-4/14Jan17-14.jpg)

The term 'parenting' is highly significant. It has become an area of equal concern for everyone living in cities and villages, whether educated or not. The abundance of publications and training classes on this subject clearly indicates this. It is no surprise that parenting has become a field requiring attention, planning, and training to secure a decent position and some advancement amidst the fiercely competitive opportunities of life. The realization that success and excellence are not automatic occurrences but a mission to be carefully applied from a child's early life, considering time and circumstances, prompts parents to seek its formulas.

If parenting is a discipline conducive to achieving success and excellence possible within the short span of this material world, then **Islamic parenting** extends beyond those limited boundaries.

## What is Islamic Parenting?

Islamic parenting is the weighty responsibility parents undertake to nurture their children, considering and training them according to the Creator's laws and directives, so that a human being can achieve their full potential as a devoted servant of Allah.

> 'Every child is born in a state of *fitra* (pure natural disposition). It is their parents who make them a Jew, a Zoroastrian, or a Christian.'

This prophetic saying is noteworthy.

Islamic parenting is not merely for minor successes in this worldly life. Rather, it is a mission that enables one to experience security and peace in this life, while also facilitating entry into Paradise and salvation from Hellfire in the eternal life.

Allah says:

> ''O you who have believed, protect yourselves and your families from a Fire whose fuel is people and stones, over which are [appointed] angels, harsh and severe.'' (**Surat At-Tahrim**: 6)

To fulfill this weighty mission, Allah did not merely issue a royal decree. Instead, He has provided precise guidance and a model necessary for its effortless execution. Islamic parenting is a task that must be carried out with careful study, teaching, patience, planning, and continuous prayer.

## Define the Goal

Any long-term project requires defining its goal and mission. The primary goal of Islamic parenting is to raise an upright citizen (**waladun salih**). The ultimate goal, however, is the development of a person with the leadership qualities of the **Muttaqeen** (the pious). Among the deeds that remain after death, the Prophet (PBUH) enumerated a righteous child who prays for the deceased. There is no doubt that a parent who maintains precise planning, effort, and patience can yield excellent results. Among the qualities of the good servants of the Most Merciful (**Ibadur Rahman**) that Allah described is the culture of praying to reach the leadership of the Muttaqeen. Indeed, prayer is the residue of hard work, isn't it?

In short, Islamic parenting is a comprehensive project aimed at shaping a Muslim worthy of finding a place somewhere between a 'waladun salih' and an '**Imamun Muttaqi**'. We should not seek its formulas in the books or training classes of experts who do not even acknowledge the afterlife. Instead, we must seek them in the scripture of the Creator, the designer of the human body and mind, in the life messages of His Messenger, and in the life experiences of the righteous predecessors who drew light from them. This column intends to embark on a journey of exploration along that path.
