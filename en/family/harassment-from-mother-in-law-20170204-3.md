**Question:** I have been married for a few years now. I am facing severe mental harassment from my mother-in-law, and my husband is not providing any solution to this. I have young children, and these mental and physical health struggles are taking a heavy toll on me. In such a situation, is it wrong for me to ask my husband for a divorce?

![](/assets/harassment-from-mother-in-law-20170204-3/04Feb17-14.jpg)

**Answer:** This is a question that many sisters wish to ask. Therefore, we hope the answer will be beneficial to many.

## The Husband's Duty and the Wife's Rights

Ensuring decent accommodation and a comfortable life for the wife he has married and brought home is part of the **husband's duty**. "Lodge them where you dwell according to your means, and do not harm them so as to distress them" (**Quran** 65:6).

> "Lodge them where you dwell according to your means, and do not harm them so as to distress them." — Quran 65:6

It is clear from this Quranic verse that the wife should reside with her husband and that he must not intentionally cause her distress. The husband is obligated to provide the necessary facilities and security for his wife. In a prophetic narration reported by **Imam Muslim**, we find: "Fear Allah regarding women, for indeed you have taken them as a trust, an **amanah**, from Allah, and their intimacy has been made lawful to you by the word of Allah." If the husband intentionally fails in this duty, it will weigh heavily against him.

> "Fear Allah regarding women, for indeed you have taken them as a trust, an amanah, from Allah, and their intimacy has been made lawful to you by the word of Allah."

## Harassment from the Mother-in-Law: Solutions

Another question that arises here is whether this sister should be forced to live with and care for a **mother-in-law** who mistreats her. A husband caught between such a mother and his wife experiences immense mental stress. In this situation, rather than becoming a mere complainant, it is highly beneficial for the wife to understand her husband's heart, stand by him, and seek practical ways to resolve the issue together.

## Patience and Divorce

One must understand that trials and difficulties are a part of life, and maintain hope that Allah will soon grant ease in this world and reward in the hereafter. There are indeed situations where **divorce** is the only solution. However, if seeking a divorce would bring greater hardship, distress, and a sense of abandonment to her, her young children, and her husband, then enduring the current situation with patience might be the better path.

## Lessons from Experience

Experience shows that those who lose patience in difficult times and make hasty, extreme decisions often regret them later. On the other hand, those who chose to remain patient have not only gone on to lead happy family lives with their husbands and grown-up children in their own homes, but their mothers-in-law also eventually became a cherished part of their families, living with them with a sense of remorse and a reformed conscience for their past mistakes. If one can manage to do this, it would be the best course of action for the questioner and others in similar situations. May Allah alleviate the hardships of these sisters and grant them a life of peace and comfort.
