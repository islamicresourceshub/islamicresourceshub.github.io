Those who take on the mission of **Islamic parenting** must find the correct answer to the question of who or what children are. One can only interact constructively with something when one understands its nature from its very source. The One who entrusted children to us is the only One who can provide the true answers to these questions.

What is the depth, breadth, scope, and limitation of our relationship with our children? What perspectives should we maintain and what stances should we adopt during their growth, their struggles, and even during times of loss, such as death? The **Holy Quran and the life of the Prophet** define children for us in various ways.

Love, interest, and affection for children, as well as the insatiable desire to have them, are qualities that **Allah** has instilled in humans as a biological nature. If this were not the case, the human race would not survive on earth.

## Children are an adornment in worldly life
When we see children, our eyes find coolness, our minds find joy, and our hearts find happiness. Allah says:
> “Wealth and children are the adornment of worldly life...” (Al-Kahf: 46).

> “Beautified for people is the love of that which they desire - of women and sons, heaped-up sums of gold and silver, fine branded horses, and cattle and tilled land. That is the enjoyment of worldly life...” (Al-Imran 3:14).

## Children are a gift from the Creator
Addressing the Children of Israel, Allah says:
> “And We provided for you with wealth and sons and made you more numerous in manpower...” (Al-Isra: 6).

## Children are a source of coolness for the eyes as long as they follow the path of the righteous
Describing the qualities of His most beloved servants, Allah says:
> “And those who say, ‘Our Lord, grant us from among our wives and offspring comfort to our eyes and make us an example for the righteous.’” (Al-Furqan: 74).

Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) taught that the compassion shown toward children is placed in the heart by Allah, and those who lack it are deprived of divine mercy. In a hadith narrated by Aisha (may Allah be pleased with her), we find:
> A Bedouin came to the Prophet (peace be upon him) and asked, “Do you kiss children? We do not do so.” The Prophet (peace be upon him) replied, “I cannot help you if Allah has removed mercy from your heart.” (**Adab al-Mufrad**, **Bukhari**).

In a hadith narrated by Usama (may Allah be pleased with him) in Bukhari and Muslim, it is recorded:
> Upon receiving the news of his grandchild's death, the Prophet (peace be upon him) arrived, took the child's body in his lap, and his eyes filled with tears. When Sa'd (may Allah be pleased with him) asked, “O Messenger of Allah, what is this?” (Are you weeping?), the Prophet (peace be upon him) replied, “This is mercy, which Allah has placed in the hearts of His servants.”

## The gender of children is determined by Allah
Parents often wish for more children, or specifically for more boys or girls. Allah teaches us that the determination of gender is within His sovereign authority, and regardless of the gender, children should be accepted as an *amanah* (trust) entrusted by Allah.
> “To Allah belongs the dominion of the heavens and the earth. He creates what He wills. He gives to whom He wills female [children], and He gives to whom He wills males. Or He makes them [both] males and females, and He renders whom He wills barren. Indeed, He is Knowing and Competent.” (Ash-Shura: 49-50).

Islam teaches that the tendency toward gender discrimination is a remnant of the dark ages, and that having daughters is not a shame or a burden, but rather a source of goodness, protection, and blessing. The social mindset that views the birth of a girl as a disgrace is a part of the darkness of the Age of Ignorance, no matter how modern the exterior may seem. Criticizing the attitudes of the uncivilized and ignorant fathers of Makkah, Allah says:
> “And when one of them is given tidings of that which he attributes to the Most Merciful as a comparison, his face becomes dark, and he suppresses grief. He hides himself from the people because of the evil of that whereof he has been given tidings. Should he keep it in humiliation or bury it in the ground? Unquestionably, evil is what they decide.” (An-Nahl: 58-59).

However, some dark traces of this vile mindset still persist in society today. The words of the Prophet (peace be upon him) regarding the virtue of having daughters and the reward for raising them well are highly significant.

Anas ibn Malik (may Allah be pleased with him) narrated that the Prophet (peace be upon him) said:
> “Whoever raises two daughters until they reach maturity, he and I will be like this on the Day of Resurrection (and he joined his fingers together).” (**Muslim**).

Uqbah ibn Amir (may Allah be pleased with him) narrated that the Prophet (peace be upon him) said:
> “Whoever has three daughters and shows patience toward them, and provides them with food and clothing from his wealth, they will be a shield for him against the Fire.” (**Ahmad**)
