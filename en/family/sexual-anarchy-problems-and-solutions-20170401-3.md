# Sexual Anarchy: Problems and Solutions

Islam requires that an individual should exercise their sexuality only through the permissible bond of marriage. All other relationships are destructive. Extramarital sexual relations sow seeds of suspicion in marital life and create psychological fractures that haunt one's future. In a context where illicit relationships are becoming widespread, it is essential to analyze sexual morality.

*An illustration representing sexual anarchy*

Sexuality is a divine blessing. Its primary purpose is procreation. Mental health, family stability, and peace in social life are all linked to sexuality. Its use outside of proper guidance can lead to mental instability, the breakdown of family cohesion, and the destruction of the social fabric. Most sexually transmitted diseases in humans are caused by extramarital sexual activity.

Islam requires that an individual should exercise their sexuality only through the permissible bond of marriage. All other relationships are destructive. Extramarital sexual relations sow seeds of suspicion in marital life and create psychological fractures that haunt one's future.

In Kerala society, which is eager to embrace Western culture, the results of studies regarding the deviations in this area are deeply concerning to those with a moral conscience. It is reported that in the capital city of Kerala, an average of 300 people arrive every month to confirm whether the child born to their spouse is actually their own!

Extramarital affairs are becoming a common sight that shatters family bonds. It is worth contemplating how there are people in our country seeking legal recognition for the sexual anarchy known as same-sex relationships.

The society envisioned by Islam is nothing like this. To ensure a calm family atmosphere and a peaceful marital life, extramarital relationships must be completely avoided. Islam teaches all the necessary guidelines for a person to use their sexuality in the best way and to prevent them from falling into corrupt paths. "Do not approach unlawful sexual intercourse. Indeed, it is ever an immorality and is evil as a way" (Quran 17:32). The Quranic command is not even to approach adultery. The Prophet ﷺ taught the world that everything leading to it must be strictly avoided.

Islam does not view adultery only as physical contact between unrelated men and women. Unnecessarily looking at unrelated women, or watching obscene videos and images through the internet or television, are all forms of unnatural adultery. Engaging in voice calls or direct conversations with unrelated men and women, or indulging in obscene talk, are all forms of adultery. Most illicit relationships begin in this manner.

Describing the qualities of the humble servants of the Most Merciful, the Quran states: "And those who do not invoke with Allah another deity or kill the soul which Allah has forbidden, except by right, and do not commit unlawful sexual intercourse. And whoever should do that will meet a penalty" (25:68).

The Prophet ﷺ has warned of the terrifying state awaiting those who depart from this world having committed this major sin. While describing the punishments of Hell he was shown, he said:

"...Then they took me further. Suddenly, I saw a group of people who were very bloated and emitted a foul odor. Their smell was like that of a latrine. I asked: 'Who are they?' They said: 'They are the adulterers and adulteresses'" (Bukhari).

Another Hadith describes a terrifying punishment that adulterers will face after death:

The angels Jibril and Mika'il came to the Prophet ﷺ. The Prophet ﷺ says: "We went until we reached something like an oven, narrow at the top and wide at the bottom. Inside were naked men and women. A flame would rise from beneath them, and when it reached them, they would scream due to the intensity of the heat. I asked: 'O Jibril, who are these people?' Jibril ﷺ said: 'These are the men and women who committed adultery'" (Bukhari). This is their punishment until the Day of Judgment. When asked about the sins that cause people to fall into Hell the most, the Prophet ﷺ answered: "The tongue and the private parts" (Bukhari).

Islam also strongly opposes unnatural same-sex relationships. Many countries in the world have legalized this. The Quran tells the story of the destruction that befell the first community to engage in this vile act. The world remembers this evil by associating it with the name of that tribe. The people of 'Sadum', to whom Prophet Lut ﷺ was sent, were the pioneers of this. Allah says:

"The people of Lut denied the messengers. When their brother Lut said to them, 'Will you not fear Allah? Indeed, I am to you a trustworthy messenger. So fear Allah and obey me. And I do not ask you for it any payment. My payment is only from the Lord of the worlds. Do you approach males among the worlds and leave what your Lord has created for you as mates? But you are a people transgressing.' They said, 'If you do not desist, O Lut, you will surely be of those who are evicted.' He said, 'Indeed, I am, toward your deed, of those who detest.' (He prayed): 'My Lord, save me and my family from what they do.' So We saved him and his family, except for an old woman among those who remained behind. Then We destroyed the others. And We rained upon them a rain. And how evil was the rain of those who were warned."

Allah has preserved that severe punishment as a lesson for those until the end of the world. That is the Dead Sea.

**Self-gratification**

The evil of masturbation, where an individual finds sexual pleasure by themselves, is also increasing in the world today. Scholars have reached a consensus that such an act is fundamentally forbidden, even if it is done through one's own effort. Anyone who understands the basic teachings of Islam knows that the religion, which has contributed a noble culture to the world, would never encourage such a vice.

It is noteworthy that Allah specifically mentioned sexual purity among the qualities of the successful believers who will attain Paradise (23:1-7).

Based on this, scholars have evaluated masturbation as a sin. It is also not physically beneficial. A person loses seven times the energy through this act compared to engaging in intimate relations with their spouse. For semen to be released from the body in a proper manner, complete stimulation must occur. Complete stimulation is not possible in this act. Therefore, the body spends a large amount of energy on this activity, which turns into significant fatigue for the body.

The new generation that delays marriage is often addicted to this bad habit. Through this, the Islamic instruction to marry is obstructed. Even if marriage takes place later, it will be difficult for such individuals to lead a happy marital life. Any person who masturbates is subject to mental adultery. Therefore, it is impossible to see this as anything other than a sin. Islam introduces as sins those things that cause unease in the heart and which one dislikes for people to know about.

Islam is the religion of nature. It contains the laws and commandments of the Creator of the world. While raising awareness about sexual deviations, Islam also suggests clear solutions. The first solution suggested by Islam is to get married when one reaches the age of marriage. It is essential for any capable and able individual to marry. The Prophet ﷺ said: "O young people! Whoever among you can afford to marry, let him do so, for it is more effective in lowering the gaze and protecting the private parts" (Bukhari, Muslim).

Parents who do not facilitate the marriage of their children who have reached the age of marriage by citing minor reasons are acting against the laws of the religion of nature. If studies or related matters are the obstacles to marriage, anyone who understands sociology and psychology would agree that studying after marriage is the most beneficial path. Modern studies prove that because mental difficulties and stress are relieved through marriage, one gains much more enthusiasm for studies. If the difficulty in marriage is financial, Islam urges not to fear it.

The Prophet ﷺ said: "There are three whom Allah is bound to help: ...the one who seeks to marry, wanting to remain chaste" (Ibn Adi, Sahih al-Jami' - 3152).

Controlling evil thoughts and not turning one's gaze unnecessarily toward unrelated women is among the solutions to protect sexual purity. "Tell the believing men to reduce [some] of their vision and guard their private parts. That is purer for them. Indeed, Allah is Acquainted with what they do" (24:30).

It is a widely known truth that unnecessary looking is the reason for many people's lives going astray. If a person is unable to marry, another path suggested by the Messenger ﷺ is fasting.

"O young people! Whoever among you can afford to marry, let him do so, for it is more effective in lowering the gaze and protecting the private parts. And whoever cannot afford it, then let him fast, for it will be a shield for him" (Bukhari).

To maintain sexual purity in life, self-awareness regarding one's own body is essential. Any person who uses their sexuality in a corrupt way does so by surrendering to their desires and demonic impulses. No human falls into Hell without it being the result of actions that subverted their own body's design. Therefore, whenever an evil thought occurs, one must have this realization. One must understand that it is a path to Hell.
