**I have noticed that my husband maintains relationships with other women through social media and phone calls. What should I do to bring about a change in him?**

## The First Step

The first thing you must do is ensure that your assumption is entirely correct. If this is merely a suspicion, the repercussions could be severe, as suspicion is often sinful. The **Holy Quran** states:

> "يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا اجْتَنِبُوا كَثِيرًا مِّنَ الظَّنِّ إِنَّ بَعْضَ الظَّنِّ إِثْمٌ..." (49:12).

A family should not be destroyed by unnecessary suspicion.

## Things You Can Do

However, since modern circumstances provide more opportunities for such illicit relationships, here are some suggested actions:

1. Make your husband aware of the dangers of illicit relationships in some form. This is a violation of Allah's laws and a grave betrayal of his wife. Seeking illicit affairs despite Allah having blessed him with a pleasant married life is a form of severe ingratitude. The consequence of ingratitude is terrifying; Allah says:

   > "وَإِذْ تَأَذَّنَ رَبُّكُمْ لَئِن شَكَرْتُمْ لَأَزِيدَنَّكُمْ ۖ وَلَئِن كَفَرْتُمْ إِنَّ عَذَابِي لَشَدِيدٌ" (14:7).

2. Continue to maintain a very good relationship with your husband, characterized by loving behavior. The absence of this may lead him further into error.

3. It is dangerous for information regarding your husband's illicit relationship to reach others. This might cause him to persist in his error and prevent him from turning back.

4. It is good to keep him occupied with some work so that he does not have the opportunity to remain idle.

5. Prayers for his heart to be guided toward righteousness will also have results.

6. When illicit relationships become dangerous to the point of affecting your safety, it is better to separate.

7. For some people, complete piety may only be possible through another marriage.

8. Once he has completely turned away from his error, it is not right to continue prying into his privacy.

**Mu'awiyah ibn Abi Sufyan** said that he heard the Prophet (PBUH) say:

> "إِنَّكَ إِنِ اتَّبَعْتَ عَوْرَاتِ النَّاسِ أَفْسَدْتَهُمْ أَوْ كِدْتَ أَنْ تُفْسِدَهُمْ" (**Abu Dawood** 4244).

For the sake of the children's future and your own security, it is best to try to reform your spouse and live together as much as possible. May Allah bless you.
