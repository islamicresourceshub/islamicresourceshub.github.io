## Husband's Insistence and Family Ties

I am in great distress. My husband is very short-tempered and stubborn. Due to some misunderstandings, he has been estranged from my family for years. He doesn't have much religious knowledge and is unwilling to correct these misunderstandings. Moreover, he insists that I also become estranged from my relatives, just as he has. Do I have to obey him in this matter?

![](/assets/husband-who-encourages-breaking-family-ties-20170218-2/18Feb17-11.jpg)

The main point in the sister's question is whether one should sever ties with one's own family due to the husband's insistence. Severing family ties is something forbidden in Islam. One should not obey anyone in sin. In such matters, the fundamental principle we must adhere to is `do not harm, nor be harmed`. Here, we need to consider ways that do not cause difficulty for either the questioner or the husband.

## A Practical Approach

As a wife, if he provides and protects you with everything you need, especially if you have children, it is safer to live with him. If he dislikes you maintaining ties with your family, then living with him and maintaining ties with your family will be very difficult. Here, some practical solutions need to be found.

Such situations create a lot of mental distress in life. It is very difficult to be unable to please both the husband and one's own family simultaneously. Given that the husband is short-tempered and stubborn, appeasing him will be difficult. If it is possible to change the husband in any way, that would be the best. We should indeed hope that any person who makes mistakes will change.

> “إِنَّ اللَّهَ يَأْمُرُ بِالْعَدْلِ وَالْإِحْسَانِ وَإِيتَاءِ ذِي الْقُرْبَىٰ وَيَنْهَىٰ عَنِ الْفَحْشَاءِ وَالْمُنكَرِ وَالْبَغْيِ ۚ يَعِظُكُمْ لَعَلَّكُمْ تَذَكَّرُونَ” (ക്വുർആൻ 20:44)

If this is what Allah instructed in the case of Fir'awn (Pharaoh), then we should hope that change can occur in anyone.

## Care in Conduct

When unpleasant experiences occur from the husband, the wife begins to distance herself. Then the husband becomes more provoked and resentful. This is a mistake many wives make. Sometimes that distancing can even lead to divorce. As a husband is a person who should be respected and obeyed, whatever unpleasant experiences occur, one should fulfill one's duties precisely and try to draw closer by behaving better.

> ”وَلَا تَسْتَوِي الْحَسَنَةُ وَلَا السَّيِّئَةُ ۚ ادْفَعْ بِالَّتِي هِيَ أَحْسَنُ فَإِذَا الَّذِي بَيْنَكَ وَبَيْنَهُ عَدَاوَةٌ كَأَنَّهُ وَلِيٌّ حَمِيمٌ“ (41:34)

If that happens, it will be a permanent solution to the problems. If the husband's situation does not change, it is best for you to explain the situation to your family and continue living with him. You can expect your family to be ready for any cooperation for your good future. Create opportunities to connect with your family in a way that does not provoke your husband, sincerely pray to Allah, and wait patiently. May the Lord, who grants ease with every difficulty, resolve your difficulties with ease!
