America's wealth and military dominance were not built solely by the labor of the American people. Over time, countless individuals have arrived in America from various countries in search of work. It would be well for Trump to take a moment to look back at history.

## America's History and Immigration
America entered the pages of history with the arrival of Italian explorers **Christopher Columbus** and **Amerigo Vespucci** in the fifteenth century. While many may have passed by before, the world only began to learn about America with the arrival of these European sailors. The original inhabitants were the 'Indians' (Amerindians) who arrived from Asia via the Bering Strait 15,000 years ago, and the 'Eskimos' (Inuit) who lived in the northern parts of the American continent.

As America gained fame through the travelogues of Amerigo Vespucci, European nations like Portugal, Spain, England, France, and the Netherlands (the Dutch) began efforts to colonize and seize power in this 'new' continent.
- The first European colony was established in Veracruz, Mexico, by Spanish sailors.
- The English seized Newfoundland.
- French explorer Jacques Cartier conquered Canada.
- The Dutch acquired Manhattan Island from the indigenous people.
- Following them, Sweden and Russia also established their own dominance.

European nations secured their power by conquering and exterminating the native Amerindians. The many infectious diseases brought by the Europeans led to the complete destruction of that society. European nations later fought among themselves to establish supremacy. On November 15, 1777, the United States of America (USA) came into existence.

## Discrimination Against Refugees
This history convinces us of the truth that America is a nation formed by countless immigrants and colonizers. However, it is the new American President, Donald Trump, who needs to be convinced of this now. As Trump slams the doors of America shut on immigrants and refugees, forgetting the very foundational nature and heritage of his own nation, one is reminded of the story of the king who wore a fool's costume. Trump's new executive order states that travelers from seven Muslim countries—Syria, Iraq, Libya, Yemen, Somalia, Iran, and Sudan—are not permitted to enter the United States. The world watched Trump's move with astonishment and, even more so, with concern. Nations across the globe strongly condemned the action one by one. The American people themselves protested against the order, transcending religious lines. Canadian Prime Minister **Justin Trudeau** responded to Trump by welcoming more Muslim refugees. France, Germany, the European Union, and the United Nations have been at the forefront of the protests. Companies like Apple, Google, and Facebook have also come out against the order.

Meanwhile, American legal experts and judges are challenging Trump's order, putting him further on the defensive. Federal Judge **James Robart** intervened in the matter and ruled that Trump's order was unconstitutional, which deeply unsettled and provoked Trump. However, when Trump appealed to the court to reinstate the order, claiming that the President has more authority than a judge, the court ruthlessly dismissed his application. It was a blow to Trump's arrogance.

## The Politics of Walls
Immediately upon coming to power, Trump decided to build a massive wall to prevent infiltration from the neighboring country of Mexico. The plan is to build a wall spanning 3,200 kilometers at a cost of 5 billion dollars. Trump's deranged decision also demands that Mexico bear the cost. However, Mexican President Enrique Peña Nieto bluntly stated that he would not pay a single cent. Furthermore, he canceled his previously scheduled visit to the United States. Trump forgets that it was the people who eventually tore down the infamous Berlin Wall, which was built by the communist regime in East Germany on the border of West Germany in 1961. Moreover, the struggle against the Berlin Wall was described by America and its then-President, Republican leader **George W. Bush**, as a symbol of human freedom. It is an irony of history that today, the President from that same Republican Party, Donald Trump, is working to build another wall.

Trump should also not forget that the influx of refugees from Syria, Iraq, and elsewhere is the result of the chaos and insecurity created by America itself. Every human being desires to live in their own land. But those who intervene to restore peace in countries where unrest and instability are spreading should not be slamming the doors of their own countries in the faces of those fleeing for their lives. Countries, borders, and governments are merely pictures drawn by humans. Everywhere, it is humans with flesh, blood, and brains who live. Their colors may differ, but the blood flowing through their veins and the beating of their hearts are the same. Beyond race, color, caste, and nation, governments and rulers must show the wisdom to see humans as humans.
