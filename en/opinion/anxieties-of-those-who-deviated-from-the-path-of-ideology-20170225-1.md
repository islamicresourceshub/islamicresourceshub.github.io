A response to the article written by **KNM** leader **Abdussalam Sullami** in *Shabab* weekly regarding unity.

![](/assets/anxieties-of-those-who-deviated-from-the-path-of-ideology-20170225-1/25Feb17-11.jpg)

I read the article written by Abdussalam Sullami in *Shabab* regarding unity. Without deviating even slightly from his characteristic style, he has filled this article with numerous distortions and allegations that do not align with the truth.

This article serves as the final proof that the entire KNM, in the name of unity, is moving toward the distorted *Tawheed* (monotheism) of the Madavoor faction. Sullami has openly written that the KNM has arrived at the arguments of the Madavoor faction regarding cause-and-effect, the unseen, and the metaphysical, and that it was commendable to ruthlessly expel those who opposed this. Since *Shabab* is now the official organ of the KNM, we can consider this a confession. It was the Madavoor faction councilors themselves who released the audio recording of **Ali Madani** admitting, in response to a worker's question about sorcery before the merger, that the alliance with the KNM was a 'Hudaybiyyah Treaty'. This means the 'conquest of Makkah' following the Hudaybiyyah Treaty mentioned by Ali Madani has drawn a little closer for the Madavoor faction.

While celebrations of unity are taking place, rendering the ideologically conscious members of the KNM mere spectators, the frustration over certain aesthetic rifts is evident throughout the article. Perhaps to dispel that frustration, a futile attempt was made to weave together numerous allegations to mislead the Mujahids. Let us analyze them one by one.

## Who Subverted Tawheed?

Sullami's first attack is directed at the Mujahids, who have been organizing lectures across the corners of Kerala under the title 'Prayer is Worship itself'. His allegation is that they claim worship and prayer are two different things. He has taken words out of context and fabricated lies of his own. This is exactly what the Mujahids expect from someone who wrote that a matter found in the Book of Allah was a Jewish belief and attempted to invent a Quranic verse for something that does not exist in the Quran.

Another allegation is that there has been a subversion regarding the cause-and-effect relationship, the unseen, and the metaphysical. Anyone with primary knowledge of the Mujahid movement's *Tawheed* can easily identify the deviation that has occurred in the Madavoor faction regarding the aforementioned subjects. Look at the response given by **K.M. Maulavi** to the question, 'Can we call upon the Badr martyrs and ask for help?'. From this, the gap between the *Tawheed* of the Madavoor faction and the *Tawheed* of the Mujahid movement will be clear to everyone.

> He wrote: ''When a person is caught in a distressing situation, the request to be saved from it can come in two ways. 1. To ask for help to be saved through things that usually happen by the hands of creatures, meaning to ask for help through ways that creatures are capable of according to the normal cause-and-effect relationship. It is a fact that everyone agrees that *Istighatha* (seeking help) from creatures in this manner is permissible...'' 2. 'Beyond such matters, to ask for help to be saved through ways that do not usually happen by the hands of creatures—such as forgiving sins, guiding to the straight path, sending down rain, healing illnesses, warding off calamities and afflictions, removing them and saving one, helping by protecting from enemies—in matters that are in the treasures of Allah alone and where Allah alone has the power, meaning to ask for help through *Ghaib* (unseen) ways. Everyone agrees that *Istighatha* in this manner should not be done to anyone other than Allah...'' ''Therefore, it is certain that our questioners here are asking about seeking help or being saved through special causes beyond the primary causes, meaning those related to the unseen world...'' ''All those with human intelligence will agree that there is extreme humility and excessive glorification of those from whom one asks for a task to be performed in a way that neither they nor creatures like them are capable of.'' (*Al-Murshid*, April 1935. Citation: *K.M. Maulaviyude Fatwakal*, pages 130, 131)

This has always been the stance of the Mujahids. The root cause of the problems is that the Madavoor faction has deviated from this.

We can summarize the words of K.M. Maulavi as follows:

1. Causes are things that usually happen by the hands of creatures.
2. The unseen way is that which does not happen by the hands of creatures.
3. That which is outside the cause-and-effect relationship is called the unseen (Ghaib) way.
4. Such requests are prayer.
5. That is something no creature is capable of. Therefore, seeking what is beyond the capability of creatures is prayer.
6. It is in such situations that extreme humility and excessive glorification occur.
7. No intelligent person would have any doubt about this.

Sullami called these clear ideological points of the Mujahids 'ramblings'.

From this *Tawheed*, the Madavoor faction strayed. They said: ''The unseen way means that which is beyond the five senses.'' (*Sensing*, April 2007, interview with Salam Sullami)

That is how the Kerala public heard the new argument that angels and jinn are outside the cause-and-effect relationship. Because we do not see them, do we? If one asks what happened with that, the Madavoor faction arrived at a very strange *Tawheed* that if one believes angels or jinn intervene in human life, *Shirk* (polytheism) occurs. They wrote: ''If anyone believes that angels and jinn can help us, *Shirk* and *Kufr* (disbelief) occur in their faith.'' (*Tawheedum Nava Yathasthithakarude Vyathyayanavum*, page 11, Youth)

Then, when the question arose about whether Satan helps the sorcerer in sorcery, they had to come up with an argument unknown to predecessors or successors: that he does not help, and that believing sorcery has reality is *Shirk* (*Vichinthanam*, February 3, 2007, credit). At the same time, the KNM opposed this argument. Back then, the Madavoor faction responded that Mujahids, including **A.P. Abdul Khadir Maulavi** (Rah), were *Mushriks* (polytheists). (*Sensing*, April 2007)

Three proofs have now emerged that they still stand by this:

1. In the speech Ali Madani gave at the Madavoor faction council, he said about the unity that this is a 'Hudaybiyyah Treaty'. That treaty was between Muslims and *Mushriks*.
2. In *Shabab* released three days after the unity conference (23.12.16), it was written that believing the Prophet (PBUH) was affected by sorcery is *Shirk*.
3. In *Shabab* dated 17.02.17, this distorted *Tawheed* was repeated again.

Sullami also wrote that the KNM accepted it. Since *Shabab* is now official, we can assume that is correct. If so, they have certainly moved from the *Tawheed* of the Mujahids to the '*Tawheed*' created by the Samastha faction, which claims that 'non-physical powers exist for others besides Allah'. Let the intelligent think!

## Are Mujahids Opposed to Unity?

Sullami has tried his best to portray Mujahids as opponents of unity. On one hand, write that the KNM ideology is *Shirk*. On the other hand, hold up the formula of unity. Mujahids have questioned this hypocrisy. Therefore, people are not going to believe it just because he shed crocodile tears by calling it opposition to unity. It is ideological unity that is needed; the Mujahids truly desire that it should happen. Kerala is well aware of the troubles that arise from uniting without ideological alignment.

Another of his complaints is that no one is raising issues like covering the face, folding hands in *I'tidal* (standing after bowing), or rising with clenched fists. In reality, none of these are controversial issues. They are jurisprudential matters where differences of opinion are possible. Trying to create unnecessary controversy by dragging in non-controversial issues is an attempt to divert attention from the core of the matter.

The problem here is the denial of fundamental matters related to *Aqeedah* (creed) such as sorcery, the evil eye, Zamzam, the Black Stone, *Mi'raj* (ascension), punishment in the grave, the False Messiah (*Dajjal*), the descent of angels at Badr, the *Sirat* (bridge), and the *Mizan* (scale). It is ridiculous to try to make jurisprudential matters controversial to cover that up.

Moreover, many things like the sitting in a two-rak'ah prayer, folding hands in *I'tidal*, moving the finger, sitting in the mosque after *Subh*, and rising with clenched fists are things that have come down in the *Hadith*.

However, no one here has criticized anyone for not doing these things. Nor have they branded them as *Mushriks*. That is not the real issue here. Many things are being denied; those who accept them are accused of *Shirk*. They are being labeled as polytheists. How are these two things equal?

Furthermore, there are plenty of people in the current KNM who do these aforementioned things. It is unfortunate that the author failed to notice that this criticism applies to those within his own movement as well.

## Sullami's Translation and the Mujahid Movement

Sullami has openly written that his **Bukhari** translation has no connection with the Mujahid movement! *Alhamdulillah*! This is something the Mujahids have been reminding him of for a long time. It contained many things that the Mujahids could never accept. This translation, which denies many things like Zamzam, *Dajjal*, punishment in the grave, *Mi'raj*, *Nuzul* (descent), the Black Stone, sorcery, the evil eye, and the *Sirat*, has been exposed before society as being diametrically opposed to the Mujahid ideology. However, for so long, the Madavoor faction was justifying it and protecting these errors by saying they would not allow Sullami to be isolated. It is good that there is enlightenment now.

However, the same things that Sullami released through his Bukhari translation have also come out through official publications like *Shabab* and *Ath-Tawheed*. Sullami can disown his translation if he wants. But the organization must change what it officially published. That has not happened until this moment. Therefore, no one should think that they will be saved if Sullami's translation is thrown into the canal.

Sorcery (*Shabab*, April 4, 2014), punishment in the grave (*Shabab*, April 11, 2011), Zamzam (August 14, 2015), the Black Stone (*Shabab*, August 7, 2015), the evil eye (*Shabab*, May 13, 2016), making music *Halal* (*Ath-Tawheed*, November 2011), is Allah the only one with non-physical power? (*Shabab*, July 13, 2013), *Nuzul* (*Shabab*, July 10, 2009), *Khabarul Ahad* (solitary reports) cannot be used for faith (May 13, 2016), the devil is metaphorical (December 12, 2014), denial of *Tabarruk* (seeking blessings) (March 30, 2012), *Mi'raj*, *Sirat* (*Salafikal Jalpanangalum Vasthuthayum*, Youth, pages 15, 50, 51)—all these were written and officially published by Sullami and his followers. All of these contain clear denials. The Mujahids will not back down unless they renounce all of this and apologize to society. The issue here is not individuals; it is ideology.

To prove that Sullami's translation has no connection with the movement, the justification that it was not published by the KNM is not correct. The policy of a believer is that one should only write and speak the truth, regardless of who publishes it. Can one say that many works of Usman Doctor, Umar Maulavi, and Amani Maulavi have no connection with the Mujahid ideology just because they were not published by the KNM? No intelligent person would say that. If his policy is that only what is written officially needs to be ideological and any denial can be written in other things, Sullami should say that openly. The common man who reads Sullami's books thinks all of this is the religion. Moreover, advertisements for Sullami's Bukhari translation appear in *Shabab*, and it is sold by Sullami's supporters. This confession has ended up deceiving them all.

Many errors that occurred in Abdussalam Sullami's works were pointed out by many scholars at the time, and they were asked to correct them. But he did not listen. He was even told to correct them at Jamia Nadwiyya. But he was not prepared to correct them. There are witnesses still alive today for this.

The remark that the footnotes provided in the Bukhari translation were merely quoting predecessors for the sake of increasing knowledge is also not factual. An example: In the footnote of Hadith number 5196, Sullami wrote: ''This Hadith makes it clear that there is no *Sirat* bridge.'' He has not quoted any predecessor in this. No one has said that before either.

Look at the footnote he wrote for another Hadith related to the *Sirat*: ''Scholars are likely to criticize this Hadith by keeping in front of them the fundamental principles of the Holy Quran, the descriptions given by the Quran about Hell and entry into it, human primary intelligence, and other established Hadiths.'' Look! The person who said I am only quoting the criticisms of predecessors is criticizing it himself without any predecessor having said any criticism. Therefore, he is often not quoting the criticism of predecessors. It is his own. And that, mostly, is what Chekanur and C.N. raised!

## Differences Among the Companions and Differences in the KNM

Sullami is trying to drag in the jurisprudential differences that existed among the Companions to justify the current differences within the KNM. All the Companions were scholars with research capability. Naturally, there would be different methods among them in the matter of understanding evidence. Just like when the Prophet (PBUH) said not to pray *Asr* except at Banu Qurayza, the Companions accepted it in two ways. One group accepted it literally. Another group accepted the concept within it. The Prophet did not criticize either group.

Is that how the issues in the KNM are? A group that denies and mocks many known things in Islam, and another group that opposes this! A group that believes the other side is in *Shirk*! Another group that says they are not in *Shirk*! How can this be compared to the Companions?! In reality, this is an insult to the Companions. Therefore, let the average intelligent Mujahid think!

Finally, let me say one more thing; in the rush to accuse others of *Shirk* and *Kufr*, it is good for everyone to do some self-reflection to see if they have arrived at any arguments of *Shirk*. We need to grasp the seriousness of arguments like 'there are non-physical powers other than Allah', 'Allah is not the only one who intervenes non-physically', etc. May Allah help us to stand with the truth.
