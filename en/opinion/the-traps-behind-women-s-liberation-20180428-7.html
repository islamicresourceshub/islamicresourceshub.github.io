The Traps Behind Women's Liberation

ഉമ്മുഖദീജ പൂക്കത്ത് പറമ്പ് · 2018-04-28 · Opinion & Analysis

What is Women's Liberation?

In modern society, women have been reduced to cheap commodities. It is her nudity that is being sold. She often fails to realize that by displaying her nudity, she becomes uncivilized and a subject of ridicule. She participates in protests labeled as 'Kiss of Love' or 'Breast-baring protests'. Women need to recognize the political and media agendas behind these movements that make them objects of mockery in the eyes of society. If all this is in the name of women's liberation, then what exactly is women's liberation?

In the early 18th century, women gained the freedom to cover their chests through the 'Channar Revolt'. Today, some women are taking to the streets to protest for the 'freedom' to bare their chests, based on a misunderstanding of a teacher's words. The arguments for and against this need to be evaluated in a flawless manner. Many criticize Islam's approach toward women. One should not view the woman in Islam through the lens of the Muslim community, but rather identify her through Islamic sources. Those who rage against Islamic culture are often the ones who criticize Islamic laws, principles, and rituals without attempting to study Islam based on its authentic sources or evaluate its beliefs and practices.

Identifying Through Islamic Sources

One does not become a Muslim simply by being born into a Muslim family, adopting a Muslim name, or having 'Islam' written as their religion in official documents. Rather, one becomes a Muslim only when they follow the Creator's commandments, that is, the Holy Quran and the traditions of the Prophet ﷺ.

"O mankind, fear your Lord, who created you from one soul and created from it its mate and dispersed from both of them many men and women. And fear Allah, through whom you ask one another, and the wombs. Indeed Allah is ever, over you, an Observer" (Quran 4:1).

Remember that this call is addressed to all of humanity. In reality, the Creator is the one who knows His creation best. We are the ones who decide the operating procedures for the vehicles and tools we create. Therefore, we will only receive true protection as creations when we follow the commandments of the One who sent us to this earth, having granted us hearing, sight, hearts, and the provisions of life, without us having decided any of it ourselves.

"It is not for a believing man or a believing woman, when Allah and His Messenger have decided a matter, that they should [thereafter] have any choice about their affair. And whoever disobeys Allah and His Messenger has certainly strayed into clear error" (Quran 33:36).

Allah on Modesty in Dress

Allah speaks about the dress codes that women who have embraced true faith must specifically follow:

"O Prophet, tell your wives and your daughters and the women of the believers to bring down over themselves [part of] their outer garments. That is more suitable that they will be known and not be abused. And ever is Allah Forgiving and Merciful" (Quran 33:59).

When adopting this style of dress, one often experiences an extraordinary sense of security. The lecherous gazes of men do not affect her. Just as men were commanded to lower their gaze and guard their private parts, the Quran commands women to do the same. In the 31st verse of Surah An-Nur, which commands women to draw their head coverings over their chests, it is also pointed out from whom beauty should be hidden and that they should not strike their feet to make noise to attract others. This long verse ends by providing a sense of purpose, reminding us that all of this is for the sake of achieving success.

"O Prophet, tell your wives and your daughters and the women of the believers to bring down over themselves [part of] their outer garments. That is more suitable that they will be known and not be abused. And ever is Allah Forgiving and Merciful" (Quran 33:33).

This verse points out that: - One should not leave parts of the body that ought to be covered exposed. - One should not wear thin clothing that reveals the body underneath. - One should not wear attire designed to attract men. - One should not use cosmetics in a seductive manner.

If a woman does not adorn herself for her husband inside her home, but dresses up and puts on makeup when stepping outside, whose pleasure is she trying to serve?

Leading Them to the Best State

The Holy Quran provides these instructions not to demean women, but to lead them to the best state and help them achieve success in the hereafter.

"Indeed, this Quran guides to that which is most suitable and gives good tidings to the believers who do righteous deeds that they will have a great reward" (Quran 17:9).

There is no doubt that the laws, instructions, warnings, and prohibitions in the Quran are beneficial to those who think and are God-fearing. Those who are enchanted by the superficial glamour of modernity are the ones who have mistakenly assumed that this is what true life is. The Prophet ﷺ said:

"The world is sweet and green, and verily Allah is going to make you vicegerents in it in order to see how you act. So beware of the world and beware of women. Verily, the first trial of the Children of Israel was in women" (Muslim).

The Woman in Islam is Honorable and Noble

The Quran (4:34) states that 'Men are the protectors and maintainers of women'. From the subsequent verses, we understand that this is because He has given some men certain advantages over women in creation. This has also been scientifically proven. Therefore, absolute equality between men and women is an impossible concept. The laws of the Quran protect the woman and grant her rights. Men have been given responsibilities and the authority to manage affairs. In matters of inheritance and bearing witness, she is considered according to her physical and mental conditions. Remember matters that require strong intervention, such as Jihad or leading prayers, which are designated for men. Excluding women from these is a recognition of her physical conditions and is an act of mercy toward her. She is permitted to go to the mosque for prayer, but it is not made mandatory for her as it is for men.

In Islam, a woman is honorable and noble. Islam honored her by giving her the right to live and the right to property. She performs her duties in society in various roles such as daughter, wife, sister, mother, mother-in-law, and grandmother, and is the administrator of the home. She is the school for her children. She plays a vital role in molding good citizens. A Muslim woman has the right to receive honorable treatment from her husband. She is the one who protects everything in her husband's absence. The 32nd verse of the 33rd chapter of the Quran warns her not to speak softly to other men, lest someone with a disease in his heart should covet her. By placing great importance on caring for orphans and widows, women are protected. While allowing polygamy, it also warns men that if they fear they cannot maintain justice among them, they should marry only one. A woman in Islam should be one who satisfies her husband and brings him peace.

Enjoining Good and Forbidding Evil

A Muslim woman must be modest.

'Modesty is part of faith' (Imam Ahmad, Tirmidhi) This saying of the Prophet must not be forgotten by either men or women. Islam views a woman revealing her nudity or displaying her beauty to men other than her husband as an immoral act. If a woman reveals her nudity to someone other than her husband, the veil of modesty that Allah has granted her will be removed.

Just like seeking knowledge, enjoining good and forbidding evil is mandatory for every Muslim.

"By time, indeed, mankind is in loss, except for those who have believed and done righteous deeds and advised each other to truth and advised each other to patience" (Quran 103: 1-3).

Islam does not teach that women are exempt from these duties. For her success in the hereafter, she herself must believe and perform righteous deeds.

"And whoever does righteous deeds, whether male or female, while being a believer - those will enter Paradise and will not be wronged, [even as much as] the speck on a date seed" (4:124).

Remember that just as there is a right in this country to live as one pleases, indulging in evils and acting recklessly, there is also the freedom to live as good people by doing good and raising one's voice against evil. However, it is regrettable that there is a trend today of cherishing cultural decay and viewing the invitation to goodness as a crime. We must not remain blind to the traps hidden behind the women's liberation movement.
