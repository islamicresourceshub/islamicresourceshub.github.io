Why Freedom?

Naseema Vadanappally

Image: A person standing and looking towards the horizon.

As the atmosphere filled with the clamor of Vande Mataram on Independence Day, she picked up her pen to write a note on the subject of freedom. When brave warriors stepped forward for the country's independence, Indians stood united in the background, with the exception of a few traitors. That is how India became free.

What is the situation today? The rulers are not foreigners. Yet, they impose restrictions even on the legitimate freedoms of the people.

Today, everyone demands a different kind of freedom. Those who use political freedom as a cover for communalism are tarnishing politics, stripping it of truth and integrity. Some demand sexual freedom to satisfy their lust. There are even depraved individuals here who argue that sexual relations with one's own mother or sisters are acceptable if consent is obtained, or even with animals, and they have their own fronts of protest. Some demand the freedom for liberal and gender-neutral ideologies to grow and flourish.

Those addicted to drugs who kill their own parents desire the freedom to use intoxicants at will. While suicides increase due to usurious interest rates, those in the finance sector demand the freedom to raise interest rates further. Spiritual exploitation under the guise of religious freedom knows no bounds. There are also those who demand the freedom to lead life according to their own whims and, if necessary, to end it.

How, then, can one write about freedom? She crumpled up each piece of paper she had written on and threw them away. A short while later, the gentle breeze that crept in with the drizzling rain slightly cooled the inner rage within her. As her mind shifted from emotion to reason, she picked up her pen again:

O human, where did you go wrong? How did you, known as the Super Animal, degenerate lower than the beasts? Yes, your moral decline began the moment you made death the very goal of your existence.

The Holy Quran asks: «هَلْ أَتَىٰ عَلَى الْإِنسَانِ حِينٌ مِّنَ الدَّهْرِ لَمْ يَكُن شَيْئًا مَّذْكُورًا» (76:1) - Has there not come upon man a period of time when he was not a thing even mentioned?

Yes, what were we? Where did we begin? Where will we end? What do we have that we can truly call our own? There are many questions.

It is the One Lord who created us from a state of nothingness, sustains us, and determines the span of our lives. The realization that Allah is my Lord and I am His servant is what transforms a human into a true human being and enables them to fulfill the purpose of life.

Along with her writing, her lips kept repeating these words. The solitude of the night, the drizzling rain, and the gentle breeze that accompanied them stood as witnesses.
