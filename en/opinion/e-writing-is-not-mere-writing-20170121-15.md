"Do not let anyone touch me except my husband"—this was the 'news' that the media sensationalized. A young Muslim couple meets with an accident and falls into a river. The husband, who does not know how to swim, screams for help as his wife is swept away. Locals rush to the scene. Suddenly, a soldier appears as a savior. As the onlookers watch, he jumps in to rescue the woman. And then, the climax! The woman cries out that no one but her husband should touch her. The husband stands by, helpless. The locals are shouting to save her, the woman is refusing to be touched, and the hero is left bewildered, not knowing what to do... Finally, the hero takes a leap of faith and saves her... Bam! The story ends!!

This is not a story from cartoons like Superman or Spider-Man! This is a top-tier 'news' piece, embellished and dramatized by the media. Whoever read it sat with their chin in their hands, expressing 'sympathy.' The local tea shops got a new 'thread' for gossip... Intellectuals analyzed the event 'philosophically.' It was a total spectacle!

It was in the midst of this that the protagonist, the soldier, appeared with a Facebook post. He clarified that what was read in the media were merely embellished stories with only a distant connection to reality. He expressed his frustration with such lies, stating that these fabricated tales would make people hesitate to help in future accidents. Once he spoke out, social media took over.

What followed was the collapse of the imaginative stories spun by the 'grand-old' newspapers that compete by boasting about their millions of readers and subscribers. Social media tore apart every nook and cranny of the 'story.' This incident is just one of many blows to the assumption held by these newspaper giants that society will accept anything printed in ink as gospel truth.

It is not just about bringing the truth behind fake stories to light; social media has much to say about things the media has kept hidden from the outside world. It was the clever minds in 'e-spaces' who dubbed mainstream media as 'Mukkiya-dhara' (a play on words implying 'suppressed' or 'hidden' stream) for their habit of burying news. When the media turned a blind eye to Gail Tredwell’s *Holy Hell*, which exposed the frauds of Mata Amritanandamayi, the 'e-world' protested by framing PDFs of the book across the internet. One can also see how they selectively promote only 'suppressed news,' realizing that the media giants, fed by advertisements, will not even print a small column against those who feed them.

Thus, e-writing spaces are not just parallel media; they are platforms for open writing and artistic expression, free from editors and the fear of red lines. The ability to reach thousands with a single click and receive real-time feedback from readers might be the factor attracting more people, including prominent figures, to these spaces.

**The Evolution of E-Writing**

Although blogs have taken a slight backseat recently due to the overwhelming presence of social media, they have always been the most important platforms for e-writing. Blogs can be described as a space to pour out one's thoughts instantly or as a shelf where articles can be organized and stored systematically. Consistent, creative engagement also earns a loyal readership. Though not as popular in Malayalam, the blogging sphere has grown into vlogging today. From professional cameras to smartphone cameras, everything is now wide open to the truth.

Perhaps because interaction with society and readers is easier than on blogs, social media writing has gained more acceptance. Today, everyone from celebrities to politicians is active on social media with their pens.

**We Need Islamic Writing!**

Just as everything has a good side and a flip side, so does e-writing. While on one hand, the potential for 'open writing' is used to speak uncomfortable truths and correct the public consciousness 'created' by the media, on the other hand, there are those who use this same freedom to spread propaganda and communal hatred. One group achieves its selfish interests by circulating expertly doctored photos and posts filled with falsehoods. The social media wings of certain fascist organizations stay awake just to spread such propaganda.

If on one side there is anti-Islamic sentiment created by mainstream media serving imperialism, on the other, we see organized anti-Islamic ideological campaigns in the e-world that twist and cut out *Quranic* verses and *Hadith*. There are dozens of blogs and Facebook pages today that copy-paste even the Islamophobic rhetoric of Zionists and missionaries to spread it in Malayalam. In this smear campaign against Islam, rationalists, missionaries, and fascist forces are joining hands.

An experience shared by a preacher who engages in ideological debates with Christians seems noteworthy here. When he went directly to the center of a Christian apologetic network—which uses its blog and Facebook page solely to criticize and mock Islam—to conduct *Da'wah*, he found the Christian pastors sitting there studying Amani Maulavi’s *Tafsir*, other Islamic texts, and *Hadith* translations! The fact that we have to face these paid critics in e-spaces, who scour Islamic texts looking for loopholes to twist and misinterpret, highlights the relevance and seriousness of our mission.

Besides misleading non-Muslim friends who are leaning towards or showing interest in Islam, the goal of these paid writers is to create confusion among ordinary Muslims!

While we can dismiss the Facebook statement of a prominent rationalist leader in Kerala—who claimed that his anti-Islamic blog writing has managed to confuse at least some Muslims and that he would disgrace the religion in the eyes of the public—as the boastful claim of a lizard supporting a wall, we must not forget the fact that at least a few are indeed being misled about Islam by these campaigns.

When I made a small attempt to compile the criticisms raised in such Islam-critical blogs and sites, I found hundreds of them. Many of them are things that even an average Islamic preacher might not have even heard of! But if one tries to take each one and study it, it becomes clear that they are just houses of cards without any foundation. However, while they spend twenty-four hours a day, paid to churn out these allegations, those on our side are busy with their jobs and studies!

One could say that e-writing spaces are like an empty wall in the middle of a city. One can use different colors to paint beautiful pictures on that wall. The same wall can also be used to write obscenities. Regrettably, it is the distorted and twisted face of Islam that fills blogs and pages today.

We need those capable of wielding the pen for truth and justice here. When poisonous anti-Islamic articles, trolls that kill ideological discourse, and online media that sow Islamophobia present a distorted image of Islam, it is we who must demonstrate the power of corrective writing through these same e-spaces and dispel the misunderstandings.

It is said that by the time truth puts on its shoes, falsehood has already traveled half the distance. Here, falsehood has already traveled more than half the distance. We must not sit idle without putting on our shoes any longer... Let everyone use their own skills and potential to resist evil. In this era, where media that fosters Islamophobia is rampant, let us recognize the vast potential of e-writing spaces to write out uncomfortable truths.
