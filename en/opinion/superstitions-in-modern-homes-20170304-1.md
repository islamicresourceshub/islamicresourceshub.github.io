## Imprisoned by Superstition

Even if we were to travel to Mars and return, the superstitions of the enlightened Indian would remain unchecked. Behind the rockets and spacecraft launched into space at the cost of billions, there lies the story of the tireless efforts of great minds who have spent their lives in research, often sacrificing their own family lives and ignoring their basic needs for food and sleep. Yet, it is regrettable that we continue to witness actions that instantly trivialize such immense efforts and dedication, as people lock themselves within the chambers of superstition.

Why is there no room number thirteen in the building of the **High Court**, the nerve center of the judicial system in enlightened Kerala? Does this not clearly show how even the most enlightened are subjugated by superstition?

## The Public Exchequer and Superstitions

No one seems ready to react even when billions from the public exchequer are written off to sustain superstitions. The construction cost of the **Shivaji** statue being installed in the sea in Mumbai is said to be around three thousand five hundred crores! No one asks what the 'great achievement' for the nation is in spending such a massive sum to install a statue in the sea. Those who ask are ostracized and sometimes branded as unpatriotic.

Indians are not unfamiliar with the pathetic plight of the millions of slum dwellers in Mumbai who work from dawn to dusk just to make ends meet and sleep in slums. They are forced to attend to their basic needs on railway tracks and along public streets. Are we to believe that those who govern the country do not know that even a fraction of the billions being poured into the sea could improve the lives of these slum dwellers?

## Rulers and Astrology

Those who perform Caesarean sections to ensure a child is born at an auspicious time and date are not uneducated. Those who check the stars and timing to announce a death are not mere commoners. It seems that court astrologers have convinced our leaders that if a death is not announced at a specific time, some calamity might occur.

There was deep-seated superstition behind the delay in announcing the death of former President **K.R. Narayanan**. Prime Minister **Manmohan Singh** had a pre-scheduled program that was determined by checking the stars, Rahu, and Gulikan. Once a death is officially announced, the Prime Minister's scheduled programs would have to be halted. Finding another suitable time that is not 'attacked' by Rahu, Ketu, Gulikan, or Kalan would require waiting a long time. Therefore, despite being a former President, K.R. Narayanan remained 'alive' until Prime Minister Manmohan Singh's programs were concluded!

It is an open secret that the lifeless body of **E. Ahamed** was carried out after he collapsed inside the Parliament. Yet, the news of his death was announced fifteen hours later! Why did this happen? The next day was February 1st, the day of Vasant Panchami. Astrologers had informed the government that if the budget could be presented on Vasant Panchami, the government would reap certain benefits, while presenting it on another day would lead to negative consequences. What else but this superstition related to Vasant Panchami could have compelled the government to wait until after entering February 1st to announce the death, rather than adjourning the house to pay respects upon the member's passing, all to ensure the budget could be presented?

## The Unfortunate Ones in History

Jayaprakash Narayan and I.K. Gujral have been recorded in history as the 'unfortunate' ones who had the 'luck' to be included in the list of those who 'did not die even after dying' and those who 'died before they actually died'.
