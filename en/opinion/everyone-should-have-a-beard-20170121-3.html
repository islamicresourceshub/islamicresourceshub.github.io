Everyone Needs a Beard!

അബൂമിസ്‌യാല്‍ · 2017-01-21 · Opinion & Analysis

O. Abdulla Sahib and his ability to dismantle anything with his pen

O. Abdulla Sahib is a person who has proven many times that he has an extraordinary ability to dismantle anyone or anything he dislikes with his pen. It is also a fact that Islam is sometimes among the things he tries to destroy. He has no hesitation in performing ideological somersaults according to the interests of his readers and the channel owners he appears before.

The reason for this recollection

The reason for mentioning this here is that I recently saw an article of his in a daily newspaper. He began the article by recounting the history of a Khutbah (sermon) he delivered, and then he went on to attack the beard. There is no particular reason for it either. The secretary of the 'Salafi' mosque where the Khutbah was held did not have a beard! So, let's kick the beard! What a situation!

The excuse of lacking taqwa

Abdulla Sahib says that he used to grow a beard, but since the caution and dignity required for the beard did not sprout in his heart, he decided not to insult the beard hairs and abandoned it halfway. He also argues that the Prophet (peace be upon him) commanded the growing of a beard only to oppose the Jews and Christians.

Understanding the Hadith

What is the meaning of the Hadith, "Oppose the polytheists; grow the beard"?

"നിങ്ങള്‍ ജൂതന്മാരോട് എതിരാവുക; താടിവളര്‍ത്തുക"

Even if one assumes that its literal meaning is intended, it cannot be evidence for shaving the beard today. The Prophet (peace be upon him) and the prophets before him, such as Prophet Musa (as) and Prophet Isa (as), had instructed their followers to grow beards.

The example of Hajj and Umrah

He cites the examples of Raml (brisk walking) and Idtiba' (exposing the right shoulder) during Hajj and Umrah as actions performed to oppose the polytheists. He argues that since there are no such polytheists today, these actions are unnecessary. If we follow such reasoning, the state of Islam would be in a sorry condition.

Critique of the Salafi movement

He has abandoned many ideals but still holds onto his opposition to the Salafis. He agrees that the beard is a Sunnah, but he is confused about why Salafis show negligence in other areas. He criticizes Salafis for their negligence regarding societal decadence. However, it is the Salafis who are most active, dynamic, and practical; they are guided by wisdom and knowledge. The mention of ISIS is often used as a tool to malign them.

The beard as a symbol

When the September 11 attacks occurred, there was advice for American Muslims to shave their beards for safety. But today, beards on the faces of people in high positions do not hurt anyone's eyes. The terror associated with the 'ISIS beard' is no different from the fear of 'Buddhist demons' in other contexts.

Conclusion: Everyone needs a beard!

All the prophets had beautiful beards, and they were never an obstruction. Abdulla Sahib has a habit of trying to cut the beards of the prophets as well. Even Maududi had a beard.

  • The beard is a symbol of faith.
  • It is a Sunnah that carries the legacy of the prophets.
  • It should not be abandoned due to the perceived negligence of others.

Everyone needs a beard!
