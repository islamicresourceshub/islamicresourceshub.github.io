Muslim Extremists: Those Who Tarnish Islam

എം.ജി.എസ്‌ നാരായണൻ / റസ്റ്റം ഉസ്മാൻ · 2017-01-14 · Opinion & Analysis

From an interview conducted by Rustam Usman with the beloved historian of Kerala, M.G.S. Narayanan... his brief assessments on the changes brought by Islam, the Kerala Muslim renaissance, extremism, and more.

Religious Leadership Against English Education

How do you view the opposition from religious leadership towards English education before the start of renaissance movements among Kerala Muslims?

Thinkers of the sixteenth century, such as Zainuddin Makhdoom, did not perceive conflicts between religions. In Tuhfat al-Mujahideen, one can see the call for Muslims to wage Jihad against the foreign, aggressive Portuguese, under the leadership of the Zamorin and the Hindus. By the nineteenth century, the idea emerged that regional languages were haram (forbidden). This did not exist before; if it had, the language of Arabi-Malayalam would never have been created. This is the language used by poets like Moyinkutty Vaidyar to write their songs. Just as Manipravalam—a blend of Sanskrit and Tamil—emerged around the time the Malayalam language was forming, the Arabi-Malayalam tradition also existed. However, by the nineteenth century, conservative religious clerics had tightened their grip. Veliyankode Umar Khazi came forward at that time, calling for the study of English and Malayalam. Vakkam Moulavi emerged with his renaissance activities. In the mid-twentieth century, Farook College was established. At that time, there was a prevailing notion among Muslims that Muslim women should not be educated. The Muslim women who studied at that institution later became the proponents of reform.

Saffronization

This is a time when saffronization is alleged to be happening in all sectors. What is your opinion on this?

That is a propaganda slogan. I have never felt that 'saffron' is bad. In the past, it was adopted as a symbol of renunciation of worldly life. The RSS is attempting to politicize it. As a result, others have reached a state of hating the color saffron itself. Just as Islamic terrorists are those who insult Islam, those who misuse saffron are insulting Hindu culture. Those who encourage aggressive tendencies are the ones who do not know what Hindu culture is. The pure ideologies that existed during the time of the Prophet and thereafter are rooted in human unity and the doctrine of monotheism. Those who cannot understand this are the ones carrying out violent acts while claiming to represent Islam.

Extremism and Misconceptions About Islam

The activities of extremists like ISIS are helping to create a situation where Islam is misunderstood. How do you evaluate this?

This is something found in almost all societies. Among those who emerge as followers of any movement—whether they are Communists, nationalists, Hindus, Christians, or Muslims—such tendencies can be seen in all of them. It is a fact that terrorist tendencies are being carried out in the name of Islam. The responsibility to defeat this lies with true Muslims who believe in the Prophet, the Quran, and the purity of Islam.

Similarly, there are social evils, superstitions, and caste divisions among Hindus. The fundamental responsibility to defeat them lies with the Hindu believers themselves.

RSS and BJP members act as the saviors of Hindus. Since they do not read the epics of the land, the Ramayana and the Mahabharata, they argue that the Pushpaka Vimana is a scientific fact and not poetic imagination. The ancient scholars had a clear understanding that none of this was history. That is why the Ramayana was described as the Adikavya (first poem) and Valmiki as the Adikavi (first poet). Otherwise, they would have called it Adicharithram (first history) or Adikavitha (first poem). It is through this that India's philosophies and Vedantic thoughts reached even the villages. However, there will be superstitions inserted in between. Sometimes, the people of those superstitions might appear as the people of true faith.

This has happened in Islam as well. Even though those who destroyed the World Trade Center acted as if they were the saviors of Islam, they only succeeded in blackening the name of Islam before the world. Consequently, those with beards and Muslim names are seen as figures of fear in the eyes of Americans.

Changes Brought by the Quran

What are the changes brought by the Quran?

It brought unity among various tribes that were previously at war with each other. The energy of that unity fostered self-confidence in them. They were already connected to other cultures through trade, weren't they? It was with the rise of Islam that they gained a leadership position in all those connections.

Islam is not a new religion. It is a renewed form of older religions. One cannot say there is a vast distance between the philosophy in the Quran and the philosophy in the Old and New Testaments of the Bible. Excluding temporal and regional differences, the core truths are the same in all three Semitic religions. It is the same tradition. Other philosophies are merely manifestations of this same tradition in different languages, races, and forms. One cannot say there is a contradiction in that. That is the result of misunderstanding. When Islam was rising in Arabia, the people there did not have much information about India. They only had some vague notions.
