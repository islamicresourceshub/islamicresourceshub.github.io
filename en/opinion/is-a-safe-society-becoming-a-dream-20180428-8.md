## Minds That Have Not Frozen in Humanity

Asifa departed from this world, leaving behind a history written in the tears of all those whose humanity has not yet frozen. Her murder was not merely an act of lust, but one fueled by the blind poison of communalism. Even as those in power boast about the nation's security and fearlessness, the state stands shamed before the world due to an unparalleled sense of insecurity. Why are there so many instances of violence against women and communal conflicts in India? This is an objective study.

![](/assets/is-a-safe-society-becoming-a-dream-20180428-8/28Apr18-03.jpg)

Asifa! Even uttering that name causes terrifying memories to surface again and again. Reading the police records of how an eight-year-old girl was starved for days, drugged, repeatedly abused by many, and then strangled—only to have her head smashed with a rock to ensure she was dead—is enough to shatter one's heart. Who would not be shocked to learn that this heinous act was committed inside a Hindu temple in **Kathua**, Kashmir, by a priest, his son, his nephew, and their friends? They even called people from kilometers away, telling them there was an 'opportunity' to come and tear that tender body apart.

We read with horror that this incident, which numbs the mind more than any other, had another dimension. It is said that the act was committed with the intention of terrorizing the Muslim community in Kathua and driving them out, so that the area would be inhabited only by Brahmins! Asifa, who knew nothing of the world, had committed no crime other than being a Muslim. The state must answer the question: Has being a Muslim in this great country of India become a crime punishable by days of physical, mental, and sexual torture, followed by a brutal death?

## This Incident is a Litmus Test

This incident is a litmus test. It is a gauge that can accurately measure the different schools of thought in the country, examine the stability and security of the nation, and clearly delineate all our anxieties and hopes.

On one hand, it reveals the terrifying face of the communal divisive politics that is eating away at the country. To describe this incident as 'bestial' would be an injustice to animals, for even animals would not behave with such diabolical cruelty. Yet, when we learn that a protest was held demanding the release of these monsters, we realize the gravity of the situation. Many rapes and murders may have occurred across the world, but has it ever been seen anywhere else—not just in India, but globally—that a protest was held to demand the release of rapists simply because they belonged to the same religion or political ideology as the protesters, while the victim was of a different faith? Is there any greater example of how much the **Sangh Parivar** forces have brainwashed a large section of society, fostering the mindset that they can do anything, no matter how cruel, to those they 'other'?

This cannot be dismissed as the actions of a crazed mob. The march demanding the release of the accused, Deepak Khajuria, was addressed by BJP ministers in the Jammu and Kashmir cabinet, specifically Choudhary Lal Singh (Forest Minister) and Chander Prakash Ganga (Commerce Minister)! When this news spread on social media, some communal fanatics from Kerala also stepped forward with justifications, which is not a matter to be taken lightly. If, even in Kerala—a land we pride ourselves on being enlightened—there are people who refer to a brutally murdered child as a 'future terrorist' (based on her religion) and celebrate her death, then we must be concerned that the country's secularism is in danger.

When we learn that these wretched creatures did not even allow the child's body to be buried in her own locality, we must speak louder that this is not just a crime born of communalism and lust, but a deep-seated mindset. It was not that there was no six-foot plot of land available; it was because the communal fanatics, whose bloodlust was not sated even after abusing and killing her, would not allow it, forcing Asifa to be buried in another place eight kilometers away. The fact that they even tried to threaten and intimidate the lawyer who stood up to argue for the child is just another point to consider. As usual, those in power have maintained their tradition of silence regarding the violence and harassment faced by minorities, backward classes, and Dalits.

## The Communal Fascists of the Sangh Parivar

While it is a stark reality that the communal fascists of the Sangh Parivar stand as a threat to the nation, its integrity, and its spirit of brotherhood, we must not overlook the rays of hope. A Kashmiri Pandit police officer, Ramesh Kumar Jalla, was at the forefront of investigating the incident impartially and bringing its horror to light. It was a non-Muslim lawyer, Adv. Deepika, who stood firm against the threats of the Sangh Parivar to raise her voice for Asifa in the court of law. The signals and hope provided by these actions are not small.

Social media showed the protests of the majority secular society, which has not been subjected to the brainwashing of the Sangh Parivar, overflowing. Beyond caste, religion, and politics, people stood together on the side of justice and secularism. Protests and cries for justice rose across the country. Prominent figures from political, cultural, and artistic fields came forward to intervene strongly. They were ready to declare that this is not a Hindu-Muslim issue, but a conflict between communal fascism and Indian secularism.

## Yes, This is an Open Struggle

Yes, this is an open struggle. It is a struggle over whether the freedom won by Hindus, Muslims, Sikhs, believers, and non-believers standing shoulder to shoulder should be granted to everyone. It is a clash between the dream of the nation's founders, who said 'Hindus and Muslims are the two eyes of Mother India,' and the fascist ideology that 'foreign religions are thorns in the eyes of Mother India, and as long as they are not removed, those eyes will remain troubled.' These are not arguments based on isolated incidents; this is the fundamental ideology of the Sangh Parivar organizations, which they have clearly proclaimed.

> 'The non-Hindu peoples in Hindustan must either adopt the Hindu culture and language, must learn to respect and hold in reverence the Hindu religion, must entertain no idea but those of the glorification of the Hindu race and culture... or may stay in the country, wholly subordinated to the Hindu nation, claiming nothing, deserving no privileges, far less any preferential treatment—not even citizen's rights.' (**M.S. Golwalkar**, **We or our Nationhood Defined**, Pg. 56).

Asifa is merely the latest victim of the terrorist activities they carry out for this stated ideology. Since the time of independence, they have been working toward this goal through countless communal riots, both covert and overt. With the opportunity to come to power at the center by exploiting the turbulent political atmosphere, India has today become a playground for intolerance. Attacking and harassing minority communities, Dalits, and backward classes has ceased to be news. A list of those killed in such violence over a short period makes the reality clear:

- Mohammad Akhlaq in Dadri, Uttar Pradesh, beaten to death on the suspicion of storing beef.
- Sixteen-year-old Shahid Rasool Bhat, killed by a bomb thrown at the tracks in Anantnag district, Jammu and Kashmir.
- Mohammad Majloom and Azad Khan, beaten to death and hanged while going to sell cattle in Chatra district, Jharkhand.
- Faisal, killed in Kodinhi, Malappuram district, Kerala, for converting to Islam.
- Riyas Maulavi, murdered inside a mosque in Kasaragod district.
- Abu Hanifa and Riazuddin Ali, killed in Assam on false allegations of cattle theft.
- Pehlu Khan, killed in an attack by cow vigilantes in Alwar.
- Ghulam Mohammad, killed by Hindu Yuva Vahini activists in Uttar Pradesh.
- Seven youths beaten to death by a mob in Jharkhand.
- Sixteen-year-old Junaid from Haryana, killed on a train on the accusation of eating beef.
- A mosque Imam murdered during prayer in Mau district.
- Sibtullah Rashidi, the son of a mosque Imam, killed in Bengal in connection with a Ram Navami procession, and the list ends with eight-year-old Asifa.

## There is No Solution Other Than Standing Together

What I am saying is that the greatest challenge the country faces is this communal polarization. A country that prided itself on 'unity in diversity,' where different communities lived together like flowers of various colors and shapes in a garden, is today bowing its head before the world due to intolerance and communal riots. Because of the actions of extremists who fill minds with poison, a nation that was once strong and secure has reached a state where citizens cannot walk out with confidence. What is the solution?

There is no solution other than standing together. The very last incident we took as an example points to this single solution. That child suffered such torture and was killed precisely because she was born into the Muslim community. Those who killed her were driven by a terrifying communal mindset, aiming to drive a community out of the land they live in. However, when the issue became a national discussion, it was not just Muslims who reacted. The general public did not take it up as a Muslim issue; rather, Hindus, Muslims, people of other faiths, and those with no faith recognized it as a problem for the country itself and faced it together. We also saw secular parties setting aside political differences to protest together. Maintaining this same approach against the fascism that threatens the nation is the solution.

These communal fanatics are most obstructed by the majority of the Hindu community itself. What disturbs them is that the majority of Hindus do not approve of the antics they perform in the name of Hindus. Minorities alone can never face this threat. It is precisely because they realized this that they keep trying to turn every issue into a Hindu-Muslim conflict. Recently, a former worker of the BJP's IT cell revealed their methods, highlighting this very point: the main project of the IT cell is to give every piece of news a communal face and present it as a conflict between communities. Their agenda is to slowly radicalize even those Hindu minds that yearn for brotherhood. We must never engage in actions that fuel this fire.

## Understand and Resist the Fascist Strategy

It is very important to understand and resist the strategy of the fascists. Their main weapon is fear. Even in the last incident, they were prepared to tear that little child apart with the goal of frightening Muslims. Often, they are the ones who film and circulate videos of attacks and lynchings of Muslims and Dalits. Their goal is clear from this. We must not fail to understand their cruel strategy: to provoke other communities here through fear, and when that happens, to highlight the natural, immature reactions that might arise to provoke the Hindu community and thereby reap the benefits.

In this matter, the restraint of the Muslim community is particularly noteworthy. When Asifa's father learned that his daughter was abused in a temple, he responded, 'We searched for her everywhere, except in that temple. Because we knew it was a sacred place.' It is clear that he could not even imagine that people from among the Hindus would commit such a heinous act in a place they consider sacred. If he had uttered words that hurt Hindu sentiments upon learning that his daughter was abused in a temple, the public's reaction and perspective on this issue would have been completely overturned. This maturity and caution are what we must show in all matters.

Even here in little Kerala, they tried to provoke us to implement their communal agenda. Despite the news of Faisal being hacked to death in Kodinhi for the sole reason of converting to religion, Riyas Maulavi being hacked to pieces while sleeping in a mosque in Kasaragod, and Hadiya being kept away from the outside world for months just because she converted to Islam, it is worth noting that Muslims did not get provoked or react in a way that would destroy communal harmony. The fact that Muslims are not reacting immaturely is exactly what disturbs those waiting to sow the seeds of communalism.

## Opposition Parties Need to Unite

To strengthen the country and build a safe environment against those striving to lead the nation into insecurity, opposition parties need to unite, setting aside their differences. Those who waste time with theoretical discussions about whether fascism has arrived or not, even at this critical juncture, will surely have to pay a heavy price. Can anyone be blamed for doubting the sincerity of those who engage in unnecessary controversies and discussions that provide fodder for the propaganda of those obsessed with anti-Muslim racism? The task that secular parties must implement together is to raise the possible unity on new issues as a public consciousness against fascism.

We need awareness campaigns that convince people of the reality against those who are working tirelessly to reap political gains by spreading lies and half-truths. Propaganda that minorities are a danger to the country and that they are gaining things unfairly is often what fuels communalism. We must be able to correct such perceptions. We must convince society that no religion calls for riots or hatred of other religions, and that the actions of emotional people who do not understand religion should not be accounted for in the name of religion. Society must understand that if communal polarization occurs, it will affect not only minorities or backward classes but every Indian, and that it is the stability of our nation that is crumbling. Only from that realization can a safe and peaceful social life be possible in the country.
