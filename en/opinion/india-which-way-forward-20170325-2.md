## Where is India Heading Today?

Everyone who wishes for a peaceful state of affairs in the country has begun to ask this question with anxiety! The danger was sensed the moment the Uttar Pradesh assembly election results were announced. With the appointment of **Yogi Adityanath** as Chief Minister—a man who founded the armed extremist group *Hindu Yuva Vahini*, initiated the first *Ghar Wapsi* in Uttar Pradesh in 2005, was jailed for his direct involvement in the 2007 communal riots, and is notorious for his venomous, anti-Muslim speeches—it has become clear that these anxieties are not unfounded. There is no doubt that this is the greatest blow dealt to the secular values of India.

## The Sharpest Weapon

With the 2019 parliamentary elections in sight, the BJP and RSS have unsheathed their sharpest weapon. It is not a positive sign that the Chief Minister of India's largest state is a hardline communalist who has called for the rape of dead Muslim women at a public meeting, initiated the *Ghar Wapsi* of 5,000 Christians, declared that "if one of ours is killed, we will kill four of yours," and suggested that for every Hindu girl who becomes a Muslim, 100 Muslim girls should be converted to Hinduism.

## Fascism Under the Guise of Democracy

Why are laws like the UAPA not invoked against such individuals? Why is the UAPA used only against certain people by interpreting their words as anti-national and extremist? This is what happens when fascism begins to play under the guise of democracy. There is no disagreement that Hitler’s Nazism in Germany, **Mussolini**’s fascism in Italy, and Zionism in Israel are ideologies of cruelty, inhumanity, and oppression. What else can one expect from those who have direct links to these, follow their models, and draw inspiration from them!

> "From 1939, the spring of fascism, **Savarkar** and the **Hindu Mahasabha** he led ultimately chose fascist Germany as their international model. A statement issued by the Hindu Mahasabha on March 25, 1939, says: 'The Hindu people in India, who are spiritually awakened and wise, welcome the German ideology for the spiritual resurgence of Aryan culture and the glorification of the Swastika'" (Religion, Marxism, Secularism, Ninan Koshy, page 49).

> "The first Hindu nationalist to maintain ties with the fascist regime and Mussolini was the RSS leader **B.S. Moonje**. Moonje was Hedgewar’s mentor and close friend. In February and March 1931, while returning from the Round Table Conference, Moonje toured Europe. He spent most of his time visiting Italy. There, he visited very important military schools and educational institutions. Most significantly, he met with Mussolini" (Religion, Marxism, Secularism, Ninan Koshy, page 48).

## India Belongs to Hindus

This is the slogan of people like Adityanath—that India is a country where only Hindus have the right to live. However, the *avarnas* (lower castes) and non-Hindus, whom *Hindutva* has ostracized, make up more than 60 percent of India's population. The indigenous people of India are *avarnas*. They are, in fact, outside the Hindu religion. Read the news of Dalit atrocities happening in North India in this context. Remember who burned several *avarnas* for skinning a dead cow and what the motive behind it was.

## Reaping Benefits by Sowing Fear

Their goal is to reap benefits by sowing fear. It is said that the 'Hindu Yuva Vahini' instructed Muslims that if they wanted to live in this land, they must chant the 'Yogi, Yogi' mantra. In this election, the slogan was 'Desh mein Modi, Pradesh mein Yogi' (Modi in the country, Yogi in the state). They might achieve temporary gains through this. But history teaches that a double backlash awaits such people. No oppressive regime has ever been able to overcome the march of truth and justice.

If secular parties realize that the majority of Hindu believers are not extremists or communalists and work together, this threat can be dismantled. What is needed is leadership with willpower.
