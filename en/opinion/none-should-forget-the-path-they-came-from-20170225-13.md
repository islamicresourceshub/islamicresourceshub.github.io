## The Goodness of 'Snehappudava'

A ceremony was taking place at the government lower primary school in my village. A social organization led by a friend of mine was distributing new clothes—a project they called 'Snehappudava' or 'Garments of Love'—to children from tribal, Dalit, and other marginalized communities. Several business friends and accounting professionals were in attendance.

No one knew that the businessman who inaugurated the event was also the sponsor of a monthly food distribution program for a family at that very school. In the middle of his speech, he shocked everyone by pledging a six-figure sum toward the school's development project. When it was my turn to offer greetings, I told the children sitting in front of me that I would share a story. Everyone listened with great interest.

## The Rise of a Bus Worker

'Once upon a time, there was a bus service running through our village. A man from a neighboring area worked on that bus. Due to financial difficulties, he had to stop his studies and start working. Eventually, he left that job and tried his hand at other things. Later, he went to Saudi Arabia, where he worked as a driver. Eventually, he took on responsibilities in logistics and distribution. He started his own shop, and over time, he brought his relatives and then his friends from home to work with him. He was fortunate to find a good Arab partner. The number of shops grew, as did the number of vehicles and employees. His business expanded to Dubai as well. He never forgot his homeland. He started a tools business back home. He traveled to China, set up a factory with local workers, and began importing goods. Thus, the man who was once a bus worker came to own over two dozen vehicles and employed nearly two hundred people.

I had the opportunity to meet him during some official work. That acquaintance grew into a friendship. Calls and messages from abroad followed. Our bond extended to our families as well. That man is here in our village today; in fact, he is here at our school for this event. He is on our stage! How many of you here have recognized him? Let us see.'

I stopped my story.

## The Honor of the Little Ones

The little children stood up. They honored him with endless applause. He accepted the love of those children with a smile.

May the children pray for more prosperity for a man who never forgot the path he came from. Not just the children, but the parents, the teachers, and my own heart as well!
