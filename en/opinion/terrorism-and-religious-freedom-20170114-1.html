The Hunt for Terrorists and Religious Freedom

Terrorism is a mental perversion that disrupts the peace of any society

Terrorism is a mental perversion that disrupts the peace of any society. Its hallmark is the production of hatred, wholesale and retail. Terrorist activities are carried out worldwide in the name of religion, regionalism, and ethnicity. It is in the context of global efforts against terrorism that discussions about the hunt for terrorists are heating up in our country.

Intelligence Systems and Wrongful Detentions

We, the residents of this country, who possess an intelligence system comparable to global standards, have much to be proud of. If those in responsible positions sincerely strive to apprehend anyone who stands as an obstacle to peace and national security, it is possible. However, what we see today is the labeling and detention of individuals who have not even come close to extremist activities as terrorists.

Sangh Parivar Agenda Behind the Hunt for Terrorists

Amidst this, there have been attempts by some law enforcement officials to single out and attack the Muslim community under the guise of hunting terrorists. Perhaps because the central government is in the hands of Narendra Modi, whose shadow looms over the Gujarat pogrom, his followers are spewing venom against Muslims. It is also to be suspected that some officials, at government expense, have taken it upon themselves to implement the Sangh Parivar agenda under the name of hunting terrorists.

Islamophobia and Dr. Zakir Naik

Some scholars, driven by the existence of Islamophobia, are engaged in insidious attempts to curtail religious freedom under the guise of hunting terrorists. The increasing acceptance of natural Islam is disturbing the sleep of many. This might be why they have laid hands on Dr. Zakir Naik, who has elevated India's pride to the highest level globally. For over two decades, he has been traveling the world spreading the message of Islam, and even his enemies have never accused him of terrorism or extremism during this time. It is noteworthy that spiritual guru Shri Shri Ravi Shankar personally engaged in a friendly dialogue with Dr. Zakir Naik and praised his abilities on several occasions. This is not to argue that this Marathi doctor is free from human-like stylistic flaws and slips of the tongue. The challenge remains that not a single call for extremist or terrorist activities can be found by searching his countless speeches.

From Chhota Bachchan's Propaganda to Actions

How quickly did the Sangh's persecution and the accompanying state terror unfold! If the fabricated propaganda initiated by the Chhota Bachchan leaders has escalated to police action and the closure of Dr. Naik's channel and other institutions, then it is time for India's secular conscience to awaken.

Attempt to Suppress Islamic Preaching

It is becoming increasingly clear that the Sangh Parivar, at government expense, is implementing a hidden agenda to prevent Islamic preaching and propagation under the pretext of hunting terrorists. The latest example of this is M.M. Akbar. It should not be forgotten that many Malayalis understood the danger of extremism through the book The Way Fascism Comes written by M.M. Akbar. There are planned efforts to chain influential Islamic speakers and preachers, who intellectually challenge communalism and terrorism, one by one. The truth is that the enemies have not started their attempts to stifle the growth of Islam by silencing preachers today or yesterday. However, history tells us that all such attempts have turned out to be mere delusions.

Time to Stand Together

We must never allow a situation where those who preach and practice religion may be stoned as terrorists sooner or later. It is high time for those active in religious, political, and cultural spheres, and all Muslim organizations, to unite and launch a strong protest against this double standard. It is the duty of every citizen to protect the pillars of democracy and secularism from being shaken.
