The Alcoholic Father

ഹാരിസ്ബിന്‍ സലീം · 2017-02-11 · Fiqh

Question: My father, who has retired from his life abroad and is living a life of leisure, is a chronic alcoholic. Since he has no other source of income, he uses a portion of the money I send him for household expenses to buy alcohol. Am I considered sinful for this?

Answer: May Allah guide your father and grant him the grace to abstain from sinful activities. May Allah grant you and those affected by his condition a sufficient reward for the hardships you are enduring.

The Gravity of Alcohol Consumption

Alcohol consumption and the use of other intoxicants are grave sins. Alcohol is something that Allah has forbidden in the Holy Quran.

''يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا إِنَّمَا الْخَمْرُ وَالْمَيْسِرُ وَالْأَنصَابُ وَالْأَزْلَامُ رِجْسٌ مِّنْ عَمَلِ الشَّيْطَانِ فَاجْتَنِبُوهُ لَعَلَّكُمْ تُفْلِحُونَ'' (5:90).

Alcohol, often described as the mother of all evils, leads a person toward every other vice. To obtain money for alcohol, one might steal. If he has a conflict with his wife, he might commit adultery. In a drunken rage, he might commit murder. He will neglect his responsibilities. Intoxication has the power to lead a person to all forms of wrongdoing. Because it is something forbidden by Allah, it creates adversity in life in many ways. It creates an addiction that is difficult to escape even if the drinker wishes to stop. It is not just the drinking companions and liquor shops, but even the sight of alcohol bottles that can draw an alcoholic back into the habit.

Those who start drinking for the sake of company, under pressure, or during celebrations often end up as full-blown alcoholics. The permanent solution is always to try and rescue those trapped in such circumstances. They can be brought back through affectionate treatment and proper medical care. De-addiction centers can assist in this process.

What You Must Do Regarding Your Father

Here is a summary of what you should do regarding your father in this situation:

  1. You must strive through effective means to bring him back to the right path so that he does not die as an alcoholic.
  2. You must ensure that his vices do not spread to others in any way. Simply abandoning him may not yield results. If you are certain that the money will be used to buy alcohol, do not give it to him. This is because Allah commands us to cooperate only in righteousness and piety: (Quran 5:2).

Therefore, you should not provide money specifically to purchase alcohol. However, you must not fail to provide for his food and other essential living needs, nor for those who depend on him.

Points to Consider

Use every possible means to bring him back. Alongside this, here are some things you should keep in mind:

  1. If there are any relatives, friends, or scholars who can influence him, try to use them to bring about a change.
  2. Keep children and other relatives in the house away from his influence.
  3. Strictly ensure that friends or associates who encourage his drinking are kept away from him.
  4. Utilize religious study circles that provide spiritual awareness and effective treatment centers.
  5. You and your close relatives should pray sincerely for a change of heart.
  6. While strongly detesting his vice, treat him with the love and compassion due to a father, and strive to change his heart. Being kind to one's father is mandatory in Islam. However, do not seek the pleasure of any person in a way that contradicts the pleasure of Allah. Remember the Prophetic tradition: 'Whoever seeks the pleasure of people at the expense of angering Allah, Allah will leave him to the people.'
