Alcohol is the root of all evil. It is impossible to completely eradicate lawlessness without a social system free from alcohol. This is why Islam has adopted a strict stance against it.

Following the government's policy shift suggesting that beer and wine are not considered alcohol, **Shamseer Mukkam** conducted an interview with **Iyyancheri Kunjikrishnan**, the president of the Anti-Liquor Committee. Below are the relevant excerpts from that conversation.

![](/assets/alcohol-islam-s-ever-relevant-approach-20170225-6/25Feb17-02.jpg)

## Alcohol and the Social Scourge

**You have been working against alcohol, recognizing it as the greatest social scourge. You dream of an alcohol-free country. Why should alcohol be opposed more than other evils?**

Islam opposes all evils. The greatest of all evils is the concept of polytheism. However, the Prophet (peace be upon him) said that alcohol is the mother of all evils. Alcohol directly affects the brain. In Arabic, alcohol is called «Khamr». The literal meaning of this word is 'that which covers the intellect.' The intellect is something that is constantly radiant, and alcohol spreads darkness over that intellect. Islam commands us to seek refuge from useless knowledge and to pray for the opening of paths toward beneficial knowledge.

To overturn the intellect is a vice; it is darkness. Once the intellect is lost, there is no distinction between a mother, a sister, or one's own offspring. A person in such a state degrades lower than an animal. Animals have certain natural laws that they follow. Human laws are divinely ordained. A human is one who must follow the command: 'Do this, and do not do that.' For animals, food, a mate, rest, and shelter are what matter. Beyond that, there are no laws they must uphold. A human can take their intellect in any direction. Allah commands that if you are grateful, walk through the path of guidance. Otherwise, Allah knows that a human can go anywhere. Allah has not left humans alone, saying 'let them go wherever they want.' He has clearly distinguished between good and evil. I chose this platform because of the realization that a plan to command good, forbid evil, and oppose that which destroys the intellect is essential for the reformation of humanity.

## The Islamic Perspective and Prohibition

**In your speeches and conversations, you frequently use Quranic verses and the sayings of the Prophet. How did you achieve this?**

Compared to other religious texts, the **Quran** is only about 1500 years old. This means the Quran is a book that never loses its freshness. All Muslims accept the Quran in the same way. Islam opposes alcohol very strictly. It is not just about those who bear Muslim names; it is about the Islamic philosophy. Islam abolished alcohol in three stages.

> First, it said: "O you who have believed, do not approach prayer while you are intoxicated until you know what you are saying."

> Later, it discouraged it by saying: "They ask you about wine and gambling. Say, 'In them is great sin and [yet, some] benefit for people. But their sin is greater than their benefit.'"

The Holy Quran forbade all evils, including alcohol and gambling, which incite hostility and hatred and are causes of destruction.

> After that, it declared alcohol forbidden, stating that it pushes humanity toward total ruin. Only after the concept of monotheism was firmly rooted in the hearts of the people was it made forbidden, with the question: "Satan only wants to cause between you animosity and hatred through intoxicants and gambling and to avert you from the remembrance of Allah and from prayer. So will you not desist?"

Malappuram is the greatest success story of our struggle. I understand that the Quran is read more in Malappuram than anywhere else in India. When I was in the tenth grade, I received a translation of the Quran. I started listening to the lectures of Mujahid scholars. I was able to read books from many Muslim organizations. Today, I have connections with prominent Muslim scholars. At home, we discuss the Quran, the Bible, and the Gita together as a family.

## Anti-Liquor Activities and Reactions

**How did society react when you entered the field with anti-liquor activities?**

Families in my village that were being destroyed by alcohol were very happy. People from all political parties cooperated. It was only when we mentioned 'prohibition' that people started to pull back. There is a section called 'liquor politicians.' Their agenda is to promote alcohol, take its money, and organize the workers. They slowly started to oppose us. However, we received support from the common people. There were no significant attacks. It was just that the liquor barons and their goons would come and say things.

## History of the Struggle and Political Interventions

**The previous government closed down many liquor shops. Did you have any notable experiences during that time?**

We decided to conduct a march through 1000 panchayats and started it. When we reached 650 panchayats, we learned that Minister Babu was sabotaging it, so we ended the march and returned to Thiruvananthapuram. As a continuation of that struggle, we raised the demand to close 418 useless bars. **V.M. Sudheeran** supported that. Finally, the Chief Minister announced that 730 could be closed. With that, we withdrew the protest. Sudheeran himself has said on many platforms that this struggle rewrote the history of the anti-liquor movement in Kerala. Some in the government lacked honesty. Oommen Chandy had to give away beer and wine parlors as a compromise. We find peace in the fact that at least this much was achieved as a result of our constant outcry.

Later, the LDF came to power. They are against prohibition. They accept the idea of 'abstinence' from alcohol. The current Supreme Court verdict is the result of our persistent efforts. The government tried to sabotage that verdict. They tried to file a review petition, but the Law Department advised that it would not work. Now, according to the Supreme Court verdict, roadside liquor shops must be removed. The government might try to sabotage this as well. We are entering the struggle again to say that it must not be sabotaged. All Muslim organizations and other religious organizations must support this.
