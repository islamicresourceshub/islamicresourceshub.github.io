## Fasting: Piety and Character

**Fasting** is a noble act of worship designed to cultivate piety and refined character. By disciplining one's life, fasting exerts a significant influence in liberating a person from corrupt habits and practices. It serves as a profound opportunity for an individual to sever ties with such vices and uproot them by subduing their base desires.

Just as the month of **Ramadan** is considered a constructive trial, a Muslim learns how to refine their thoughts, character, and behavior. Their habitual practices and imitations are re-evaluated. In this way, fasting strengthens a Muslim's resolve and teaches them to be mindful of Allah in all their actions.

## The Harmful Effects of Smoking

**Smoking** is among those things that neither religion nor reason can justify. It results in countless losses—financial, physical, and health-related.

Science has already proven a strong link between smoking and numerous diseases, including lung cancer, stomach-related ailments, chest pain, and oral cancer.

Studies indicate that millions of people between the ages of 34 and 65 die every year worldwide due to smoking-related illnesses. Furthermore, even unborn children are not spared from the devastating dangers of smoking.

## How to Quit Smoking?

You may follow these steps:

1. Develop a firm determination to quit smoking. It is among the matters that Allah has strictly addressed.
2. Set a specific time to quit. Ensure it is very soon, without unnecessary delay, in a way that reflects a change in your personality and resolve.
3. Seek help and pray to Allah for the divine assistance and strength to make that decision a reality.

    > The Prophet ﷺ said: 'If you seek help, seek help from Allah' (**Tirmidhi**).

4. Keep the dangers and evil consequences of smoking before your eyes, and remember that Allah will question you about your health, your lifespan, and your wealth.
5. If you have a classmate or friend who smokes, try to form a collective pact against smoking. This will be more effective in staying firm in your decision to quit. Allah says:

    > "And cooperate in righteousness and piety, but do not cooperate in sin and aggression" (**Quran** 5:2).

    > The Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever guides someone to a good deed will have a reward similar to the one who performs it."

6. Be wary of friends who discourage you from quitting.

    > The Prophet ﷺ said: "A person is upon the religion of his friend, so let each of you look at whom he befriends" (**Abu Dawud**, Tirmidhi).

7. Inform your wife and family of your decision. InshaAllah, they will be a great source of support and comfort in implementing it.
8. Donate the money you used to spend daily on smoking to the needy and orphans.

    > Allah says: "And whatever good you put forward for yourselves - you will find it with Allah. It is better and greater in reward" (Quran 73:20).

9. Use fasting as an effective tool to implement your decision by drawing closer to Allah. Allah said:

    > "Do you not see that Allah knows what is in the heavens and what is on the earth? There is no private conversation of three but that He is the fourth of them, nor are there five but that He is the sixth of them - and no less than that and no more except that He is with them [in knowledge] wherever they are. Then He will inform them of what they did, on the Day of Resurrection" (Quran 58:7).

10. If any psychological urge to return to smoking arises, remember the words of Allah:

    > "Indeed, those who fear Allah - when an impulse touches them from Satan, they remember [Him] and at once they have insight" (Quran 7:201).

    Likewise:

    > "And if there comes to you from Satan an evil suggestion, then seek refuge in Allah. Indeed, He is the Hearing, the Knowing" (Quran 41:36).

11. Visit a dentist to clean the stains from your teeth caused by smoking to eliminate bad breath, and subsequently use the **Miswak**, toothpaste, and a brush. Also, remember the Prophetic tradition: 'Using the Miswak cleanses the mouth and is pleasing to the Lord.'
12. Remember that you are in a blessed month of fasting, which demands the avoidance of all evil things and vices. Smoking is among the most lethal of these evils, and abstaining from it is mandatory. Allah says:

    > "He makes lawful for them the good things and prohibits for them the evil" (Quran 7:157).

13. The disease of smoking usually intensifies during the first three months after quitting. Therefore, be prepared to face all circumstances that previously triggered the urge to smoke, such as anxiety, restlessness, or the desire to please others. Seek intellectual ways to cope, as smoking does not help the mind resolve any difficulties. Know that whoever fears Allah, He will make for him a way out.

    Do not assume that smoking makes you energetic, as studies have concluded the exact opposite.

14. The willpower demonstrated during fasting and the act of abstaining from things that break the fast are great aids in quitting smoking. Therefore, seek help through patience and prayer, and do not let this opportunity pass.
15. After quitting, you may experience sleep disturbances, fatigue, irritability, or a dry mouth. This is a natural occurrence in the beginning as your body adjusts. Give yourself some rest. Avoid drinking coffee, tea, Pepsi, Cola, or other carbonated drinks after breaking your fast. Resting while lying down and bathing before sleep are beneficial for health. If necessary, consult a doctor. Maintain a good relationship with Allah to overcome these difficulties.

16. Avoid places where there are many smokers.

    > The Prophet ﷺ said: 'Whoever avoids doubtful matters has cleared himself in regard to his religion and his honor.'

17. If you are among those who believe that smoking cigarettes is permissible, why do you not praise Allah by saying 'Alhamdulillah' after each cigarette, just as you would after consuming something Allah has made permissible? Why do you not say 'Bismillah' before smoking, as you would before consuming what Allah has permitted?
18. If a cigarette is a commonly used item, why do you not use it in front of those you respect, such as your parents or superiors?
19. If there is a special pleasure in smoking, why do you not teach your children to do it?

Therefore, brother, let your commitment be true. Be certain that Allah will help you. May Allah grant you success.
