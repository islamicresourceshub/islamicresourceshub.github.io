## The Divinity and Invincibility of the Sources

The most fundamental distinction between Islam, the religion of Allah, and other faiths is the divine nature and invincibility of its sources. Today, the world is closely observing the foundational sources of Islam—**the Quran and the Hadith**—despite all the misinformation surrounding them. The primary reason for the increasing number of people embracing Islamic sources is that they provide precise, clear, and practical guidance for every sphere of human life.

## Discomfort for Those Who Love Darkness

There is no doubt that the world drawing closer to these sources creates discomfort for those who love darkness. Such unease has grown and reached a specific state today. The new allegations being raised against the Quran and Hadith are products of this condition. The truth is that those who level such criticisms have not studied the sources with an open mind or in any depth. History shows that whoever has set out to study these sources directly has eventually become a proponent of them.

## The Efforts of Opponents

Recent events suggest that opponents who have correctly recognized the power of these sources have intensified their efforts to extinguish their light. Their method involves constantly fabricating criticisms of the Quran and Hadith through news media and social media. This includes those who bear **Muslim** names and those who don the guise of scholars. They attempt to create the impression that many things in the sources, especially in the Hadith, should be discarded. As part of this, they write and speak in a manner that fosters contempt for the Hadith among the general public.

## Become a Guardian of the Sources

In truth, there is no doubt that Islamic sources—whether the Quran or the Hadith—are free from error. The fundamental mission of believers today is to proclaim this truth loudly and become guardians of these sources. In the current climate, where rejecting the sources has become a trend, the responsibilities of those with firm principles have increased. Consider these words of Allah:

> Those who follow the Messenger, the unlettered prophet, whom they find written in what they have of the Torah and the Gospel, who enjoins upon them what is right and forbids them what is wrong and makes lawful for them the good things and prohibits for them the evil and relieves them of their burden and the shackles which were upon them. So they who have believed in him, honored him, supported him and followed the light which was sent down with him - it is those who will be the successful (7:157).

> And whoever obeys Allah and the Messenger - those will be with the ones upon whom Allah has bestowed favor of the prophets, the steadfast affirmers of truth, the martyrs and the righteous. And excellent are those as companions (4:69).

> He who obeys the Messenger has obeyed Allah (An-Nisa: 80).

> ...So let those who dissent from the Prophet's order beware, lest fitnah strike them or a painful punishment (24:63).

## Ibn Kathir Says

**Ibn Kathir** states: The intent behind the Quranic verse "let those who dissent from the Prophet's order beware" refers to the laws, practices, ways, and methodologies established by the **Messenger (peace be upon him)**. Therefore, the words and deeds of any person are evaluated based on whether they align with his words and actions. If they follow him, they are accepted; if they contradict him, they are rejected, regardless of their source. In a Hadith reported by **Bukhari**, **Muslim**, and others, we find that the **Prophet (peace be upon him)** said: "Whoever performs an action which is not in accordance with our matter, it will be rejected."
