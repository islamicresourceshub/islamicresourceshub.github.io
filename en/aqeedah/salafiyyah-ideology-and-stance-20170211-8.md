Humanity requires light to navigate through darkness. Light is also essential to dispel spiritual darkness. Allah sent the prophets with such light. What they brought to the world was light upon light. That is **Islam**. It provides solutions to problems and spiritual lessons for the purity of life. The completion of Islam occurred through the final Prophet. It has traversed more than fourteen centuries since then.

## Salafiyyah: Meaning and Concept

Those who guard its original purity are the **Ahlus-Sunnah**. However, when counterfeit versions emerged even within the Ahlus-Sunnah, the name **Salafiyyah** was adopted by scholars to promote the ideology of Islam and the Ahlus-Sunnah. It is derived from the term 'Salaf', which means those who have passed before. Therefore, those who adopt the path of the predecessors and propagate it are called Salafis.

'Salafiyyah' is not a faction. It is not an invitation to follow any individual. Its strength does not lie in manpower or material resources. Rather, it lies in flawless evidence. The **Quran and Sunnah** are the constitution of Salafiyyah. It is not a regional perspective; nor is it an ideology to be confined to a single nation. It is synonymous with the ever-fresh ideology of Islam. Wherever the bright light of Islam shines in the corners of the world, Salafiyyah is there too. In other words, Salafiyyah and Islam are terms that have become inseparable.

## The Path Based on Evidence

Salafiyyah stands apart due to the clarity and authenticity of its ideological teaching. As an ideology based on the Quran and Sunnah, Salafiyyah follows the path of the righteous predecessors from the first three centuries of Islam in matters of faith, deeds, and rituals. This is based on the fact that this best generation—comprising the companions of the Prophet (**Sahabah**), those who followed them (**Tabi'un**), and those who followed the followers (**Tabi' al-Tabi'in**)—received the praise of the Prophet (peace be upon him).

They are world-class models of knowledge and wisdom. As messengers of peace, this best generation identified all innovations in religion, superstitions, and extremism, and took a firm stance against them. The policy of Salafiyyah is the same.

Another policy of Salafiyyah is to submit before evidence. This is also the requirement of the Quran and Hadith. Observe this verse of Allah:

> فَلا وَرَبِّكَ لا يُؤْمِنُونَ حَتَّى يُحَكِّمُوكَ فِيمَا شَجَرَ بَيْنَهُمْ ثُمَّ لا يَجِدُوا فِي أَنْفُسِهِمْ حَرَجًا مِمَّا قَضَيْتَ وَيُسَلِّمُوا تَسْلِيمًا

"But no, by your Lord, they will not [truly] believe until they make you, [O Muhammad], judge concerning that over which they dispute among themselves and then find within themselves no discomfort from what you have judged and submit in [full, willing] submission." (Surah An-Nisa: 65).

It is an undeniable reality that those who embrace Salafiyyah as an ideology are the ones who truly uphold this verse.

## Stance on Innovations

Salafis have adopted a stance of strongly rejecting all new inventions that have sprouted in the name of religion, as well as those who create them. This is because the Prophet (peace be upon him) issued serious warnings against such innovations. The prophetic teaching that every innovation introduced into religion is a path to the Fire is the light for Salafis on this subject.

Salafiyyah stands between extremism and passivity. It is not wrong to say that Salafiyyah is, in reality, the effort to implement the 'Wasatiyyah' (middle path) of Islam in its literal sense. A hallmark of Salafis is the extreme caution they exercise in labeling individuals as Kafir (disbeliever) or Mushrik (polytheist). At the same time, they are always at the forefront of teaching the distinction between Shirk and Kufr. We must not ignore the fact that blind critics sometimes attempt to put Salafis, who adopt a middle path in this field, in the dock without cause.

## Methods of Propagation

Salafis follow the method of knowledge, reformation, and propagation. This is also the style of the prophets. Knowledge is the weapon of propagation. There is no doubt that teaching conducted by those who have reformed themselves through the acquisition of knowledge will create a unique revolution. That is the message given to us by the pages of the history of prophets and other great figures.

Giving primary consideration to the propagation of Tawheed is what sets Salafiyyah apart. Anyone who studies the propagation methods of the prophets will be convinced of the authenticity of this path adopted by Salafis. Salafis have the ability to recognize that building structures without a proper foundation is foolishness. Da'wah (propagation) is not merely superficial service activities, but the communication of ideas from heart to heart.

Part of the propagation of Tawheed is the struggle against Shirk and its promoters. Salafis have declared an uncompromising struggle against centers of faith and ritual exploitation that lead society astray:
- Sorcerers
- Astrologers
- Palmists
- Shrine-industry operators
- Those who earn money in the name of fake relics

Salafis have been at the forefront of putting all such people on trial before society.

## Stance Against Terrorism

Salafis are those who take a strong stance against excesses like terrorism and extremism, leading the fight from the opposing side. A study of history makes it clear to everyone that Salafis have always led the way in identifying the roots of terrorism and nipping them in the bud. It is also comforting that the world is now recognizing the Jewish, Shia, and Sufi lobbies that struggle to link these same Salafis to terrorism.

Without diluting their ideology, Salafis do not close the doors of cooperation in the vast world of humanity. Salafis have always been at the forefront of human unity.

Whether it is the attributes of Allah, predestination, magic, the hereafter, or the signs of the Last Day, Salafis hold the same evidence-based beliefs and stances as the predecessors. The defect of modernists, who give more importance to their limited intellect than to evidence, has not affected Salafiyyah. True Salafis have not made any mistakes in these matters. The world needs to realize that contemporary events, such as rejecting even acceptable prophetic traditions, are the result of the worship of intellect.

Salafis have opposed blind imitation from the very beginning. However, Salafis have never opposed following scholars when they speak based on evidence. The Mujahids in Kerala follow the ideological policies of Salafiyyah, regardless of the subject. Those who maintain untouchability towards Salafiyyah and its ideological stances, and who attempt to bundle Salafiyyah into an 'ism', are not true Mujahids.

In short, Salafiyyah is synonymous with Islam. Salafis do not tell the world anything that Islam does not teach. Nor do they reject any of the teachings of Islam. Therefore, it is the single straight path to Paradise. May Allah bless us to join that path.
