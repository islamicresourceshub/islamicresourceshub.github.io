Allegations of Deviation and Response

History of Muslim Renaissance in Malayalam: 6

The meeting resumed at 4:30 PM. Based on the discussions held, the chairman explained the proceedings as follows:

'Those who claimed there was a deviation should present it, and after the other side responds, we can make a decision on each issue.

Description, Response, and Decisions Regarding Deviation:

The argument that it is not necessary to follow the methodology (manhaj) of the righteous predecessors (Salaf) in understanding and interpreting the Quran and Hadith, and that such a methodology does not even exist, is a deviation from the ideological principles we have upheld so far.

Response: We do not hold the view that the methodology of the righteous predecessors is not necessary for understanding and interpreting the Quran and Hadith, and for accepting religious matters. There is no other methodology different from this.

Decision: The meeting agreed that we must adopt the methodology of the righteous predecessors in understanding and interpreting the Quran and Hadith, and for accepting religious matters.

The interpretations of ‘Iqra’ bismi rabbikalladhee khalaq’ as 'gather all that is related to the names of your Lord who created and nurtured you,' and ‘La tuharrik bihee lisaanaka lita’jila bihee’ as referring to the precise accounting and judgment on the Day of Judgment, are contrary to the authentic texts of the Hadith. Such interpretations are also against the methodology of the righteous predecessors. This is a deviation from the methods and principles we have followed so far.

Response: K.K. Muhammad Sulami stated that the above interpretation is not contrary to authentic Hadith. The opposing side argued that it is contrary to authentic Hadith. K.K. clarified that he does not insist on these meanings.

Decision: The meeting decided that the above meaning, which is contrary to authentic Hadith, should be corrected. The meeting also decided that such isolated opinions should not be communicated to the public.

The theory that the three elements considered in Tawhid are Rububiyyah, Wala', and Hakimiyyah is a deviation from the fundamental principles we have upheld so far. The belief that Hakimiyyah and Wala' are elements to be considered in Tawhid is the belief of Jamaat-e-Islami and the Ikhwan. In reality, the division of Tawhid is into Rububiyyah, Uluhiyyah, and Asma' wa Sifat.

Response: I do not argue that Wala' and Hakimiyyah should be considered in Tawhid.

Decision: The meeting announced that the factual situation has been accepted based on the explanation previously provided by Abdul Hameed Madani of Chaliyamundam. The meeting affirmed the division of Tawhid into Rububiyyah, Uluhiyyah, and Asma' wa Sifat, and decided that it should be taught in schools.

The statement that Tawhid is not Iman, and that one does not attain Iman simply by being free from Shirk and Bid'ah, is a departure and deviation from our fundamental principles.

Response and Decision: The meeting approved the clarification that the statement in Shabāb, which implied that Tawhid is not Iman, was confusing. Shabāb published an article expressing disagreement on this matter, and the author of the previous statement clarified that he did not endorse it. The meeting approved this. The author stated that such remarks would be avoided in the future.

The notion that God is a despotic emperor, transcendent and seated on a throne above the seventh heaven, is a deviation from the beliefs we have held regarding the descriptions of Allah in the Holy Quran such as 'Man fis-samaa'i' (He who is in the heaven), 'Itawa 'alal-'arshi' (He ascended the Throne), 'Jabbar' (Tyrant), 'Kafir' (Disbeliever), 'Kaffar' (Disbeliever), 'Malikul-Mulk' (King of Kings).

Decision: The meeting decided that any errors found in books regarding this matter should be corrected.

To argue for the interpretation (ta'wil) of Allah's attributes like 'Man fis-samaa'i' is against the methodology of the righteous predecessors (Salafus-Salih) and a deviation from the beliefs and principles we have upheld.

Response: A note explaining the Salafi view on this matter has been published in Shabāb.

The statement that one who avoids Shirk and Bid'ah but deviates from the Prophet's example in matters of conduct, love, and devotion, will not be considered a believer in Tawhid, is the argument of the misguided Khawarij sect and a deviation from the principles we have upheld so far.

Response and Decision: The meeting approved the explanation provided by Maulavi Muhammad Kuttiyasseri in point number four.

While acknowledging that the Sunnah established from the Prophet (peace be upon him) is eleven rak'ahs for Taraweeh prayer, the argument that there is no objection to praying more than eleven rak'ahs based on weak Hadith or scholarly opinions, or to cause division in society, is false and a deviation from the principles we have upheld so far.

Response and Decision: Such an argument has not been made. The meeting approved the clarification by the author of the book related to this matter, who stated his willingness to correct the errors in his book according to our principles, and that no one should justify the remarks in the book. The author stated that such remarks would be avoided in the future.

While acknowledging that the Qunut in the Subh prayer is not established in the Prophet's Sunnah, adopting the stance that one should pray it in a mosque where Qunut is recited and refrain from it in a mosque where it is not recited, and acting and propagating accordingly, is a deviation from our principles.

Response: The accusers clarified that they do not hold such a view.

Decision: The meeting agreed that the Qunut in the Sunnah is not established in the practice of the Prophet ﷺ and is an innovation.

Based on the principle of determining the fast and Eid by observing the sight which does not contradict the definitive calculations of astronomy, we have had differences with others regarding Eid. The argument that in such situations, Eid should be observed with the majority of the people in the locality is a deviation from our principles.

Response: They clarified that such an argument has not been made. However, the person concerned explained that the matter mentioned in the accusation was not said against the organization, which the meeting approved.

Decision: The meeting decided that Mujahid workers should not express opposition to the decision of the Kerala Hilal Committee.

The theory that one should first find a common interest between the preacher and the preached-to, and then use the friendship formed through discussions on that subject as a bridge to convey the message of Islam to the heart of the preached-to. Since it is inappropriate to preach philosophy to a hungry stomach, one should first engage in social welfare activities. Or, find common interest topics like sports or share business and use them as a bridge. Then, use the friendship formed through them as a bridge to convey the message of Islam. This method of preaching is contrary to the practice followed by the Islahi movement so far and the example of the Prophet; it is also a deviation from our principles.

Decision: Based on the initial explanation by Abdul Hameed Madani of Chaliyamundam, the meeting decided that no further discussion is needed on this matter.

The argument that when evil is rampant in society, the fight against social evil should be undertaken before preaching Tawhid, is a deviation from the example of the Prophets and the principles we have upheld so far.

Decision: The meeting approved the explanation of Maulavi Kuttasseri on this matter. While not ignoring rampant evils, the meeting decided that Tawhid should be given the first priority in preaching.

What should be the primary focus of Da'wah? To instill Iman in human hearts! Strong Iman as described in the Quran and Sunnah, Iman firmly rooted in the heart and manifested in actions. If one tries to implement Allah's law on this earth, it does not mean Iman will be absent. Tawhid is not Iman. The argument that our Da'wah should primarily be for such an Iman, which is not Tawhid, is a refutation of the principles the Islahi movement has upheld so far and a deviation from them.

Decision: The meeting approved the explanation previously given by the author on this matter and decided to ensure that such remarks are not made in the future.

To argue and propagate that the Da'wah carried out by the Islahi movement so far needs to be free from refutation and counter-argument and should be reconsidered, is false and a deviation from the principles we have upheld so far.

Response: The concerned individuals explained that although such remarks appeared in the ISM policy document, they were not decided upon by the ISM meeting and were not circulated as a circular.

Decision: The meeting decided that the policy hitherto followed by our movement should continue.

To transform our organizational headquarters into a common center for all, working for common goals, a cultural center, and a time center; to suggest removing the names Mujahid and Salafi from mosques, madrasas, and other institutions and giving them common names; to state that seminars and study classes are for creating direction... all these are sufficient evidence that the deviation in principles was intentional.

Response: The concerned individuals explained that the idea of making organizational headquarters a center that unites everyone and works for common goals was an idea expressed during the circumstances of its founding period, but now it is only used for the activities of ISM.

Decision: The meeting decided that organizational headquarters should be run only in accordance with the principles and objectives of the Kerala Nadwathul Mujahideen. Similarly, mosques and other institutions can be given suitable names. However, it is not permissible to adopt the stance of not giving them the names Salafi and Mujahid.

Writings that describe scholars, leaders, the aged, and those whose minds have dried up as 'sleepyheads dragging the world backward,' creations devoid of any Islamic content, stories full of obscenity and vulgarity... publishing these in our periodicals is not only unsuitable for Da'wah but also greatly damages our propagation activities and is a deviation from the policies we have followed so far.

Decision: The meeting expressed strong dissatisfaction with such writings and instructed that they should not be repeated in the future.

The secret wing called 'Khandeer Kor' that emerged in our organization some time ago is contrary to the transparency and policies we have upheld so far, both ideologically and organizationally.

Decision: The meeting acknowledged this matter and decided to take steps to end all such secret organizations.

It is understood from the nature of the problems that the Ikhwan's 'Suriyyah' movement, formed to create ideological deviation among Salafis globally, also played a role in creating ideological deviation within the Islahi movement in Kerala.

Decision: The meeting evaluated this matter and decided that the issue should be studied.

Here, what was considered an ideological deviation was accepted as evidence, but the fact was denied. Some issues raised in the theses and differing opinions should not be considered deviations. They are mistakes, differences of research-based views, ignorance, or opinions taken out of context. However, the statements 'the Quran, the creation of Allah,' and 'one who intentionally abandons the fast is a disbeliever' are mistakes. If justified, they would have to be considered deviations.

The organizational matters raised in both theses do not fall within the purview of knowledge that the Jam'iyyatul Ulama should handle. Such matters should be handled by the KNM.

Decision: Statements in our publications such as 'the Quran, the creation of Allah,' and 'one who intentionally abandons the fast is a disbeliever' are incorrect and should be corrected.
