They Want to See God!

അബ്ദുല്ല ബാസിൽ സി.പി · 2017-02-04 · Aqeedah & Belief

Science and Faith in God

For me, science is the final word. I will not accept anything that has not been scientifically proven. That is precisely why I do not believe in God; I will only believe in God when science shows Him to me.

These are the words of a rationalist friend. This is just one of the typical cliché arguments used by rationalists, validating the saying that when knowledge of science is combined with humility, one becomes a believer, but when combined with arrogance and limited understanding, one becomes an atheist.

The True Limitations of Science

There is no point in asking these people questions like: What is the soul? How did life originate? What is right and wrong, and why? What is the purpose of life? These are questions for those who think using reason. The formula E=mc² can only teach us how much energy can be obtained by converting matter. It does not provide the wisdom to refrain from using that energy to kill or harm one's brothers. When this discovery was used to commit the horrific atrocities in Hiroshima and Nagasaki that shocked the world's conscience, Einstein had to call out to the world:

Be human. Remember humanity.

This is precisely why he said that. This humanity cannot be discovered through research in any laboratory. Therefore, those who say there is nothing beyond science are those who do not even know what science is.

What is Science? Can God be Seen?

Science is the study of matter. The field of discussion for science is how matter behaves. Its methods are observation and experimentation. Given this, how can science speak about a power that transcends matter? To put it more simply, science is the study of what is within the universe, or the study of creation. Therefore, the methods of science are insufficient to study or know God, the Creator, who is beyond the universe and beyond matter. No scripture claims that no matter how much science advances or how powerful the instruments invented are, God can be seen.

The Limitations of Science Within the Universe

For some, hearing this much is enough. They walk away saying, 'No further explanation is needed; you have already admitted that science will not discover God for us. I don't want to hear anything more.' It is true; talking to a rationalist about a power beyond the universe and beyond matter is like teaching trigonometry to a primary school student. Alright, let us lower ourselves to the level of the rationalist. Let us set aside whether science can speak about a power beyond the universe. Can science at least fully explain what is within the universe? Let us examine this with two small examples.

The Micro-Universe

Science has progressed far beyond the ignorance that there is nothing smaller than an atom. Research into the world of electrons, protons, and neutrons is leading us to even greater amazement and wonder. To those with humility, these complexities in the micro-universe are evidence that the hands of an All-Subtle Creator have been at work. The arrogant, however, credit this to 'coincidence' and make fools of themselves.

Let us return to the subject. The dual nature of the electron is one of the biggest problems in the scientific world today. When using the de Broglie equation λ=h/p to explain an electron, λ denotes the wave nature and p denotes the particle nature. The problem is further complicated by Heisenberg's Uncertainty Principle:

ΔxΔp ≥ h/4π

According to this, it is impossible to accurately calculate the position and momentum of an electron at the same time! This is said to be because the object we use to calculate the position of the electron significantly influences its momentum, and the object used to calculate momentum influences its mass.

In short, we cannot even fully understand a simple electron! When will those who shout that our research has no limits or boundaries wake up? Those who cannot even fully understand the intricacies of the material world are the ones insisting on seeing a power that is beyond matter. How pathetic!

The Macro-Universe

We still speak about the size of the universe like a frog in a well talking about the outside world. There are stars that our eyes have not even reached yet. Even research into whether there is a habitable place outside Earth is moving at a snail's pace. We can hope that these will be solved as science leaps forward. Science is leaping forward, and in that leap, we may discover telescopes more powerful than we can even imagine today. We may be able to learn about more stars and galaxies. But science itself says there are limits even there. This is because the entire consensus of scientists is that the universe is expanding. Just as the dots on an inflating balloon move away from each other, the galaxies within the expanding universe are moving away from each other.

We are in the Milky Way galaxy. Other galaxies are moving away from us. The speed of this recession is calculated by Hubble's Law: v=HoD. Here, v is the speed at which the galaxy is receding. Ho is the Hubble Constant. (Studies are still ongoing regarding what this constant is. We can take the figure of 20km/sec/1,000,000 Light Years for now). D is the distance from us to that galaxy. According to this, galaxies 15 billion light-years or more away are moving away from us faster than the speed of light! This means the light from them will never reach us! In other words, no matter how powerful a telescope we have, we will never gain any knowledge about those galaxies! No telescope, no matter how powerful, can capture light that does not come here.

Look. No matter how much we progress, we can only see objects within 15 billion light-years. Those who deny what they cannot see—will they deny the material world beyond that as well? Who, having set aside prejudice and arrogance and freed their thoughts, could still repeat such childish arguments?

The Undeniable Reality

I have described two examples just to point to the reality that we are not even capable of fully understanding and studying the macro-universe and the micro-universe. Are we the ones preparing to deny the power beyond the universe just because our weak instruments cannot detect Him? Those who cannot even fully study matter deny the One beyond matter because they cannot see Him! What a paradox! While there are proofs of the work of that Great Creator all around us, and while these complexities are enough to convince one of the Creator's power and majesty, for those who cling to the most irrational 'belief' that everything is a 'coincidence,' there is nothing left to do but pray that their hearts may open to think and learn.
