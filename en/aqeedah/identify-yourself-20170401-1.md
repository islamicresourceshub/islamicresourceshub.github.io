# Recognize Yourself

The Lord of the universe, who created humanity and ordained life and death, has fashioned human nature with a diverse array of characteristics. As a living being, it is impossible for a human to simply move forward by suppressing or ignoring the habits and traits that are inherently part of their existence. Instead, the Holy Quran teaches humanity that we are obligated to lead our lives by regulating these traits, ensuring they are utilized in a manner that is lawful and in accordance with the will of the Creator. The Holy Quran states:

“فَأَقِمْ وَجْهَكَ لِلدِّينِ حَنِيفًا ۚ فِطْرَتَ اللَّهِ الَّتِي فَطَرَ النَّاسَ عَلَيْهَا ۚ لَا تَبْدِيلَ لِخَلْقِ اللَّهِ ۚ ذَٰلِكَ الدِّينُ الْقَيِّمُ وَلَٰكِنَّ أَكْثَرَ النَّاسِ لَا يَعْلَمُونَ” (30:30).

This verse points out that harmony with one's innate nature is a natural quality of Islam and that it serves as an essential form of guidance for humanity. Denying this connection between Islam and human nature, or failing to accept Islam as a way of life, is a futile stance.

“مُنِيبِينَ إِلَيْهِ وَاتَّقُوهُ وَأَقِيمُوا الصَّلَاةَ وَلَا تَكُونُوا مِنَ الْمُشْرِكِينَ. مِنَ الَّذِينَ فَرَّقُوا دِينَهُمْ وَكَانُوا شِيَعًا ۖ كُلُّ حِزْبٍ بِمَا لَدَيْهِمْ فَرِحُونَ” (30:31,32).

The Holy Quran teaches that Islam is the only means for the unification of mankind. The biological characteristics and qualities inherent in human nature all serve as a testament to the necessity of worshipping the One God.

“وَإِذْ أَخَذَ رَبُّكَ مِن بَنِي آدَمَ مِن ظُهُورِهِمْ ذُرِّيَّتَهُمْ وَأَشْهَدَهُمْ عَلَىٰ أَنفُسِهِمْ أَلَسْتُ بِرَبِّكُمْ ۖ قَالُوا بَلَىٰ ۛ شَهِدْنَا ۛ أَن تَقُولُوا يَوْمَ الْقِيَامَةِ إِنَّا كُنَّا عَنْ هَٰذَا غَافِلِينَ” (7:172).

Any person with a sound mind will openly admit that they did not simply come into existence by chance; rather, they have been wondrously fashioned by an All-Knowing and All-Powerful Creator. This realization is paramount. Those who lack it seek refuge in the weak creations of the Almighty instead of seeking refuge in the Almighty Himself. They prostrate before those who are ignorant and weak. No matter how many reports one hears of people losing their health, honor, and wealth to the exploitation of those who masquerade in divine guise, such stories fail to influence these individuals. Among them are men and women, the educated and the illiterate, the wealthy and the poor. It means that if one lets go of the rope of Allah, anyone can fall into degradation. Allah says:

“حُنَفَاءَ لِلَّهِ غَيْرَ مُشْرِكِينَ بِهِ ۚ وَمَن يُشْرِكْ بِاللَّهِ فَكَأَنَّمَا خَرَّ مِنَ السَّمَاءِ فَتَخْطَفُهُ الطَّيْرُ أَوْ تَهْوِي بِهِ الرِّيحُ فِي مَكَانٍ سَحِيقٍ” (22:31).
