We live in an era where unity and cohesion are absolutely essential. It is vital for those who stand for peace to unite against terrorism, which threatens global peace itself. It is long past time for the global conscience to awaken and expose the carnage orchestrated by imperialist powers, who use individuals bearing Muslim names to form terrorist groups like ISIS to destroy Islam, the religion of peace. The media's anti-Islamic agenda is evident in their refusal to label the genocide of Rohingya Muslims by the Buddhist-majority government of Myanmar as «Buddhist terrorism», while simultaneously pinning the actions of ISIS on Islam.

## India Seeking Broad Unity

At a time when communal fascist forces are using the rod of power to dismantle India's lifeblood—unity in diversity—and attempting to imprison the country in a monolithic cage, the times demand a broad unity and cohesion among all peace-loving Indian citizens. We must never allow our country to be carved up along communal lines. All religious believers must come forward against the communalization of the country. Religious leaders must be prepared to isolate those who attempt to create division by using religion as a cover for political interests. Mainstream political parties must not succumb to internal fragmentation but instead formulate joint action plans to preserve the country's peaceful atmosphere.

## Kerala Making Community Unity Essential

To isolate those waiting to sow the seeds of communal division by destabilizing Kerala's peaceful landscape, which remains a safe haven in India, a unified movement is essential. The murder of Faisal in Kodinhi, Tirurangadi, and the actions taken by police officials against certain preachers highlight the depth of the threat rising against Islam, its propagation, and its preachers. The controversy over the Uniform Civil Code is merely a continuation of a move aimed at making minorities fight each other while consolidating the majority. In this context, Muslim organizations must choose the path of wisdom.

## End the Accusations of Extremism

If the accusations of extremism leveled by some speakers within the Samastha against the Salafis are not reined in, a heavy price will have to be paid in the future. Anyone can question an idea that contradicts established proofs, and those concerned are obligated to provide explanations. Beyond that, it is unlikely that even the common people of Samastha believe the narrative that ties terrorism to the Mujahid activists of Kerala, based on findings scavenged from rumors circulating in the world. No Mujahid has ever attempted to put Samastha in the dock by hiding behind the accusation of terrorism leveled against Thadiyantevida Nazeer.

The leadership of Jamaat-e-Islami should no longer delay in disowning the extremist and non-scriptural ideas found in the books of authors like Mawdudi, ending their justifications, and withdrawing those books. As long as Mawdudi's books are being sold, it is not fair to interpret criticism of the extremist ideas contained within them as an accusation of terrorism.

We must not view the hunt against Islamic preachers merely as a move against Muslims. It is an encroachment upon the fundamental rights guaranteed by our Constitution—an open war against the Constitution itself. It is an insurrection against the country's pluralism. This will not affect Muslims alone; it is a sword that slaughters every individual who holds an independent opinion. It has already begun to move toward artists and public activists. This intolerance will spread at the speed of light toward the total destruction of society.

This is a stage where minorities must move with unity to bring this issue to the attention of the secular society. Community organizations must provide leadership for this. It must be possible to unite against a common threat while maintaining ideological differences. We have the history of the 1930s before us, where Muslims worked in unity to protect the Sharia even while all religious groups stood together to fight for the country. The times demand a repetition of history. Those with wisdom are not moths to be burnt in the fire lit by a common enemy while fighting among themselves. To overcome the challenges faced by the community and the country, we must move forward in unity.

## For Mujahid Unity to Become Reality

No matter how much we strive, it is Allah who must grant success. Allah helps only those who uphold His boundaries. If we abandon our faith and religious rigor in the name of the country's challenges, Allah will abandon us as well. The unity among the Mujahids of Kerala must be evaluated by focusing on this core principle.

There is no one who is not saddened by the split in 2002 of the **Kerala Nadvathul Mujahideen**, the movement that ignited the renaissance in Kerala. There is no one who does not wish for this to be united. Therefore, the news that the Mujahids have united brings joy. However, this unity must not be a temporary phenomenon; it must become a reality. That is what those who love the ideology and the movement desire. It is understood by all that the separation for the last fifteen years was due to ideological reasons. It is noteworthy that it was specifically mentioned in the timeline of unity that it took eleven months to discuss ideological matters. However, it is regrettable to have to say that the official explanations issued after the unity have disappointed those with ideological awareness and betrayed those who embraced the unity.

## Unity and the Press Conference

News of the unity first emerged at a press conference called in Kozhikode on December 5, 2016. After informing the press of the unity resolution, T.P. Abdullakoya Madani announced that there would be an opportunity to ask questions.

## The Dispute Regarding Sihr

Since this was a subject that dominated past controversies, the press asked about it first. The Madavoor faction argued that believing *Sihr* (magic) is effective is *Shirk* (polytheism), while the T.P. faction opposed this. For so long, the explanation given was that *Sihr* is effective, but practicing it is a major sin. Since ideological matters had been decided, the reporter asked what the organization's official stance on the subject of *Sihr* would be. In a situation where it was announced that they had united on the Quran and Sunnah regarding disputed matters, this question could have been answered easily. The scriptural answer is that *Sihr* has a reality, but it is forbidden to practice it. This was clearly written in the Holy Quran translation by Amani Maulavi before 2002. Furthermore, the decisions of the Kerala Jamiyyathul Ulama have cited proofs that demonic possession is a truth. Moreover, this is a subject that has been taught to children in madrasa textbooks.

Instead of clarifying this, T.P. replied that the Jamiyyathul Ulama would meet and decide on the *Sihr* issue again. It is very clear that while ideological matters may have been discussed, they were not decided. When asked again, he evaded the question by saying it would be announced at the unity conference held at Kozhikode beach on December 20.

## Vichinthanam, Explanation, and Asgarali's Resignation

At the Kozhikode unity conference, ideological stances on issues including *Sihr* were not openly stated. It was later learned that the decision was not to discuss such matters in public. However, violating the unity agreement, they were forced to provide an explanation in the February 3 issue of *Vichinthanam*, using the names of Janab A. Asgarali and M. Abdurahman Salafi, the masterminds of the Madavoor faction. The explanation has only served to worsen the crisis. Another note and resignation letter written by the current organization secretary, Asgarali Sahib, against the explanation in *Vichinthanam* is proof that the crisis has deepened. Through this, the truth that the unity was achieved through adjustment rather than ideological alignment has been authoritatively revealed.

According to the new explanation, two ideologies still exist between the two factions. *Vichinthanam* writes that believing *Sihr* is effective is not *Shirk* and that there has been no dispute among the predecessors or successors on that matter. However, with Asgarali Sahib coming out with a rebuttal the very next day, it is clear that two opinions still exist on this subject. This means that even after discussing for eleven months, there is no agreement on a core issue. As Ali Madani said in their council, it is clear that the Madavoor faction has united without changing its ideology. Now, what should the followers believe? Is what was written in *Vichinthanam* the official stance? If that is the official stance, is it enough to just accept Asgarali Sahib's resignation? Should they not openly state that what he wrote against *Vichinthanam* is wrong? Can the office-bearers who publicly attacked *Vichinthanam* on online media be allowed to continue in their positions? Or will they apologize?

Or, if what was written in *Vichinthanam* is wrong and Asgarali's note is correct, it will have to be admitted that KNM has changed its stance and moved toward the Madavoor ideology. Moreover, action will have to be taken against Asgarali and Salafi for hijacking the KJU to publish this article.

If they are not prepared to set aside prejudices and rationalism and return to the scriptures on fundamental issues, the unity will not only fail, but they will also have to face massive trials.
