## The Prophets attained Paradise by fighting through severe trials without wavering

They did not become role models for the world by living in luxury, sleeping on rooftops, or wearing the robes of authority while immersed in worldly distractions. Even when they were bombarded with trials like arrows in the field of propagating the truth, those righteous souls overcame them all with patience and prayer rooted in faith. History is a witness that no matter who the forces trying to destroy the light of the true religion were, they never achieved ultimate victory. Indeed, that very reality inspires us believers to respond with composure to the crises that erupt like volcanic explosions on the path of propagation and to move forward toward our goal.

## This worldly life is a testing ground

Believers should view this worldly life entirely as a testing ground. See what the Lord of the Universe says:

> "And We will surely test you with something of fear and hunger and a loss of wealth and lives and fruits, but give good tidings to the patient" (2:155).

This verse teaches us the reality that the Lord's tests can occur in various spheres of life. Even if they appear difficult to us on the surface, we must accept the truth that they contain both known and unknown blessings.

## Believers are not meant to waver in trials

Believers are not meant to waver in trials. Turning toward areas displeasing to the Lord the moment a difficulty arises is a great act of ingratitude. No matter what crisis we face, the Lord is the only one from whom we should seek protection and who provides security. It is in Him that we must place our trust. It must be said that anyone who thinks it is acceptable to say, propagate, or do things displeasing to the Lord because it is necessary for material gain is living in a fool's paradise. The devil is destroying their faith. Such people forget the fundamental fact that their provision was decreed by Allah beforehand and that no one can stop it, no matter what they think. This is a clear example of how the devil misleads people during times of trial. May Allah grant guidance to everyone.

## The perspective of true believers

However, no matter how great a crisis they face, true believers have nothing but good thoughts about Allah. If one remains steadfast on the path of truth:

- One might lose their current job.
- One may have to hear many insults and stinging remarks from people.
- Family members might distance themselves.
- There might be a boycott by local mosque authorities.
- Positions and status might be lost.
- Friendships might be severed.

And so many difficulties...! But no matter how many hardships they have to endure, believers are not saddened by them. This is because the Prophet (peace be upon him) taught them that Allah subjects those He loves to many trials. They can only happily praise the Lord who gave them the opportunity to hold the teachings of that leader close to their hearts.

## The life of the Prophet (peace be upon him) is a model

**The life of the Prophet (peace be upon him)** confirms this matter.

- The Prophet never had the opportunity to experience a father's affection in his life.
- His mother also passed away before he could get enough of her love.
- The Prophet had to hear the mockery of the people of his land.
- They called him a madman and a troublemaker to his face.
- In **Ta'if**, his own relatives pelted him with stones and drove him away.
- There were times in the Prophet's life when he had to leave his house due to hunger.
- He endured financial hardships.
- Allah called back his beloved wife **Khadijah** while the Prophet was still alive.
- All his sons passed away to the hereafter before his very eyes.
- There were situations in the life of the Holy Prophet where he had to stand helplessly and shed tears while praying before the mutilated bodies of followers who were ready to give their lives for him.
- Blood flowed from his blessed body on the battlefield of **Uhud**.
- Illnesses afflicted the Prophet.

Thus, we can see in the life of the Prophet (peace be upon him) a tidal wave of trials that lasted until his death.

## Not retreating in the field of propagating the truth

Because of all this, the Prophet (peace be upon him) did not retreat even an inch from the field of propagating the truth. He faced accusations with courage. Because of that, Allah's help flowed abundantly. When history repeats itself, we have nothing else to do. We must realize that no matter how great the crises before us are, they do not even come close to what the Prophet and his followers experienced. If we move forward along the path of the **Salaf**, it is certain that the Lord's help will be with us.

## The magnitude of the reward depends on the trials

The Prophet (peace be upon him) also taught that the magnitude of the reward we receive is based on the trials. Narrated by **Anas (may Allah be pleased with him)**, the Prophet said:

> "The magnitude of the reward is proportional to the magnitude of the trial. Indeed, if Allah loves a people, He tests them. So whoever is satisfied, for him is (Allah's) satisfaction. And whoever is dissatisfied, for him is Allah's anger" (**Tirmidhi**).

## Purifying true believers completely

Purifying true believers completely is also a purpose of trials. Believers had to experience severe hardships on the battlefield of Uhud. See what Allah says about the wisdom behind it:

> "So do not weaken and do not grieve, and you will be superior if you are [true] believers. If a wound should touch you - there has already touched the [opposing] people a wound similar to it. And these days [of varying conditions] We alternate among the people so that Allah may make evident those who believe and [may] take to Himself from among you martyrs - and Allah does not like the wrongdoers. And that Allah may purify the believers [through trials] and destroy the disbelievers. Or do you think that you will enter Paradise while Allah has not yet made evident those of you who fight in His cause and made evident those who are steadfast?" (**Al-Imran 139-142**).

These verses clarify the purpose of the trials that occurred at Uhud.

## Understanding Allah's power

Moreover, through trials, believers understand Allah's power precisely. This enables them to submit to the Lord without arrogance. See what Allah says:

> "No disaster strikes upon the earth or among yourselves except that it is in a register before We bring it into being - indeed that, for Allah, is easy. [We inform you of this] that you do not despair over what has eluded you and not exult [in pride] over what He has given you. And Allah does not like everyone self-deluded and boastful" (Al-Hadid 22, 23).

Let us strive to move forward without stumbling, remaining steadfast on the path of truth with the realization that ultimately, there is good for believers in any kind of trial.
