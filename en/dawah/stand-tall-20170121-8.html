Stand Tall

പത്രാധിപർ · 2017-01-21 · Dawah & Spirituality

True Faith: A Source of Strength

True faith (Iman) is a source of strength. A true believer remains noble and strong under any circumstance. Iman empowers a person to remain steadfast, overcome challenges, and actively engage in righteous deeds without faltering, even in the face of adversity.

To Enjoin Good and Forbid Evil

It is Iman that instills the courage to enjoin good and forbid evil, without compromising with the corrupt values and evil customs prevalent in society. It represents goodness and sincere well-wishing toward oneself and others. The journey along this path will not be easy. What lies ahead on this path is not a carpet strewn with flowers, but a rugged terrain covered with stones and thorns. Sharp arrows of opposition and ridicule may come flying. How can the friends of darkness love the light? The Quran teaches us what we should say to them:

"Say, 'O my people, work according to your position; indeed, I am working. And you are going to know to whom will come a humiliating punishment and upon whom will descend an enduring punishment.'" (39:39-40)

Rejecting Polytheistic Beliefs

Only a person who has gained self-awareness through Iman and surrendered everything to Allah can make such a declaration. Before him lies the path shown by the Creator. Therefore, he will not simply follow the crowd or conform to whoever he meets. There is a warning from the Prophet on this matter:

"Do not be conformists (blind followers) who say, 'If people do good, we will do good, and if they do wrong, we will do wrong.' Rather, make up your minds: if people do good, do good, and if they do evil, do not commit evil." (Tirmidhi)

It takes spiritual strength to reject the polytheistic beliefs, superstitions, and un-Islamic innovations (Bid'ah) prevalent in Muslim society, and to educate the community. Only those with pure, unadulterated Iman will possess this strength. Others will remain weak. Those who lack this capacity will always and everywhere remain slaves to local customs and habitual practices. Whether right or wrong, they will prefer the traditional beliefs and practices existing in society. If everyone around him worships shrines, he too will become a shrine-worshipper; if they abandon the Sunnah and adopt innovations, he too will follow that path!

Remaining Committed

If one distances oneself from and opposes unscriptural customs and erroneous beliefs, people will criticize, ridicule, and sometimes even harm them. In such situations, a true believer will never seek to please people at the cost of inviting the wrath of Allah.

Allah will never abandon one who embraces true faith and lives putting their trust in Him; His help will continuously reach them. A believer must remain firmly committed to what they have understood to be right according to the scriptures. The humiliation, ridicule, and hardships faced in that path should not deter them. Because the believer's sole desire is to attain the pleasure and reward of Allah. This is the stance that Allah commanded the Prophet to adopt.

Standing Tall with Courage

Empowered by self-awareness, a believer must stand tall and courageous before anyone. No one can sway them from the path of truth. With Allah's protection, they need not fear anyone else.

"So do not weaken and do not grieve, and you will be superior if you are [true] believers." (Holy Quran 3:139)
