# The Religion That Detests Extremism: 2

![](/assets/muslims-in-a-pluralistic-society-20170128-1/28Jan17-09.jpg)

## We Must Treat Non-Muslims with Kindness

> "Allah does not forbid you from those who do not fight you because of religion and do not expel you from your homes - from being righteous toward them and acting justly toward them. Indeed, Allah loves those who act justly" (**Quran** 60:8).

## Only If Enemies Initiate War

Even in such cases, Islam sets forth conditions that must be followed. From the leadership level downwards, there are strict instructions such as:

- Do not kill innocent people
- Do not destroy crops or vegetation
- Do not harm women, children, and the like

There is a clear example for every Muslim in the wars led by the Prophet during his time. He demonstrated the commands of the Holy Quran through his own life.

> "Fight in the way of Allah those who fight you but do not transgress. Indeed. Allah does not like transgressors" (Quran 2:190).

## Those Who Seek Refuge on the Battlefield

> "And if any one of the polytheists seeks your protection, then grant him protection so that he may hear the words of Allah. Then deliver him to his place of safety. That is because they are a people who do not know" (Quran 9:6).

## The Treatment of Non-Muslim Citizens Taught by Prophet Muhammad (pbuh)

> "Beware! If anyone oppresses a non-Muslim citizen, burdens them with taxes beyond their capacity, treats them cruelly, or deprives them of their rights, I will be their advocate against them on the Day of Judgment" (**Abu Dawud**).

We have already understood that Islam permits fighting against enemies only in the inevitable situation where they initiate war. In all other circumstances, Islam provides the motivation to constantly strive to spread goodness in society.

> "Indeed, Allah orders justice and good conduct and giving to relatives and forbids immorality and bad conduct and oppression. He admonishes you that perhaps you will be reminded" (Quran 16:90).

## Islam Detests the Spread of Evil in Society

If any individual attempts to commit an evil act, it is the duty of the believer to try to stop it. The Prophet (pbuh) said:

> "Help your brother, whether he is an oppressor or oppressed." A companion asked, "O Prophet, I understand helping him when he is oppressed, but how do I help him if he is an oppressor?" The Prophet (pbuh) replied, "You must restrain him from oppression. That is how you help him" (**Bukhari**, **Muslim**).

## In an Era Filled with Violence and Murder, Human Life Has No Value

Islam places immense value on the life of every human being among Allah's creations.

> "Whoever kills a soul unless for a soul or for corruption [done] in the land - it is as if he had slain mankind entirely. And whoever saves one - it is as if he had saved mankind entirely" (Quran 5:32).

## Islam Does Not Permit Moves to Kill Opponents or Suicide

> "And do not kill the soul which Allah has forbidden, except by right" (Quran 17:33).
> 
> "...And do not kill yourselves [or one another]. Indeed, Allah is to you ever Merciful" (Quran 4:29).

This is the guidance from Allah. Prophet Muhammad (pbuh) said:

> "Whoever commits suicide with a piece of iron will be in the Hellfire, holding that iron and stabbing himself in his stomach forever and ever" (Bukhari).

Those within Islam can never validate or justify the terrorist activities and suicide attacks that we hear about every day. Prophet Muhammad (pbuh) said:

> "Whoever is killed under a banner of ambiguity, supporting tribalism or calling towards tribalism, then his death is a death of ignorance" (Muslim).

## Islam Provides Justice and Good Counsel

Its influence in nurturing righteous individuals is significant.

> "O you who have believed, be persistently standing firm for Allah, witnesses in justice, and do not let the hatred of a people prevent you from being just. Be just; that is nearer to righteousness. And fear Allah; indeed, Allah is acquainted with what you do" (Quran 5:8).

## Islam Is in Harmony with Human Nature

It is impossible to find any instructions in Islamic teachings that are contrary to nature. The command of Allah, "Do not exceed limits in your religion" (Quran 4:171), points to this. Prophet Muhammad (pbuh) taught:

> "Beware of exceeding the limits in religion, for those before you were destroyed because they exceeded the limits in their religion" (**Ibn Majah**, **Nasa'i**, **Ahmad**)
