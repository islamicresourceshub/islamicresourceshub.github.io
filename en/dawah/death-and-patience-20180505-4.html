Death and Patience

വെള്ളില പി. അബ്ദുല്ല · 2018-05-05 · Dawah & Spirituality

('Afkarun Ukhrawiyya' - Thoughts on the Hereafter: 7)

The Trial That Befalls You

My friend, the trial that befalls you, Remember that the Lord has spoken of it. It is in the Quran, look and see, In the thirty-first verse of Surah Muhammad.

Trials are many: fear and hunger, Loss of crops, wealth, and the toll of death. Even if these come, you must be patient, Remember, you will find this in Surah al-Baqarah.

Great is the reward for patience, Yet it is a difficult path to tread.

The Wonder of the Believer

The affairs of the believer are all wonderful, No one else possesses this quality. If joy comes, he praises the Lord, And receives the reward for that. If sorrow comes, he remains patient, And for that, he receives a reward as well.

These are the words of the Prophet, peace be upon him, Found in the collections of Darimi and Ahmad.

The Meaning of Patience

You must understand the true meaning of patience, Otherwise, you will not attain its fruits. When a calamity befalls you, Forget the sorrow of the loss, Accept it as the decree of the Lord and endure, Surrendering to Him from the very beginning. This is the definition of patience, As found in many scholarly books. Abu Mansur al-Maturidi has also mentioned this In his commentary, the Tafsir.

If a Calamity Reaches You

If a calamity reaches you, And you cannot prevent it, my dear, After you have sought every remedy, And found no way to escape, Some may call you a martyr or a saint, Saying you possess great patience. But that is false, my dear; if you are helpless, Enduring it is not the same as being truly patient.

Do You Not Know of Prophet Ayyub?

Do you not know of Prophet Ayyub, my friend? Do not forget the trials that befell him. Do you not remember what his wife said to him, Asking him to pray to the Lord for relief? He had lived in blessing for seventy years, How could he not endure for a time? Have you not heard this said of them? Can you not find this in the Tafsir of al-Qurtubi?

Some People, Upon Seeing a Deceased Person

There are some who, upon seeing a corpse, Begin to wail and cry out loudly. Do not do this; it is not permitted, It brings punishment upon the deceased. Rafi ibn Khadij passed away, And some women stood there weeping. They were told to stop repeatedly.

If the family of the deceased wails, The deceased is punished in the grave. 'Woe to you if you continue to wail,' They were rebuked with these words.

This rebuke came from Ibn Umar, And it is found in the Musannaf of Abdur Razzaq.

Shedding Tears and Feeling Sorrow

Shedding tears and feeling sorrow Are signs of the mercy Allah has placed within you. On the day the Prophet's son passed away, Remember the words the Prophet spoke:

Shedding tears and sorrow are from mercy, Wailing and striking the chest are from the devil.

This statement is found in the Musnad, Do not forget the Musnad of Abu Shaybah.

Therefore, You Must Avoid Wailing

Therefore, you must avoid wailing, When the time of trial comes. Pray for patience in that moment, Make me one who is grateful in times of blessing, And grant me the strength to be patient in times of sorrow. Grant me the success to achieve this, O Most Merciful, For without it, there is only loss, O Judge of all.
