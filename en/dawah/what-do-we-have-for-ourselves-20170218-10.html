What Do We Have For Ourselves?

"People's reckoning has drawn near to them, while they are heedless and turn away."

(Quran 21:1)

Many hearts are heedless in remembering Allah and in fulfilling His commands as they should. Most people are engrossed in trivial matters, enslaved by their desires, and only invite loss in this life and the hereafter. This world, adorned with ambitions, selfishness, and the lust for power, is capable of deceiving even those believers who possess some measure of God-consciousness and piety! Whoever can escape this great calamity that distracts from the remembrance of Allah has succeeded.

A Time for Self-Reflection

It is high time for each of us to conduct a self-examination. The prophetic saying reminds us that we will be questioned about how we spent our lifespan, health, and time. For those who understand that there is an eternal life after death and have set their sights on Paradise, the question arises: are we utilizing our health, vitality, and time in ways that will help us achieve that goal?

Even as we embrace the unequivocal principle that prayer should be directed solely to Allah, do our hands reach out to Him when we face life's crises? When confronted with severe trials, do we place our complete trust in Him? Do we rise in the middle of the night, shedding tears as we reflect on the sins we have committed day and night? Do we feel remorse when we are compelled to hear or see inappropriate things? Do we refrain from gossip when we start to engage in it, remembering Allah? When the call to prayer, "Come to prayer, come to success," rings out, do we set aside whatever we are doing and head to the mosque? Do we obey our parents as we should? Have we completely liberated ourselves from associating partners with Allah (shirk) and innovations in religion (bid'ah)? Have we purified our hearts and bodies from usury (riba), lotteries, falsehood, deceit, envy, malice, hatred, and anger? If the answer to all these is yes, then we have succeeded.

Signs of True Believers

True believers are those who contemplate Allah's signs in the visible universe, humble themselves before His immense power, repent for their shortcomings, and constantly pray for protection from the punishment of Hellfire.

"Indeed, in the creation of the heavens and the earth and the alternation of the night and the day are signs for those of understanding. Those who remember Allah while standing and sitting and lying on their sides and give thought to the creation of the heavens and the earth, [saying], 'Our Lord, You did not create this aimlessly; exalted are You! Then protect us from the punishment of the Fire.'"

(Quran 3:190-191)

The reality is that for many who claim to believe in Allah, the Holy Quran, and the prophets, this belief often remains only on their tongues. How many are there who, enslaved by their desires, neglect acts of worship and waste precious time in various forms of entertainment!

Chasing Worldly Desires

Someone might say, "My goal is to build a good house." Once the house is completed, their next goal will be to fill it with expensive furniture. After that, the next objective will be to buy a luxury car. In this way, days, months, and years pass by with desires and expectations that never end, and people hardly notice!

Even if one acquires many blessings in this worldly life, such as a good house and a vehicle, what does one truly have that can be called 'one's own' in this world? A house, property, a spouse... how long can these be claimed as one's own?

How many wives betray their husbands and elope with other men! We hear daily news of husbands being murdered by their wives, and wives by their husbands. Parents are killed by their children. Did they ever imagine, even in their wildest dreams, that their end would come at the hands of the very child they doted on and raised? Does not all connection and ownership cease with death? Do not the heirs become separate entities?

Someone spends all the savings earned through back-breaking labor in the Gulf on their dream house, only to die when the concrete structure collapses or due to some other mishap just as the construction is completed. Another meets their end in an accident while taking a new car for registration.

Eternal Savings

So, what do we have here that we can call our own? What do we have here that is not temporary? There is only one answer: our faith and our good deeds! Only these will truly remain ours eternally. Through them, we can attain Paradise. Once a person enters Paradise, all those who are there will be their relatives and companions, never to be separated. Attaining Paradise is not easy; it requires, as mentioned earlier, true faith and good deeds. It also requires the ability to live without faltering through Allah's tests.

"Do the people think that they will be left [to say], 'We believe' and they will not be tested? But We have certainly tested those before them, and Allah will surely make evident those who are truthful, and He will surely make evident the liars."

(Quran 29:2-3)

Is our faith sincere? Is there earnestness in it? Are we performing good deeds to show others and gain respect in society? Are we showing gratitude for the countless blessings Allah has bestowed upon us? Are we willing to sacrifice in the way of Allah? Yes, all of these are tests!

However, Allah only gives us tests that we can bear. All that is needed is firm faith and patience. The heart of a believer who stands firm through any trial, places their complete trust in Allah, believing that everything is from their Lord, becomes calm. It is purified. And for those true believers who die with such a purified heart and a tranquil soul, Paradise, the abode of joy, awaits them.

For those who sincerely desire to attain it, nothing in this worldly life will be an obstacle. A day is coming when none of what one has earned will be of any benefit, and no relatives or loved ones will be of any help; everyone will look out for themselves.

"That Day, no wealth will be of benefit, nor will children, Except for one who comes to Allah with a sound heart."

(Quran 26:88-89)

On that Day, only one's good deeds will be of assistance. Therefore, make preparations for that day:

"O you who have believed, fear Allah. And let every soul look to what it has put forward for tomorrow; and fear Allah. Indeed, Allah is Acquainted with what you do."

(Quran 59:18)
