Living with an Awareness of the Value of Time

നാസിർ ബാലുശ്ശേരി · 2017-01-14 · Dawah & Spirituality

What can be achieved in a single moment?

What can be achieved in a single moment? If someone says that nothing can be done, there is no one more foolish in this world than them. This is because modern humans, standing at the pinnacle of information technology, can effortlessly perform many tasks with infinite possibilities in just a second. By skillfully utilizing an Android phone in their pocket or a computer on their desk, they can scatter pearls of goodness across the world. Millions of people can receive that message and plant countless beautiful seeds that bloom into rays of goodness.

Likewise

Likewise, through a single poisonous word or the misuse of modern technology, a human today can cause the destruction of thousands of lives and sow the seeds of epidemics that last for generations. This means that every moment in life and every action performed is precious. Therefore, we must be conscious of our time and our deeds.

Without our permission, knowledge, or request

We must enrich every moment through good words and righteous deeds, remembering the Creator who gifted us this magnificent life without our permission, knowledge, or request. We must stay completely away from the realms of violence, injustice, and immoral activities. When a day comes to an end, we should conduct a proper self-evaluation. As we pass through each stage of life, we should be able to recall the good deeds done in previous stages and dream of Paradise with spiritual contentment. Such a person is a true believer.

When the sky is cleft asunder

When the sky is cleft asunder, when the stars fall, when the mountains are crushed to dust... the fortunate true believer is the one who meets their Rabb without fear.

"Indeed, those who have believed and done righteous deeds - they will have the Gardens of Paradise as a lodging, wherein they will abide eternally. They will not desire from it any transfer. Say, 'If the sea were ink for the words of my Lord, the sea would be exhausted before the words of my Lord were exhausted, even if We brought the like of it as a supplement.' Say, 'I am only a man like you, to whom has been revealed that your god is one God. So whoever would hope for the meeting with his Lord - let him do righteous work and not associate in the worship of his Lord anyone.'" (Quran 18:107-110).
