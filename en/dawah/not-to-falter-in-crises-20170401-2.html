Staying Steadfast During Crises

Shameer Madani

"Whoever submits their face to Allah while being a doer of good has grasped the most trustworthy handhold. And to Allah will be the outcome of all matters." (Quran 31:22)

There is no one who does not experience hardships in life. When things happen contrary to their desires and they are forced to drink from the cup of bitterness, many people fail. Despair and passivity take hold of their lives. Some seek refuge in drugs and alcohol, while others end their lives in suicide. In truth, none of these are the right solutions to problems; rather, they only make the issues more complex.

However, if we examine the lifestyles of great devotees and righteous figures, we see that they too faced many trials and crises. Yet, in such situations, they did not become cowards who surrendered to defeat and retreated. The Prophets and their followers, who faced crises on the path of propagating the truth, did not stop their mission in the face of adversity.

Prophet Ibrahim (AS) and Prophet Zakariyya (AS), who prayed to Allah without despairing even after many years of married life without children; Prophet Ayyub (AS), who suffered through a long period of illness; and Prophet Yaqub (AS), who stood firm without faltering while complaining only to his Lord with tear-filled eyes when he 'lost' his two beloved sons whom he had raised with such love and affection—all of them passed through the furnace of trials.

There are some important matters that should serve as inspiration for us during such stages, helping us to stand firm without faltering during trials and crises.

Firstly, it is to grasp the purpose of this life. For those who believe that this worldly life is the only life and that the temporary victories and defeats here are the true ones, it is natural for losses here to cause severe trauma. They may rejoice excessively in the temporary gains of this world. But those who believe in Allah and the life of the Hereafter are not like that. They are the ones who strive to turn the hardships of this life into provisions for the eternal life. Therefore, regarding who it is that falls into despair, we see this description in the Quran: "And despair not of the mercy of Allah. Indeed, no one despairs of the mercy of Allah except the disbelieving people." (12:87).

Likewise, if we can remember those who are suffering more than us during stages of crisis and trial, the intensity of our own troubles and pain can be reduced. The Prophet (PBUH) says: "Look at those who are below you and do not look at those who are above you, for that is more likely to hold you back from belittling the blessings of Allah upon you." (Muslim).

Convince your mind of the reality that things happen according to Allah's decree, beyond our own decisions. That is also a major aspect of the belief in divine destiny taught by Islam. If one can realize that Allah's decree will ultimately be beneficial for us, a believer will find no difficulty in finding solace in any experience that is part of divine destiny.

Above all, as is clearly seen in the lives of the Prophets, constantly seek deliverance and help from the All-Knowing Allah. Those prayers are soothing, rewarding, and provide the courage needed to ignore crises and move forward. Finding the Almighty and Most Merciful Allah as one's refuge and support brings a joy and peace of mind to a believer that is beyond description. "...Whoever disbelieves in Taghut and believes in Allah has grasped the most trustworthy handhold with no break in it. And Allah is Hearing and Knowing." (2:256).
