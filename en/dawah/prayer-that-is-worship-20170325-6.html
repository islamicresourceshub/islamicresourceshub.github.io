Prayer: That is Worship

മൂസ സ്വലാഹി, കാര · 2017-03-25 · Dawah & Spirituality

Upholding Acts of Worship

The primary purpose of human life is to uphold acts of worship in the manner and method taught by Islam. Allah says:

''وَمَا خَلَقْتُ الْجِنَّ وَالْإِنسَ إِلَّا لِيَعْبُدُونِ'' (51:56).

Allah has given this command to all of humanity without distinction or division:

''يَا أَيُّهَا النَّاسُ اعْبُدُوا رَبَّكُمُ الَّذِي خَلَقَكُمْ وَالَّذِينَ مِن قَبْلِكُمْ لَعَلَّكُمْ تَتَّقُونَ'' (2:21).

What is 'Ibadah'?

We can understand the fundamental definition of 'Ibadah' from what Shaykh al-Islam Ibn Taymiyyah (rah) stated:

''Ibadah is a comprehensive term for all words and actions, both outward and inward, that Allah loves and is pleased with'' (Al-Ubudiyyah fil-Islam, p. 4).

Shaykh Nasir as-Sa'di (rah) emphasized this in his explanation of Surah al-Fatihah. Prayer, which is the supplication of the heart filled with ultimate humility and submissiveness, is central to worship. Allah says:

''ذَٰلِكَ بِأَنَّ اللَّهَ هُوَ الْحَقُّ وَأَنَّ مَا يَدْعُونَ مِن دُونِهِ هُوَ الْبَاطِلُ وَأَنَّ اللَّهَ هُوَ الْعَلِيُّ الْكَبِيرُ'' (31:30).

Islam commands us to perform worship sincerely, submitting only to Allah. Allah says:

''قُلْ إِنِّي أُمِرْتُ أَنْ أَعْبُدَ اللَّهَ مُخْلِصًا لَّهُ الدِّينَ. وَأُمِرْتُ لِأَنْ أَكُونَ أَوَّلَ الْمُسْلِمِينَ'' (39:11,12).

The Claim that Prayer and Worship are Different

A Muslim living by this command cannot tolerate even an atom's weight of Shirk. However, there are those within the Muslim community who believe and propagate that prayer and worship are not the same, and that it is permissible to pray to others besides Allah, claiming there are different types of 'calling.' Observe what some scholars of the Samastha faction—who follow the hollow beliefs and rituals fabricated by the Shia, the arch-enemies of Islam—have written:

''Tawassul through the essence (dhat), status (jah), right (haqq), and blessings (barakah) of deceased Prophets, Awliya, and the righteous; calling upon them directly; seeking their help; taking blessings from their relics; giving charity for the benefit of the deceased; making vows (nercha) of chickens or goats; reciting the Quran for them; visiting graves (ziyarat); greeting the dead and praying for them; traveling for ziyarat; using amulets (urukku) and blessed water/threads based on Quranic verses, Hadith, and sacred names; reciting Burda; following established Tariqas like Qadiriyya, Shadhiliyya, and Rifaiyya; performing Ratheeb and Dhikr; reciting Dala'il al-Khayrat, Hizb al-Nabawi, Asma al-Nabawi, Asma al-Badriyyin, and Hizb al-Bahr; using prayer beads (tasbih); and reciting Mawlids like Mankhus, Badriyyat Bait, Muhyiddin Mala, and Rifai Mala—these are matters known in the religion by the scholars of Ahlus Sunnah wal-Jama'ah. Those who call these anti-religious or Shirk are not Sunnis, and they are not fit for the positions of Qazi, Khatib, or Imam.'' (Why They Should Be Shunned / Chaliyam A.P. Aboobacker Musliyar / pp. 14, 15).

This is the eighth resolution passed at the 6th conference of the Samastha Kerala Jam-iyyathul Ulama held in Feroke in 1933, presided over by Shihabuddin Ahmad Koya Shaliyati. This stems from the agreement made at a joint meeting in Kunduthode on June 2, 1974, for a 'Sunni-Mujahid debate,' where it was decided that calling upon deceased saints—like 'Help me, Muhyiddin Shaykh' or 'Protect us, Badriyyin'—is not Shirk but Mubah (permissible) (Kunduthode Debate, p. 2).

''The Maulavi's other insistence was to show an example in the Quran of praying to others besides Allah. When A.P. proved with the Quran itself that one can call upon the dead, the Maulavi fell flat there too'' (O.M. Tharuvana / Kottapuram Debate).

''It is permissible to call upon and pray to the deceased, such as 'Help me, Muhyiddin Shaykh' or 'Protect us, Badriyyin''' (Ponmala Abdul Qadir Musliyar / Fatawa Muhyissunna-2 / p. 38).

''Sunni scholars have convinced the Wahhabis that the principle of 'Prayer only to Allah' is actually the principle of Iblis'' (Hashim Na'imi / What Happened to Those Who Deviated / p. 37).

A Belief Entirely Alien to Islam

This is a belief entirely alien to Islam. The message of Islam is that 'Prayer; that is worship.' Allah says:

''وَقَالَ رَبُّكُمُ ادْعُونِي أَسْتَجِبْ لَكُمْ ۚ إِنَّ الَّذِينَ يَسْتَكْبِرُونَ عَنْ عِبَادَتِي سَيَدْخُلُونَ جَهَنَّمَ دَاخِرِينَ'' (40:60).

From Nu'man ibn Bashir (ra), the Prophet (saw) said: ''Supplication (Du'a) is worship'' (Tirmidhi).

When K.M. Maulavi led the renaissance movement pointing out the theological errors of the time, the book he wrote, titled 'Prayer and Worship', which was published by the Kerala Jam-iyyathul Ulama of that era, begins with this very verse.

Observe how Abdurrahman Makhdoomi Ponnani, a prominent Sunni scholar, openly wrote this truth in his Tafsir:

''Prayer is not just an act of worship; nothing is worship without prayer. Both prayer and worship are for Allah alone. Pray; He will answer us. Only the arrogant refuse to worship and pray to Allah. They are destined for an eternal and humiliating life in Hell'' (Quran Translation and Commentary 2/1032).

The Truth Proclaimed by All Prophets

This truth, which all Prophets who came to the world proclaimed to their people, was also declared by the Prophet (saw) to his community. Allah says:

''قُلْ إِنَّمَا أَدْعُو رَبِّي وَلَا أُشْرِكُ بِهِ أَحَدًا'' (72:20).

The Quran also clarifies that the true slaves of the Most Merciful are those who pray only to their Lord:

''وَالَّذِينَ لَا يَدْعُونَ مَعَ اللَّهِ إِلَٰهًا آخَرَ وَلَا يَقْتُلُونَ النَّفْسَ الَّتِي حَرَّمَ اللَّهُ إِلَّا بِالْحَقِّ وَلَا يَزْنُونَ ۚ وَمَن يَفْعَلْ ذَٰلِكَ يَلْقَ أَثَامًا'' (25:68).

The Truth Taught by the Sources

The sources teach us that there is no evidence to argue or believe that prayer can be directed to anyone other than Allah:

''وَمَن يَدْعُ مَعَ اللَّهِ إِلَٰهًا آخَرَ لَا بُرْهَانَ لَهُ بِهِ فَإِنَّمَا حِسَابُهُ عِندَ رَبِّهِ ۚ إِنَّهُ لَا يُفْلِحُ الْكَافِرُونَ'' (23:117).

Those who try to present false arguments to mix the impurity of Shirk into prayer are pretending not to see the warning of the Quran. Allah says:

''وَمَنْ أَضَلُّ مِمَّن يَدْعُو مِن دُونِ اللَّهِ مَن لَّا يَسْتَجِيبُ لَهُ إِلَىٰ يَوْمِ الْقِيَامَةِ وَهُمْ عَن دُعَائِهِمْ غَافِلُونَ. وَإِذَا حُشِرَ النَّاسُ كَانُوا لَهُمْ أَعْدَاءً وَكَانُوا بِعِبَادَتِهِمْ كَافِرِينَ'' (46:5,6).

Allah alone is the All-Hearing, the All-Knowing. No creature has a share in this. The knowledge and hearing of creatures have limits and constraints. Yet, when problems and crises arise, some in the Muslim community, listening to anti-scriptural words, go to shrines and tombs to prostrate and pray. They worship stones and trees planted in the ground. They are unable to understand the gravity of their error, or rather, priests prevent them from understanding it. The greatest ignorance is not knowing that there is a Creator waiting to answer the prayers of His slaves. Allah says:

''وَإِذَا سَأَلَكَ عِبَادِي عَنِّي فَإِنِّي قَرِيبٌ ۖ أُجِيبُ دَعْوَةَ الدَّاعِ إِذَا دَعَانِ ۖ فَلْيَسْتَجِيبُوا لِي وَلْيُؤْمِنُوا بِي لَعَلَّهُمْ يَرْشُدُونَ'' (2:186).

Simple Examples

Allah has provided simple examples to convince us that directing the act of worship known as prayer to anyone other than Allah is entirely wrong. Allah says:

''لَهُ دَعْوَةُ الْحَقِّ ۖ وَالَّذِينَ يَدْعُونَ مِن دُونِهِ لَا يَسْتَجِيبُونَ لَهُم بِشَيْءٍ إِلَّا كَبَاسِطِ كَفَّيْهِ إِلَى الْمَاءِ لِيَبْلُغَ فَاهُ وَمَا هُوَ بِبَالِغِهِ ۚ وَمَا دُعَاءُ الْكَافِرِينَ إِلَّا فِي ضَلَالٍ'' (13:14).

''يَا أَيُّهَا النَّاسُ ضُرِبَ مَثَلٌ فَاسْتَمِعُوا لَهُ ۚ إِنَّ الَّذِينَ تَدْعُونَ مِن دُونِ اللَّهِ لَن يَخْلُقُوا ذُبَابًا وَلَوِ اجْتَمَعُوا لَهُ ۖ وَإِن يَسْلُبْهُمُ الذُّبَابُ شَيْئًا لَّا يَسْتَنقِذُوهُ مِنْهُ ۚ ضَعُفَ الطَّالِبُ وَالْمَطْلُوبُ'' (22:73).

''مَثَلُ الَّذِينَ اتَّخَذُوا مِن دُونِ اللَّهِ أَوْلِيَاءَ كَمَثَلِ الْعَنكَبُوتِ اتَّخَذَتْ بَيْتًا ۖ وَإِنَّ أَوْهَنَ الْبُيُوتِ لَبَيْتُ الْعَنكَبُوتِ ۖ لَوْ كَانُوا يَعْلَمُونَ'' (29:41).

For those who treat what Islam has given such importance to as something trivial, and who issue resolutions and fatwas to pave the way for the major sin of Shirk, what else can be said other than that worldly gain is more important to them than salvation in the Hereafter!
