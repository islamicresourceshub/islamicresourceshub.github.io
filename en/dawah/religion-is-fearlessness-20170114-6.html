Religion is Fearlessness

ഉസ്മാൻ പാലക്കാഴി · 2017-01-14 · Dawah & Spirituality

There are many religions in the world today. Most of them are associated with individuals' names or some culture. Hinduism, Christianity, and Buddhism are some examples.

However, Islam is different from all of these. The very word Islam signifies its distinctiveness. The word 'Islam' is derived from the root word 'silm', which means peace and tranquility. A person becomes a Muslim when they completely surrender their life to Almighty Allah.

The Holy Quran introduces Islam as the religion of the universe and human nature. That is, the religion of the heavens, the earth, the celestial bodies, the stars, and all living beings. Observe what the Quran says:

“Then is it other than the religion of Allah they seek, while to Him have submitted [all] who are in the heavens and the earth, willingly or by compulsion, and to Him they will be returned.” (3:83)

“So set your face [O Muhammad] towards the religion, uprightly. [It is] the nature [created by] Allah in which He created people. No change is there in the creation of Allah. That is the correct religion, but most of the people do not know.” (30:30)

A Muslim is also called a 'Mu'min' or 'believer'. The word 'Iman' (faith) is derived from the root word 'amn', which means fearlessness and security. There is no doubt that a Muslim who lives by completely submitting to the Creator, entrusting their affairs to Him, firmly believing in all that needs to be believed, and performing all prescribed deeds, will experience indescribable fearlessness.

The words Mu'min, Iman, Aman, Amanat, Ameen, and others all indicate the same meaning. Various forms of 'amn' are used more than eight hundred times in the Holy Quran. Blessings, comfort, happiness, and freedom from fear and sorrow are things that those who order their lives according to the obedience of Allah and His Messenger receive.

No one should be physically harmed. No one's honor should be tarnished, and no one's wealth should be stolen or misappropriated. There should be no situation where anyone fears for their beliefs and practices or for propagating them. Individuals, families, and society should be within this sense of security. Everyone desires this fearlessness.

Islam has given great importance to fearlessness. There is no doubt that enjoining good, forbidding evil, and implementing punishments for crimes are essential for maintaining the fearlessness of society. Fearlessness is also essential for the nation and its people to achieve high goals and for economic and social prosperity.

A Fearless Nation

A fearless and fertile land is a great blessing:

“And they said, ‘We will surely be driven out from our land if we do not leave.’ Did We not establish for them a safe sanctuary to which the fruits of all things are brought as provision from Us? But, most of them do not know.” (28:57)

“So let them worship the Lord of this House [the Ka‘bah], Who has fed them, [saved them] from hunger and made them safe from fear.” (106:3,4)

We only realize the value of this blessing when it is lost. Palestine, Iraq, Syria, Myanmar... Millions of people burning in the furnace of warmongering and racism... Those living their lives amidst the smoke of unrest and fear... Those who were tragically killed... Those who fled seeking a place of safety and are living as refugees in foreign lands... Those who died on the way.... They know that fearlessness is a great blessing and how severe the consequences are when it is lost.

Fearlessness for Those Who Do Not Associate Partners with the Creator

Observe Allah's promise to those who worship the One Creator alone (those who live by embracing Tawhid):

“It is those who have believed and not confused their faith with injustice – those will have security, and they are the [rightly] guided.” (6:82)

The injustice mentioned here is associating partners with Allah, i.e., Shirk.

“And [mention, O Muhammad], when Luqman said to his son while he was instructing him, ‘O my son, do not associate [anything] with Allah. Indeed, associating [others] with Him is a great injustice.’” (31:13)

“Allah has promised those who believe among you and do righteous deeds that He will surely make them successors [in authority] upon the earth as He granted it to those before you and will establish for them [the dominance of] their religion which He has chosen for them and will substitute for them, after their fear, security. They worship Me, not associating anything with Me...” (24:55)

Fearlessness in Body, Honor, and Wealth

Ibn Umar (may Allah be pleased with him) said: The Prophet (peace be upon him) asked in Mina: “Do you know what day it is?” They said: “Allah and His Messenger know best.” He said: “This is a sacred day. Do you know what place it is?” They said: “Allah and His Messenger know best.” He said: “This is a sacred place. Do you know what month it is?” They said: “Allah and His Messenger know best.” He said: “This is a sacred month.” Then he said: “Indeed, your blood, your wealth, and your honor are sacred to you, just as this day, this place, and this month of yours are sacred.” (Bukhari)

From the perspective of Islamic law, the standard of a person's honor is not their social status or financial situation. Every individual's honor must be protected. Islam does not permit its violation. The young and the old, the rich and the poor, all find it unthinkable to lose their honor. However, modern society has a strange obsession with insulting, humiliating, and mocking others. This is often considered permissible in politics and journalism!

The above Hadith clarifies the value and standard of another person's honor.

Murders, assaults, fake encounters, robbery, looting, theft in the name of partisan politics and sectarianism... Actions that do not value the life and property of others! Islam, which completely opposes all these and considers them grave offenses, teaches that Islam is a fertile ground for fearlessness.

Injustice and Favoritism Eliminate Fearlessness

Aisha (may Allah be pleased with her) narrated: The case of a woman from the Makhzum tribe who stole became a serious problem for the Quraysh. “Who will speak to the Messenger of Allah about her case?” they asked each other. “Who would dare to do so except Usama ibn Zayd, whom the Messenger of Allah loves?” was their consensus. So, Usama spoke to the Prophet. The Prophet then asked: “Are you interceding in a matter of Allah's prescribed punishment?” Then, in a sermon he gave, he said: “The people before you were destroyed because if a noble person among them stole, they would let him go, but if a weak person stole, they would inflict the prescribed punishment on him. By Allah, if Fatimah, the daughter of Muhammad, were to steal, I would cut off her hand.” (Bukhari, Muslim)

This brilliant declaration of Islam is unique in world history; a declaration that modern rulers cannot even contemplate. The Prophet (peace be upon him) built a society that stood for justice, advocated for justice, and based its actions on justice, thereby granting fearlessness to his people.

The Holy Quran says: “O you who have believed, be persistently standing firm in justice, witnesses to Allah, even if it be against yourselves or parents, relatives...” (4:135).

Fearlessness Granted to Parents

Today, people greatly fear old age; not because they fear death, but because they fear being cast out of their homes and suffering without care when they get old! The fearlessness that Islam grants to the elderly is unparalleled. The Quran says:

“And your Lord has decreed that you not worship except Him, and to parents, good treatment. Whether one or both of them reach old age [while] with you, say not to them [so much as] 'uff' and do not repel them but speak to them a noble word. And lower to them the wing of humility out of mercy and say, ‘My Lord, have mercy on them even as they cared for me when I was young.’” (17:23,24)

It was narrated from Abu Hurayrah that the Prophet (peace be upon him) said: “Woe to him! Woe to him! Woe to him!” It was asked: “Who is that, O Messenger of Allah?” He said: “The one who lives to see his parents, one or both of them, reach old age [while he is alive] and does not enter Paradise [by serving them].” (Muslim)

These scriptural verses clearly show how seriously Islam views the duties towards parents. The Prophet, who declared destruction for those who fail in these duties, also taught us that fulfilling these duties makes entry into Paradise easier.

Fearlessness Through the Prohibition of Intoxicants

Intoxicants are the mother of all evils. Millions of individuals and families in our country are suffering from its consequences. How many wives live in distress, fearing the husband who comes home intoxicated... How many young children fear even the shadow of their father...! Drunkards who are a threat to the peace and fearlessness of neighbors and the community!

The enlightened people of Kerala are not unaware of the extent of the social menace caused by intoxicants. Yet, their use is increasing in our country. The fever for intoxicants is rapidly spreading even among students!

Islam provides fearlessness to individuals, families, society, and even the nation by prohibiting all kinds of intoxicants that corrupt and destroy individuals, families, and society.

It was narrated from Jabir ibn Abdullah that the Prophet (peace be upon him) said: “Whatever intoxicates in large quantities is forbidden in small quantities.” (Abu Dawud)

Islam Against Sectarianism That Spreads Fear

Sectarianism is a much-discussed topic in the contemporary world. What is sectarianism? Many people have different definitions. Observe the definition given by Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him): It was narrated by Wasila ibn al-Asqa': “I asked, ‘O Messenger of Allah, what is sectarianism?’ He replied, ‘To support your people in injustice.’” (Abu Dawud)

Is it sectarianism for a religious believer to believe that his faith and his religion are correct? Is it sectarianism for him to live according to his religious instructions? Is it sectarianism to be meticulous in performing religious rituals? Never! Such a person will not cause any trouble to anyone.

“Indeed, the religion in the sight of Allah is Islam...” (3:19).

“And whoever desires other than Islam as religion – it will never be accepted from him...” (3:85).

Based on these Quranic verses, Muslims believe that Islam is the only true religion; they proclaim and propagate it. Others have the right to do the same; to prove it in the light of evidence. None of this will cause any trouble to anyone.

Supporting and helping someone who has committed injustice, simply because he is from your community or your own people, is a grave transgression. This is what Islam calls sectarianism. It will lead to great calamities. It will cause anarchy in the country. It will spread fear. It will destroy fearlessness.

Yes, Islam is the religion of fearlessness. The message it offers to the world is one of fearlessness. The long beard and men's clothing that does not go below the ankle, and women's attire that does not reveal the body, are not symbols of fear; they are a message of security and fearlessness to the world. To reach Paradise, the sanctuary of fearlessness in the hereafter, Islam demands that we live in this world by providing fearlessness and comfort to others.
