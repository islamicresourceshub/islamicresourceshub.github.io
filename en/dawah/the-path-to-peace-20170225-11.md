Even as facilities and systems improve day by day, the new generation is forced to wander, having lost their peace of mind. The shores they reach in search of peace often create new continents of problems. The Creator has provided a system for peace in this world and the hereafter. Only through that can eternal peace be attained. What is it? An inquiry.

![](/assets/the-path-to-peace-20170225-11/25Feb17-06.jpg)

## The Race in Search of Peace

There is no human who does not experience tension. It makes no difference whether one is rich or poor, a worker or an employer. There are those who sigh over children who have reached marriageable age but remain unmarried... those who are desperate due to incurable diseases... those whose minds are shattered by failure in business... and many others.

Studies show that the number of modern humans wandering in search of peace and tranquility is increasing daily. Eventually, struggling with sleeplessness, they rely on sleeping pills. Others resort to alcohol and drugs to forget these tensions, at least temporarily. Still others decide to run away from life itself and choose suicide. The distress felt when one has no support to even open up and share their sorrows and grief is indescribable. The reality is that if a human is willing to accept the guidance of **Allah**, their Creator and Sustainer, they can find solutions to any of their problems and experience the vastness of peace and tranquility to the fullest.

## Searching in the Wrong Paths

When a human caught in the quagmire of tension and unrest searches for an island of peace, they often grab onto any straw, mistaking it for a strong rope. That is why people go to self-proclaimed godmen and mystics for problem-solving and peace.

The messages of the True God, the Creator and Protector of all living beings including humans, are not as weak as the ideas born from the limited intellect and thoughts of a weak human. But the fundamental problem is the unwillingness to know or search for them. When the first parents of humanity were sent down to the face of the earth, this was the message Allah gave. See what the **Quran** says:

> "We said: Go down from it, all of you. And when guidance comes to you from Me, whoever follows My guidance - there will be no fear concerning them, nor will they grieve. And those who disbelieve and deny Our signs - those will be companions of the Fire; they will abide therein eternally" (2:38,39).

Pointing out the consequences of ignoring divine guidance, the Quran says in another place:

> "He (Allah) said: Descend, both of you, from it together, some of you being enemies to others. And when there comes to you guidance from Me, then whoever follows My guidance will neither go astray nor suffer. And whoever turns away from My remembrance - indeed, he will have a depressed life, and We will gather him on the Day of Resurrection blind" (20:123,124).

## Islam: The Religion of Peace

Even the name of the divine religion, **Islam**, denotes peace and tranquility. While many other religions are known by names linked to their founders, teachers, regions, or races, Islam stands apart from all of that. The word Islam is derived from the Arabic word 'Silm'. The meaning of that word is safety, peace, and tranquility. The Holy Quran (in 8:61) can be seen using this word in this sense. Another meaning of the word Islam is submission and surrender. That is, by submitting everything before the Almighty God and surrendering to His commands and prohibitions, one reaches complete peace and salvation.

Likewise, the greetings exchanged when meeting one another contain prayers for peace and tranquility. When one greets with 'Assalamu Alaikum' (May the safety and peace of Allah be upon you), the response is also a prayer for peace and tranquility. Let us also remember the fact that this is suitable for any situation. 'Good morning' is not appropriate at a disaster site or a time of grief. But 'Salam' is suitable for any occasion.

Islam, the religion of peace, offers its messages for the salvation and peace of humans. Through divine guidance, Islam leads humans who are wandering in search of peace to a safe haven of tranquility. Here, we note a few things that Islam suggests to achieve peace by keeping away things that cause unrest.

## Control Greed and Urgency

A human with greed and urgency will not hesitate to do anything to fulfill them. A mind that is never satisfied drives them to many things. There will be a state where boundaries of good and evil, religious laws, justice, or rules are no obstacles. When trying to double their earnings, if they try to acquire the coins earned by poor brothers through their sweat and blood via deception and interest, they might consider it their greatness. But the 'tension' that arises when the castles in the air they built due to the urge of greed collapse might drive them to commit cruel acts that push not only their own lives but sometimes their family and society itself into danger.

This is why we often hear news of those who, trying to double their money through easy means, invested in various 'blade' companies and finally got trapped in fraud, or those who were cheated after approaching companies, mystics, and 'bibis' seen in advertisements with the delusion that they could become rich easily.

However, if one controls greed and starts any venture only after investigating in detail, it is possible to eliminate many miseries and tensions by oneself.

> **The Prophet (PBUH)** says: "Allah has said: If the son of Adam had a valley full of wealth, he would desire a second one. If he had two, he would crave a third. Nothing but dust will fill his belly" (**Ahmad**). This means his greed and urgency will be with him until he dies and is buried.

If there is a memory of death and an awareness of the life after death, one would not run without considering any boundaries for enjoyment and the fulfillment of pleasures. The Prophet (PBUH) says: "Remember frequently the destroyer of all pleasures (death)" (**Bukhari**).

## Practice Deliberation

Acting in haste often leads to regret. Things done with deliberation, order, and discipline will be beautiful and provide inner satisfaction. The Prophet (PBUH) says: "Deliberation is from Allah, and haste is from the devil" (**Bayhaqi**, Silsila Sahiha, Hadith number: 1795).

It may not be possible to correct the dangers caused by acting in haste later. Instead of getting 'tension' by feeling at the end that it should not have been done that way, one should make mature decisions through contemplation.

The Quran says:
> "O you who have believed, if there comes to you a disobedient one with information, investigate, lest you harm a people out of ignorance and become, over what you have done, regretful" (49:6).

The Prophet (PBUH) has forbidden such unnecessary haste even in acts of worship.

## Maintain Punctuality

Saying that one should be deliberate does not mean one should be lazy and put everything off. Do what needs to be done after thinking and according to priority. Stay as far away as possible from time-wasting activities and such company. If what needs to be done is not done at the right time, there will be 'tension' and difficulties later. The Prophet (PBUH) says: "There are two blessings regarding which many people are deceived: health and free time" (Bukhari, Muslim).

Perhaps later, we will have busyness, work, and problems where we do not get even a little free time. Therefore, time management is essential for a peaceful life.

Time-bound acts of worship are also a means to convince believers of the necessity of time management.

The Quran says:
> "Indeed, prayer has been decreed upon the believers a decree of specified times" (4:103).

## Be Content

Contentment is true wealth. The implication of the prophetic saying is that having plenty of wealth and other blessings cannot be considered true richness. The Prophet (PBUH) says: "Richness is not the abundance of material wealth. Rather, it is contentment of the mind; that is true richness and wealth" (Bukhari, Muslim).

If there is a thought of how to live with what one has, without worrying about what is missing, many worries can be reduced. As a means for that, the Prophet (PBUH) taught to look at those who are below oneself in terms of material blessings. Instead of sighing with envy looking at the mansions of the rich, **Muhammad (PBUH)** teaches to look at the poor who suffer more than oneself and offer praises to God who showered blessings. "Look at those who are below you. Do not look at those who are above you. That is best so that you do not belittle the blessings Allah has bestowed upon you" (Muslim).

Being content with what one has is no small matter. It is a great character trait. Even if someone earns tens of thousands in weeks, if they lose this mindset, they cannot enjoy life peacefully. But even if someone can only earn thousands in months, if they have such a mindset, they are the truly lucky ones. That is why when those who cannot sleep despite lying on silk mattresses in air-conditioned rooms of bungalows see those who sleep snoring soundly on newspapers spread on the roadside, they feel envious.

The Prophet (PBUH) says: "He who has submitted to Allah, whose provision is sufficient for his daily needs, and whom Allah has made content with what he has been given, has certainly succeeded" (Muslim).

## Excessive Spending is Dangerous

Some people, even if they get thousands in a day, can be seen blowing it all in hours and struggling the next day. If the path of wealth they are receiving is blocked, they might fall into a pathetic state of jumping to suicide, or a state of committing robbery and murder for wealth. It can be seen that excessive spending, luxury, and showing off play a big part in falling into debt. The Prophet (PBUH) has taught that debt destroys peace. If one lives without extravagance, financial problems can be largely eliminated. The Quran says:

> "And give the relative his right, and [also] the poor and the traveler, and do not spend wastefully. Indeed, the wasteful are brothers of the devils, and ever has Satan been to his Lord ungrateful. And if you [must] turn away from the needy awaiting mercy from your Lord which you expect, then speak to them a gentle word. And do not make your hand [as] chained to your neck or extend it completely and [thereby] become blamed and insolvent" (17:26-29).
