Do Not Become Slaves to Your Desires

ശമീര്‍ മദീനി · 2017-02-11 · Dawah & Spirituality

''فَإِن لَّمْ يَسْتَجِيبُوا لَكَ فَاعْلَمْ أَنَّمَا يَتَّبِعُونَ أَهْوَاءَهُمْ ۚ وَمَنْ أَضَلُّ مِمَّنِ اتَّبَعَ هَوَاهُ بِغَيْرِ هُدًى مِّنَ اللَّهِ ۚ إِنَّ اللَّهَ لَا يَهْدِي الْقَوْمَ الظَّالِمِينَ'' (القرآن 28:50)

The Importance of Divine Guidance

To be able to live according to the guidance of Allah, the Lord of the worlds, is a profound blessing. A true Muslim is one who has declared their readiness to accept and follow the commands and prohibitions of the Creator who fashioned and sustains this universe and all living things within it. In reality, every human being is obligated to follow the guidance of the Lord who has showered them with thousands of blessings. The entire universe moves in precise accordance with His strict laws.

Allah says: ''So is it other than the religion of Allah they desire, while to Him have submitted all those within the heavens and earth, willingly or by compulsion, and to Him they will be returned?'' (3:83).

Personal Desires and the Path of Truth

The ultimate path to salvation and peace is simply to follow that divine guidance. There are many who do not try to know or seek out that path. There are also not a few who, despite knowing and being convinced of it, hesitate to embrace it. Similarly, it is not rare to see those who become advocates of this path and take pride in it, yet conveniently turn a blind eye to that light by presenting various lame excuses before the laws of the Creator. The root cause of all this is nothing but personal desire, followed by worldly interests, stubbornness, and false justifications.

Ultimately, each of us must be committed to the God who created and sustains us, and to the truth He has revealed. Any other commitment only helps us bear the yoke of a mental slavery that is more unbearable than physical bondage. This means that neither temptations nor provocations should be allowed to hinder us from moving forward firmly on the path of truth.

The Example of the Steadfast

The Quran introduces the magicians of Pharaoh who declared their faith in Prophet Musa (peace be upon him) and Prophet Harun (peace be upon him) and stood firm on the path of truth. Those noble souls who stood with courage on the path of principles should be an example for us. Regarding them, Allah says:

''They said: Never will we prefer you over what has come to us of clear proofs and [over] He who created us. So decree whatever you will. You can only decree for this worldly life. Indeed, we have believed in our Lord, that He may forgive us our sins and what you compelled us [to do] of magic. And Allah is better and more enduring.'' (20:72, 73).

Promises on the Path of Truth

The Holy Quran has highlighted in many places the glad tidings that the Creator will be there to help those who have decided to live according to His guidance and have set out on the divine path.

It is also a promise of Allah that if one accepts the right path and stands firmly upon it, there will be relief and expansion from the crises of life.

For those idealists who live with a commitment to truth and justice rather than material gains, the Quran (41:30) announces a blessed outcome and success in the hereafter.

May the Almighty bless us to live with loyalty and commitment to the Creator and the true religion He has revealed, and to attain ultimate success.
