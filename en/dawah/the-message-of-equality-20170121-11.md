> The Prophet (PBUH) said: Verily, your Lord is One. Your father is also one.
> 
> All of you are from Adam, and Adam was from dust (**Muslim**, **Abu Dawud**).

## All humans are creations of the One Creator

**Islam** teaches that all human beings are creations of the One Creator and live under His protection. A unique feature of Islam is its declaration that the master and the slave, the rich and the poor, the employer and the employee are all equal. It does not permit discrimination among humans based on class, color, nationality, caste, or language. The Holy **Quran** declares:

> يَا أَيُّهَا النَّاسُ إِنَّا خَلَقْنَاكُم مِّن ذَكَرٍ وَأُنثَىٰ... إِنَّ أَكْرَمَكُمْ عِندَ اللَّهِ أَتْقَاكُمْ (49:13).
> (O mankind, indeed We have created you from male and female... Indeed, the most noble of you in the sight of Allah is the most righteous of you.)

> The Prophet (PBUH) said: No one has superiority over another except through piety and righteousness (**Mishkat**).

## Islam strikes at the root of all forms of inequality

The aforementioned verses inform us that in the eyes of Islam, all humans are equal, and only the God-fearing hold a special status before God. Islam strikes at the root of all forms of inequality. Islam desires justice for all. When the Prophet (PBUH) heard someone calling another person "son of a black woman," he warned him, saying:

> You still possess the ignorance of the pre-Islamic era.

When it was proven before the Prophet (PBUH) that a woman from the Makhzum tribe had committed theft, Usama (RA) interceded on her behalf. The Prophet (PBUH) then gathered the people and warned them:

> Those before you were destroyed because they would let the noble go free if they stole, but would take legal action if the lowly stole. By Allah, if Fatimah, the daughter of Muhammad, were to steal, I would cut off her hand.

Once, the story of the second Caliph, Umar (RA), and his servant taking turns riding a camel is famous. While the people were waiting to welcome the arrival of the Caliph, they saw the servant riding the camel while the Caliph was leading it by the rope!

When a high-ranking official described the grandeur and glory of the Emperor Caesar to Mu'adh ibn Jabal (RA), who had gone to Rome as a diplomat, Mu'adh described his own ruler as follows:

> Our ruler is one of us. If he follows our scripture and the Prophet's tradition, we appoint him as Caliph. If he does not accept this, we will remove him from power. If he commits theft, we will cut off his hand. If he commits adultery, we will stone him to death. If he injures anyone, we will seek retribution. It is not possible for him to remain in power while blocking our access to him. He does not act with arrogance among us, nor does he rule over us with pride. He has no more right to the spoils of war than we do. He is just a human being like us.
> 
> — (**Futuh al-Sham**)

## The religion that spread light in the darkness of the sixth century

There are countless Quranic verses, Prophetic sayings, and citations from Islamic history that demonstrate that Islam is the religion that spread the light of true human equality in the darkness of the sixth century.

> The Prophet (PBUH) declared in his farewell sermon: All evil customs maintained on the basis of wealth, lineage, and the like are today trampled under my two feet. You are all from Adam, and Adam was from dust.
