Maintaining Faith

അഡ്വ.കെ.എ. അബ്ദുസ്സമദ് കലൂര്‍ · 2017-02-11 · Dawah & Spirituality

Modern science acknowledges that humans have not been able to utilize even a quarter of their brain, the center of human intellect. The stream of thought originating from this brain, which holds vast dormant potential, is guided, controlled, and influenced by various emotions arising from the heart. Emotions are feelings that flash through the heart or the mind in mere seconds. Feelings such as joy, sorrow, anger, jealousy, despair, doubt, thirst, desire, addiction, pride, shame, and hunger constantly arise in the mind and continue to influence our thoughts.

Taqwa and the Heart

«Taqwa» - or God-consciousness - is also an emotion.

In a Hadith narrated by Abu Huraira (RA) and recorded by Imam Muslim, we see that the Prophet (SAW) pointed to his chest three times and said, "God-consciousness is here."

We see that the Holy Quran constantly reminds us to maintain God-consciousness. However, the mind is not always able to maintain this state because the human heart («Qalb») changes rapidly. The word «Qalb» itself carries the meaning of something that flips or changes constantly.

Abdullah ibn Amr (RA) narrates that the Prophet (SAW) said: "The hearts of the children of Adam are between the fingers of the Most Merciful, like a single heart. He turns them however He wills."

Then he prayed: "O Allah, Controller of hearts, turn our hearts toward Your obedience..." (Muslim 2654).

"O you who have believed, respond to Allah and to the Messenger when he calls you to that which gives you life. And know that Allah intervenes between a man and his heart and that to Him you will be gathered" (8:24).

"Except for one who comes to Allah with a sound heart" (26:89).

"And they were not commanded except to worship Allah, [being] sincere to Him in religion, inclining to truth, and to establish prayer and to give zakah. And that is the correct religion" (98:5).

"Their meat will not reach Allah, nor will their blood, but what reaches Him is piety from you" (22:37).

Renewing Faith

Every word and action of a true believer should aim for the pleasure of Allah. This is only possible if «Iman» - or true faith - remains firm in the heart.

The Prophet (SAW) said: "Faith in your heart will wear out just as a garment wears out. So ask Allah to renew the faith in your hearts" (Tabarani, Hakim).

Religious texts teach us that faith is subject to increase and decrease.

"It is He who sent down tranquility into the hearts of the believers that they would increase in faith along with their [present] faith..." (48:4).

"And whenever a surah is revealed, there are among the hypocrites those who say, 'Which of you has this increased in faith?' As for those who believed, it has increased them in faith, while they are rejoicing" (9:124).

There are many ways to strengthen faith. Here are a few:

  • Recite the Holy Quran and delve into its meanings:

    "The believers are only those who, when Allah is mentioned, their hearts become fearful, and when His verses are recited to them, it increases them in faith; and upon their Lord they rely" (8:2). "And We send down of the Quran that which is healing and mercy for the believers..." (17:82).

  • Develop an awareness of the greatness of Allah. Understand His attributes, reflect upon them, and glorify His name.
  • Acquire religious knowledge:

    "...Only those fear Allah, from among His servants, who have knowledge..." (35:28). "Say, 'Are those who know equal to those who do not know?'" (39:9).

  • Spend time in good deeds and be swift in performing them:

    Allah says: "And hasten to forgiveness from your Lord and a garden as wide as the heavens and earth, prepared for the righteous" (3:133).

Self-Evaluation

Along with the hope that deeds will be accepted, there must also be a fear that they might be rejected. Fearing a bad end and maintaining thoughts of death will increase faith. Those who hate the thought of death will delay repentance. They will never be content with what they have and will become lazy in their worship.

Place your hope in Allah and be humble before Him. Reflect on the insignificance of this worldly life. Honor the symbols of Allah's religion. Abandon arrogance. Love Allah. Maintain good thoughts about Him. Rely upon Him. Accept His decree. Be grateful to Him and return to Him in repentance. Evaluate yourself:

"O you who have believed, fear Allah. And let every soul look to what it has put forth for tomorrow - and fear Allah. Indeed, Allah is Acquainted with what you do" (59:18).
