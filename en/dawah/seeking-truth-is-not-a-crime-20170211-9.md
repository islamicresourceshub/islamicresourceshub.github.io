## Fundamental Human Needs

Food, clothing, and shelter are often cited as the basic needs of humanity. While water, food, and air are essential for sustaining life, the right to ask why we live is a fundamental necessity for understanding the meaning and relevance of life itself. There may not be many in society who actively seek the answer to this question, but it is rare to find someone who has not, at some point, asked it of others or themselves.

## The Quest and Religion

For someone in whom this question has sprouted, finding an answer becomes essential. Those who embark on this search do not initially know what the answer is or where to find it. Through reading and speaking with others, a direction begins to emerge. This search eventually leads to the study of **God** and His involvement in the universe. If someone, in their quest for the purpose of life, attempts to understand religions, conducts a comparative study, and subsequently embraces a faith they find convincing, what is wrong with that?

Today, many in our society view a truth-seeker who wishes to walk the path of spirituality as a culprit. We must not ignore the fact that many incidents are occurring in our country that reinforce the suspicion that the legal system, the government, the public, and the media are all trying to view the act of embracing a religion that answers one's inner questions and brings peace as a grave crime.

## Misconceptions About Islam

What we are discussing here is **Islam**. Misconceptions about Islam are at the root of all these problems. Religion is divinely ordained; it is His guidance. It invites us toward truth and goodness. It teaches us the truth that we have a God to whom we can submit our agonizing problems. It brings the glad tidings that there is a reward after death for our struggles and sacrifices in the path of God.

That Creator is One. He is the Creator of all human beings, all living creatures, and indeed the entire universe, regardless of caste, religion, class, color, wealth, poverty, region, or language. Religion teaches us to worship Him alone, follow the commands of His messenger, and live as good human beings. A good human is a fertile ground for all virtues—a person who understands that it is their duty to help those who are suffering in their family and society. As a reward for all this, religion offers the glad tidings of Paradise as a gift from God in the afterlife.

Then why do so many people hate or fear Islam?

## Allegations and Reality

Allegations such as terrorism and the denial of women's freedom are leveled against Islam. There are constant efforts from various quarters to establish these claims, and global media provides all the support needed for this. These ideas are deeply embedded in the public mind. When some renegades or terrorist organizations with Arabic names commit acts of terror, interpretations are presented as if all these allegations are true. This is what is happening today.

Terrorism is alien to religion. It is extreme cruelty toward the innocent. In reality, it often stems from the economic, social, and political crises within respective countries. The protests and reactions of the youth in those countries against these conditions are what manifest as terrorism. It is just that a religious mask is later placed upon it.

In truth, religion desires the safety of women, not the infringement of their freedom. It is for this reason that certain guidelines were given regarding dress and that behavioral etiquette was taught. Furthermore, Islam instructs both men and women to lower their gaze equally. Let us consider whether incidents of harassment would increase or decrease if these were followed. How do these protective restrictions become a denial of freedom?

## Humanity and Religion

Islam does not seek to distance people from one another but to bring them closer. It commands mutual love and brotherhood. Efforts are being made in our country to create rifts between religious communities to secure seats of power. This is entirely anti-humanitarian.

Islam, the final scripture, views all prophets appointed by God to lead humanity out of darkness and into the light as equal. It was the discrimination between them that previously became a cause for the growth of sectarianism in society.

Religion holds the solution to terrorism, violence, anarchy, and the violation of rights. One who fears God cannot commit these acts.

Religion is God's guidance. One must understand that certain international powers have an agenda to ensure that humans do not live well by following it. Western power centers that control the media have crafted an agenda to foster anti-Islamic sentiment among people worldwide. As a result, for many, even hearing the word Islam today evokes fear. It has become that repulsive to them.

Let us work together for a state where the fear of God and human brotherhood prevail.
