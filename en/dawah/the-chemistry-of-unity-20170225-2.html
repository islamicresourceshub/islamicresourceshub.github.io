The Chemistry of Unity

ടി.കെ.അശ്‌റഫ് · 2017-02-25 · Dawah & Spirituality

Is Unity a Barrier to the Propagation of Tawheed?

As long as the decision remains to keep the public in the dark, the call for a leap forward in the propagation of Tawheed will remain nothing more than a hollow sound echoing within the walls of the organization's council. How can the call to move forward with Tawheed be implemented if one cannot answer the questions posed by the traditionalist faction? Why, despite uniting and gathering strength, has it been impossible to respond to the constant criticisms leveled by the traditionalists since December 5th? The failure to challenge the clergy in places like Pulikkal—the ancestral home of the Mujahids—and many other regions was due to the loss of ideological clarity that occurred through this unity. However, the Mujahid outreach initiative, Wisdom Global Islamic Mission, has been able to provide responses that break the edge of these false allegations, Alhamdulillah! This was possible only because they stood firm without making any compromises in their ideology.

At the conference of Pattikkad Jamia Nooriya, Abdul Hameed Faizi Ambalakkadavu repeatedly issued challenges while launching a scathing attack on Tawheed and the Mujahid movement. Why have the Mujahids, who united under the banner of moving forward with Tawheed, been unable to take up this challenge?

At the ideological clarification conference held by Wisdom in Pattikkad on January 23, 2017, the ability to courageously accept Hameed Faizi's challenge was solely due to their adherence to ideological principles.

Unity or New Division?

Unity becomes a reality only when it enables one to correct mistakes and move forward without compromising on ideology. If, instead, the two factions have united merely to suppress the other, it is a step toward their own destruction. One would hope that once the two organizations become one, their disputes and arguments would end, and that chaos in mosques and institutions would cease—this was the expectation that led community leaders, politicians, and the public to bless and congratulate the Mujahid unity. However, events following the unity show that it has only paved the way for further conflict and litigation in Mujahid institutions. In many peaceful mosques, the existing Khateebs were replaced under the guise of unity. People were removed from institutional responsibilities simply for attending a program. Some were mentally harassed and forced to step back. There was commotion inside mosques after Jumu'ah prayers. Locks on Madrasas were broken. Complaints were filed at police stations. New cases were registered.

Allegation or Fact?

Those running the institutions are being persecuted under the pretext that those who left the KNM and are operating under a different name have no right to its assets. Furthermore, they spread the lie that these people pray to Jinn. They also add the spice that these people have established treatment centers for spiritual exploitation. What is the truth?

  1. During the crisis in 2012, prominent scholars, preachers, organizers, youth and student organizations, and subordinate units were dismissed from the organization in violation of the rules. They did not leave voluntarily. Only a few sincere workers, heartbroken by this, stepped aside on their own.
  2. It is a fact that we did not commit the folly that Hussain Madavoor did in 2002, when he claimed to be the new General Secretary of KNM by misleading the court with fake minutes of a KNM council meeting.
  3. Instead of open confrontation, those subjected to these actions became active in the field of Dawah. The desire not to create discord in the field of propagation by using the name KNM is what motivated this approach. However, to identify this collective and to comply with the laws for organized work in the country, the Dawah system known as the Wisdom Global Islamic Mission was formed.

How does this make them people with no rights in the KNM organization? This collective fully accepts the evidence-based positions held by the KNM from its inception until 2012. No one has yet been able to objectively prove that they have made any changes to those positions.

Since the actions taken were without following constitutional rules, the organizational disciplinary actions have no validity. Therefore, those subjected to these actions have not ceased to be members of the KNM.

When the KNM failed to fulfill the purpose for which it was formed, when the organization was hijacked by a group of self-interested individuals, and when those working within the KNM could no longer carry out Dawah activities in tune with the pulse of the times, it is true that they utilized a new system under the name Wisdom, as there is no rule in the KNM constitution prohibiting membership in another society with similar objectives. In the new organizational situation, this collective adopted the democratic norm of bringing the issue before the court instead of choosing the path of violence. Workers and well-wishers have been forced to take necessary legal action to ensure justice and to protect the assets and institutions built by the sweat and blood of the workers.

Since the Madavoor faction was brought into organizational bodies in violation of the KNM constitution without changing their ideological positions, this fact was naturally brought to the attention of the court.

When the Madavoor faction, which lost the legal battle fought by both sides up to the Supreme Court at the cost of lakhs, is easily given entry into the governing body, is it not natural to question it? The gravity of this increases when it is done while masking their ideology. Interpreting this move as filing a case against unity is merely a sign of helplessness, having nothing else to say.

Who Prayed to Jinn?

In the last four years, has anyone from this collective been found praying to Jinn? Not only has that not happened, but this collective is actively engaged in Kerala with multifaceted programs that strongly convince society that one must pray only to Allah, and that praying to the dead, Jinn, angels, or anyone else is Shirk.

  • Through the 'Door-to-Door' Dawah system, this collective has been able to bring the message of Tawheed to about ten million people in nearly two million homes.
  • Another Dawah method is the internet radio called 'Peace', which is downloaded by over a hundred thousand people in various parts of the world and listened to by tens of thousands at the same time.
  • The five-minute lecture titled 'Ner Vazhi' (The Right Path), which is listened to by tens of thousands every day, is approaching its six-hundredth day.

All of these are for the sake of Tawheed.

Where Were the Spiritual Exploitation Centers Started?

This collective has demanded that the government enact legislation against spiritual exploitation. If the goal was to establish Jinn centers, as the critics claim, wouldn't they have started them during the last four years? Not only did that not happen, but this collective has waged a strong campaign against such centers.

It was this collective that worked at the forefront against such spiritual exploitation in places like Mukkam Kalanthode and Wayanad, and submitted a blueprint to the government to enact legislation against it.

In a pathetic situation where the organization and its institutions are being seized by spreading the lie that they are people who pray to Jinn, and the founders are being ousted, seeking legitimate paths of resistance is natural.

Unity Without Precedent in History

It is a fact that Hussain Madavoor said during his speech at the unity conference on the beach that the Mujahid unity is unprecedented in the fourteen centuries of Islamic history. This may be why no example cited from history to justify the unity actually fits.

The Hudaybiyyah Treaty between the Mushrikeen and the Muslims is the example cited by the Madavoor faction. If that is true, they would have to admit that one faction consists of Mushrikeen.

Moreover, the Madavoor faction has committed unparalleled hypocrisy by secretly watering down the unity formula, while teaching the followers the lesson from the Hudaybiyyah Treaty that victory comes to those who wait patiently, without compromising on ideology.

The other example cited is the incident where Prophet Yusuf (AS) forgave his brothers after they had thrown him into a well and he later gained power. According to this, one faction would have to admit that they are like the brothers who threw him into the well.

Let Us Move Forward Together

If the desire for those who were divided for fourteen years to unite stems from sincerity, then the ideologically lingering issues should be explained and resolved based on evidence. The day is not far when the sincere followers of both factions, who have not yet understood the danger of the changes made to the definition of Tawheed taught since the time of K.M. Moulavi for the sake of group rivalry, will realize it. May the Lord bless everyone to live together after becoming righteous, holding fast to the Sunnah and remaining firm in Tawheed.
