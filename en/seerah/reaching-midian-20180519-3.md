**(Prophet Musa (peace be upon him): 5)**

![Musa (peace be upon him) at the well in Midian](/assets/reaching-midian-20180519-3/19May18-14.jpg)

> «وَلَمَّا وَرَدَ مَاءَ مَدْيَنَ وَجَدَ عَلَيْهِ أُمَّةً مِّنَ النَّاسِ يَسْقُونَ وَوَجَدَ مِن دُونِهِمُ امْرَأَتَيْنِ تَذُودَانِ ۖ قَالَ مَا خَطْبُكُمَا ۖ قَالَتَا لَا نَسْقِي حَتَّىٰ يُصْدِرَ الرِّعَاءُ ۖ وَأَبُونَا شَيْخٌ كَبِيرٌ فَسَقَىٰ لَهُمَا ثُمَّ تَوَلَّىٰ إِلَى الظِّلِّ فَقَالَ رَبِّ إِنِّي لِمَا أَنزَلْتَ إِلَيَّ مِنْ خَيْرٍ فَقِيرٌ»
> ''And when he arrived at the watering place of Midian, he found there a crowd of people watering [their flocks], and he found aside from them two women driving back [their flocks]. He said, 'What is your circumstance?' They said, 'We cannot water until the shepherds depart [with their flocks]; and our father is an old man.' So he watered [their flocks] for them; then he turned aside to the shade and said, 'My Lord, indeed I am, for whatever good You would send down to me, in need.''' (Quran 28:23,24).

## Scenes in Midian

After enduring all the hardships of his journey, Musa (peace be upon him) arrived in **Midian** with great difficulty. The Quran tells us about the beginning of Musa's (peace be upon him) life in Midian through a scene he witnessed there.

Near a well, a crowd of people stood gathered, watering their sheep. Amidst their hustle and bustle, two women stood a little distance away, holding back their flock of sheep. The strong men were watering their sheep by their might.

## Musa's (peace be upon him) Help

Musa (peace be upon him) approached both of them and inquired why they were standing aside without watering their sheep. They replied, 'We cannot water our sheep until these male shepherds finish watering theirs. Among these strong shepherds, we have no man to help us push through the crowd and water our sheep. Our father is an old man, and he is unable to do it. That is why we have brought them here.'

Musa (peace be upon him) understood their situation. From their standing apart, not mingling with the men, he also understood that they were chaste women from a respectable family.

Musa (peace be upon him) decided to help them. He watered their sheep from among the men there. Musa (peace be upon him) did not offer his help expecting any reward from them.

## Musa's (peace be upon him) Supplication

It was a very hot time of day. After watering their sheep, Musa (peace be upon him) moved a little distance away and rested in the shade. Then he supplicated to Allah, saying: 'My Lord, indeed I am, for whatever good You would send down to me, in need.'

Everyone in Midian was a stranger to him. He had no extended family or acquaintances there. Only Allah was his Protector.

## The Virtue of Helping Others

We should not hesitate to help those in need of assistance. We must not pretend not to see them. We must not forget that Allah loves those who are benevolent to others.

> It is narrated from **Ibn Umar (may Allah be pleased with him)** that a man came to the **Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him)**. He then asked: 'O Messenger of Allah, who among people is most beloved to Allah? And what are the deeds most beloved to Allah?' The Messenger of Allah (peace and blessings be upon him) replied: 'The most beloved of people to Allah are those who are most beneficial to people. The most beloved deeds to Allah are those that bring joy to a Muslim, or the actions you take to relieve a Muslim's distress, or actions that alleviate his debt, or actions that satisfy his hunger.' (Then the Prophet (peace and blessings be upon him) said:) 'For me to walk with a brother to fulfill his need is more beloved to me than to seclude myself in this Masjid an-Nabawi for a month. Whoever suppresses his anger, Allah will conceal his faults. Whoever walks with his brother to fulfill his need, Allah will make his feet firm on the Day when feet will slip' (**at-Tabarani**). In another narration, it states: 'Whoever walks with his brother until his need is fulfilled, Allah will make his feet firm on the Day when feet will slip' (at-Tabarani).

These prophetic sayings make us understand how great the virtue of helping others is. Allah introduces those who do not help others as deniers of the faith through **Surah al-Ma'un**.

We must help others. And our help should not be motivated by any desire for gain from those being helped. We should undertake it solely desiring the reward from Allah.

## Prayer Answered

Allah answered Musa's (peace be upon him) sincere supplication, which was devoid of any trace of impatience or despair. Allah says:

> «فَجَاءَتْهُ إِحْدَاهُمَا تَمْشِي عَلَى اسْتِحْيَاءٍ قَالَتْ إِنَّ أَبِي يَدْعُوكَ لِيَجْزِيَكَ أَجْرَ مَا سَقَيْتَ لَنَا ۚ فَلَمَّا جَاءَهُ وَقَصَّ عَلَيْهِ الْقَصَصَ قَالَ لَا تَخَفْ ۖ نَجَوْتَ مِنَ الْقَوْمِ الظَّالِمِينَ»
> ''Then one of the two women came to him walking with shyness. She said, 'Indeed, my father invites you that he may reward you for having watered for us.' So when he came to him and related to him the story, he said, 'Fear not. You have escaped from the wrongdoing people.''' (Quran 28:25).

## Modesty and Faith

It is noteworthy that the Quran specifically highlights the modesty of that woman here. Modesty is indeed a part of faith. There should be no modesty in doing good deeds. And it should be something that keeps one away from evil. Only then does it become part of faith. There should be no modesty in asking to understand religious matters or in practicing them.

## The Father's Invitation

The father also understood that the young man who sincerely helped his daughters when they were struggling to water their sheep was a good person. So, he sent his daughter to Musa (peace be upon him) to invite him home.

## Musa's (peace be upon him) Prudence

Accepting the invitation, Musa (peace be upon him) went with her to her father. At that time, Musa (peace be upon him) asked her to walk behind him and guide him on the way. When a man walks behind a woman, there is a greater possibility of seeing her form or being attracted to her beauty if there is wind or other factors. This prudence might have prompted Musa (peace be upon him) to give such a suggestion.

Musa (peace be upon him) reached the father of the two women. Musa (peace be upon him) recounted to him all the reasons for his journey from Egypt to Midian. The father listened to all of Musa's (peace be upon him) account. He offered comforting words and reassurance.

## The Father's Identity: Scholarly Opinions

There are differing opinions among Quranic exegetes regarding the identity of that man. One group says that it was **Prophet Shu'aib (peace be upon him)**. Another group says that this is not correct. The reason they point out is that there was a difference of several generations between Prophet Shu'aib (peace be upon him) and Musa (peace be upon him). During the time of Prophet Ibrahim (peace be upon him), Lut (peace be upon him) was also present. After the destruction of Lut's people, Shu'aib (peace be upon him) was sent to Midian, the neighboring region. When Shu'aib (peace be upon him) spoke to the people who disbelieved in him, he mentioned the fate of Prophet Lut's (peace be upon him) people, who were their close neighbors and acquaintances. Therefore, Shu'aib (peace be upon him) lived close to the time of Prophet Ibrahim (peace be upon him) and Prophet Lut (peace be upon him). Hence, some scholars say that it is unlikely for this father to be Shu'aib (peace be upon him). Since there are no clear textual proofs on this matter, it is not necessary to state whether it was Prophet Shu'aib (peace be upon him) or not. This is the opinion held by **Ibn Jarir**. Moreover, after the destruction of Prophet Shu'aib's (peace be upon him) people, only those who believed in him remained there. Would such believers cause hardship to the daughters of their Prophet? All this emphasizes that it was not Shu'aib (peace be upon him). If that were the case, it would imply that Prophet Shu'aib (peace be upon him) was from a later generation.

## The Best Hired Hand

While Musa (peace be upon him) and the father of the two women were conversing, one of the women said:

> «قَالَتْ إِحْدَاهُمَا يَا أَبَتِ اسْتَأْجِرْهُ ۖ إِنَّ خَيْرَ مَنِ اسْتَأْجَرْتَ الْقَوِيُّ الْأَمِينُ»
> ''...One of them said, 'O my father, hire him. Indeed, the best of those you can hire is the strong and the trustworthy.''' (Quran 28:26).

Musa (peace be upon him) was invited to their home to be given a reward. Musa (peace be upon him), on the other hand, was a stranger with no one to support him. It was a time when even finding a job for livelihood would be a great relief.

His physical strength, demonstrated by watering their sheep amidst the strong men, and the prudence he showed when they inquired about him and when walking home, impressed them. She also informed her father that he was suitable for them in every way.

The two qualities employers must ensure in a worker are competence and trustworthiness.
- If the worker is incompetent, resentment will arise in both parties. The employer's frustration from the worker not doing what is commanded, and the worker's frustration from being made to do what they cannot. It goes without saying that this can create great problems.
- The same applies to trustworthiness. If there is mutual trust, the work will proceed happily.
