# Salafi Da'wah: A Philosophical Analysis: 2

![](/assets/criteria-for-accepting-hadith-20170128-2/28Jan17-07.jpg)

If anyone truly wishes to follow the Quran and Sunnah, it is essential to rely on the path traversed by the **Salaf**—the companions of the Prophet (peace be upon him), the Tabi'un (successors), and those who followed them.

## The Counter-Arguments of Scholars and Their Contradictions

You may have heard words spoken by some who are referred to as scholars that contradict this. However, this is not the path of true knowledge that we have previously explained—which is based on the Quran, the Sunnah, and the way of the Salaf. Instead, by 'knowledge,' they mean only what they personally grasp directly from the Quran and Sunnah. They do not even look toward the path of the Salaf, which protects them from the misguided sects.

Consequently, in cassettes and books published in this era, you may see and hear many things attributed to those who are called scholars and pose as their spokespersons, contradicting the evidence we have cited. This is what they often say:

> The path of the Salaf (the predecessors) is the safest, but the path of the Khalaf (the later generations) is the most robust and scholarly!

This is a clear, public admission of corruption. In reality, this is a confession that they acknowledge what we have said—that it is mandatory to follow the path of the righteous companions of the Prophet (peace be upon him)—while simultaneously rejecting it.

In other words, when they say the knowledge of the Salaf is 'safe' but what the Khalaf did is 'robust,' they mean they have abandoned the path of the Salaf that directed them to follow the Prophet (peace be upon him).

We can provide several examples that clarify the difference between those who follow the path of the Salaf and those who have abandoned it through innovative arguments.

We can definitively state that the new arguments and thoughts brought forth by these people, who do not even look at what the Salaf al-Salih said, are futile arguments contrary to the Quran and Sunnah. This is because they stand against the path held by the Prophet (peace be upon him), his companions, and the Tabi'un.

## The Modern Innovation in Distinguishing Mutawatir and Ahad Hadith

One such example is the verbal gymnastics performed by some in this era who distinguish between **Mutawatir** (mass-transmitted) and **Ahad** (solitary-transmitted) Hadith to reject those pertaining to matters of faith. This is because the **Salaf al-Salih** were unfamiliar with such a distinction. The later generations who fabricated this innovation also derived many new rulings based on it. Thus, they claimed that even if an Ahad Hadith is Sahih (authentic), it should not be accepted if it pertains to matters of faith. This division—and the resulting distinction that Hadith regarding matters of faith are unacceptable unless they are Mutawatir, while they are acceptable in Ahkam (legal rulings)—is a new innovation that anyone who understands the methodology of the Salaf al-Salih can firmly state was introduced in the name of Islam. It is a philosophy unrelated to Islam. This is a fact known to both us and them, yet they deny it knowingly. As Allah said about others:

> "And they denied them, while their souls were convinced thereof, out of injustice and haughtiness. So see how was the end of the corrupters." (27:14).

## The Prophet (peace be upon him) Sending the Companions and the Hadith of Mu'adh (may Allah be pleased with him)

It is a well-known fact that the Prophet (peace be upon him) sent his companions to invite people outside of Medina to Islam. Who does not know that Islam consists of the faith and actions brought by the Prophet (peace be upon him)?

It is a well-established event in the Sunnah that the Prophet (peace be upon him) sent Mu'adh (may Allah be pleased with him) to Yemen on some occasions, Abu Musa al-Ash'ari (may Allah be pleased with him) on others, and Ali (may Allah be pleased with him) at other times. They know this, yet they feign ignorance!

When the Prophet (peace be upon him) sent them there, what did these companions do? Without a doubt, they invited the people to believe in Allah and His Messenger. That is, in reality, the foundation of all matters of faith. Afterward, they invited them to the Islamic teachings brought by the Prophet (peace be upon him).

In a Hadith narrated by Anas (may Allah be pleased with him) in **Sahih al-Bukhari and Muslim**, it is stated:

> "When the Prophet (peace be upon him) sent Mu'adh (may Allah be pleased with him) to Yemen, he said to him: 'The first thing you should invite them to is the testimony that there is no god worthy of worship except Allah and that Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah. If they obey you in that, then command them regarding prayer...'"

Here, the Prophet (peace be upon him) is commanding Mu'adh (may Allah be pleased with him), who is a single individual. According to the technical terminology of the later generations, his report is an Ahad Hadith. Yet, the Prophet (peace be upon him) instructs him to invite them first to believe in the oneness of Allah and that He has no partner.

(The first thing they should be invited to is the testimony that there is no god worthy of worship except Allah.)

You have rejected this Hadith, which both those who follow the Salaf and those who oppose them agree is Sahih. That is, the Prophet (peace be upon him) sent Mu'adh (may Allah be pleased with him) to Yemen and commanded him to invite them to the truth (Tawhid) that there is no god worthy of worship except Allah. How then can you say that this Hadith is Sahih, yet Ahad Hadith are not acceptable in matters of faith (Aqeedah)?

You can see from this the deviation from the path of the Prophet's companions, who invited people to Islam and propagated it without the distinction of Mutawatir and Ahad. In truth, for any believer with intellect, this single issue is enough to realize the danger and gravity of deviating from following the Quran, the Sunnah, and the Manhaj (methodology) of the Salaf.

Therefore, anyone who wishes to follow the path of these righteous predecessors must recognize the stances and procedures of the Salaf—including the companions, the Tabi'un, and their followers—just as they study the Quran and Sunnah. This is because they are the ones who delivered this ideology to us in its correct form.

## Aqeedah and Hukm in the Hadith of Abu Hurayrah (may Allah be pleased with him)

By distinguishing Hadith into Mutawatir and Ahad and claiming that Ahad cannot be used as evidence for matters of faith, these people fall into astonishing contradictions. This is because some Hadith contain both Aqeedah (faith) and Hukm (legal ruling) simultaneously; like the Hadith of Abu Hurayrah (may Allah be pleased with him) narrated by Bukhari and Muslim: The Prophet (peace be upon him) said:

> "When any of you sits for the final Tashahhud, let him seek refuge in Allah from four things." He says: "O Allah, I seek refuge in You from the severe punishment of Hell, from the punishment of the grave, from the trial of the Al-Masih ad-Dajjal, and from the trials of life and death."

This Hadith contains an instruction to seek refuge from four things. It is one of the laws of the Sharia. They, like us, say that matters of action are established by Ahad Hadith (non-Mutawatir Hadith). Therefore, they have no choice but to act according to this Hadith. But it contains seeking refuge from the punishment of the grave, the trials of life and death, and the tribulations of the Dajjal. Do they believe in the punishment of the grave?

Here, these people fall into a terrible trap. The punishment of the grave is a matter of faith. According to their argument, the punishment of the grave has not been established by a Mutawatir Hadith. Therefore, they do not even believe in the punishment of the grave! Except for the fact that it is mentioned in the Quran regarding Pharaoh, it is impossible to confirm it for anyone else:

> "The Fire: they are exposed to it morning and evening. And on the Day the Hour appears [it will be said], 'Make the people of Pharaoh enter the severest punishment.'" (40:46).

## The Punishment of the Grave - The Hadith of Ibn Abbas (may Allah be pleased with him) and Other Evidence

Regarding this Fire, these people say it is a punishment for Pharaoh and his people. However, they do not believe it for other disbelievers or for some Muslims for whom the punishment of the grave is established. This is because of their aforementioned misguided argument! That is, even if a Hadith is Sahih, if it has not reached the level of Mutawatir, Aqeedah cannot be established by it! For that reason, these people deny a large number of Hadith. According to their argument, it is for the sole reason that none of them have reached the level of Mutawatir.

You may be aware of the Hadith narrated by Imam Bukhari from **Ibn Abbas (may Allah be pleased with him)**. Once, the Prophet (peace be upon him) passed by two graves of Muslims and said:

> "These two are being punished, and they are not being punished for something major. One of them used to walk around with slander, and the other did not clean himself thoroughly from urine."

Then the Prophet (peace be upon him) asked for a palm frond, split it into two, and planted one on each grave. When the companions asked about it, the Prophet (peace be upon him) said:

> "Perhaps Allah will lighten their punishment as long as these fronds remain green" (Bukhari, Nasa'i).

This Hadith is in Sahih al-Bukhari. The Prophet (peace be upon him) clarified that these two individuals were Muslims. Yet they are being punished, and he prayed to Allah to lighten their punishment for as long as those two sticks remained green.

There are other Hadith like this. The Prophet (peace be upon him) says:

> "Purify yourselves from urine, for indeed the majority of the punishment of the grave is due to not purifying oneself from it" (Ibn Majah, Daraqutni).

There are numerous such Hadith. I will not prolong this. Let me mention one more: The Prophet (peace be upon him) passed by the graves of two polytheists who died during the Jahiliyyah and said:

> "If it were not for the fear that you would stop burying your dead and be terrified, I would have asked Allah to let you hear the punishment of the grave that I am hearing" (Bukhari, Muslim).

Even though it is clear through such Hadith that there is punishment in the grave for polytheists and some Muslims, these people have become bold enough to deny them entirely, refusing to accept them or acknowledge their meaning. They do so solely because of their philosophy that these are all Ahad Hadith and not Mutawatir.

What stance, then, will they take regarding the aforementioned Hadith of Abu Hurayrah (may Allah be pleased with him)? That is, the Hadith: "When any of you sits for the final Tashahhud, let him seek refuge in Allah from four things..."!
