## The Strong is the One Who Suppresses Anger

Narrated by Abu Hurairah (RA), the Prophet (PBUH) said:

> 'لَيْسَ الشَّدِيدُ بِالصُّرَعَةِ، إِنَّمَا الشَّدِيدُ الَّذِي يَكُفُّ نَفْسَهُ عِنْدَ الْغَضَبِ'
> (The strong person is not the one who wins in wrestling; rather, the strong person is the one who controls himself when angry.) (**Bukhari**, **Muslim**)

![](/assets/control-your-mind-20170325-3/25Mar17-15.jpg)

## Anger: A Vile Trait

Anger! It is a vile trait that invites many disasters, renders a person blind and deaf, and can lead to the destruction of individuals, families, societies, and even nations. Anyone can get angry and act out aggressively according to their ability. However, those who have the capacity to suppress their anger and forgive are very rare.

## Who Possesses True Strength?

The Prophet (PBUH) teaches that the truly strong person is the one who can suppress their anger and forgive even when they have the power to do whatever they wish to their opponent. Such a person can find success in life. One who quickly becomes a slave to anger and turns violent will have to face regret. Their life will be filled with tragedy.

## Anger in the Quran

In the Holy **Quran**, we find:

> 'وَالَّذِينَ يَجْتَنِبُونَ كَبَائِرَ الْإِثْمِ وَالْفَوَاحِشَ وَإِذَا مَا غَضِبُوا هُمْ يَغْفِرُونَ'
> (And those who avoid the major sins and immoralities, and when they are angry, they forgive.) (Ash-Shura: 37).

## The Experience of Prophet Yunus (AS)

When describing the incident where Prophet Yunus (AS) left his people in anger after his preaching did not yield the desired results, the Quran states:

> 'وَذَا النُّونِ إِذ ذَّهَبَ مُغَاضِبًا فَظَنَّ أَن لَّن نَّقْدِرَ عَلَيْهِ'
> (And [mention] the man of the fish, when he went off in anger and thought that We would not decree [anything] upon him...) (21:87).

It was not befitting for a Prophet to leave his people without the permission of Allah. Therefore, Allah subjected him to a trial. He had to jump into the sea after boarding a ship. A fish swallowed him, and after several days, it brought him to the shore. The reason he fell into such distress and trial was that he had left in anger.

## 'Do Not Become Angry'

Once, a man came to the Prophet (PBUH) and asked for advice. The Prophet (PBUH) replied, "Do not become angry." When the same request was repeated several times, the Prophet (PBUH) consistently gave the same advice: "Do not become angry."

## The Prophet's Sermon

One evening, while addressing the people, the Prophet (PBUH) said:

> "Human beings are created in different categories. Know that among them are those who are slow to anger and quick to cool down. There are those who are quick to anger and quick to cool down. There are those who are slow to anger and slow to cool down. These two balance each other out. But know this: there are also those who are quick to anger and slow to cool down. Therefore, the best among them are those who are slow to anger and quick to cool down. The worst are those who are quick to anger and slow to cool down."

## Suppress Your Anger

Uncontrolled anger is a vile trait that leads to serious consequences. Therefore, strive to suppress your anger. Be mindful. Save yourself from far-reaching repercussions.
