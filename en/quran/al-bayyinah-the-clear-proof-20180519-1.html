Al-Bayyinah (The Clear Proof)

അബ്ദുറഹ്മാന്‍ നാസിര്‍ അസ്സഅദി · 2018-05-19 · Quran & Tafsir

Chapter: 98

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ

لَمْ يَكُنِ الَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا مِنْ أَهْلِ الْكِتَابِ وَالْمُشْرِكِينَ مُنْفَكِّينَ حَتَّىٰ تَأْتِيَهُمُ الْبَيِّنَةُ (١) رَسُولٌ مِنَ اللَّهِ يَتْلُو صُحُفًا مُطَهَّرَةً (٢) فِيهَا كُتُبٌ قَيِّمَةٌ (٣) وَمَا تَفَرَّقَ الَّذِينَ أُوتُوا الْكِتَابَ إِلَّا مِنْ بَعْدِ مَا جَاءَتْهُمُ الْبَيِّنَةُ (٤) وَمَا أُمِرُوا إِلَّا لِيَعْبُدُوا اللَّهَ مُخْلِصِينَ لَهُ الدِّينَ حُنَفَاءَ وَيُقِيمُوا الصَّلَاةَ وَيُؤْتُوا الزَّكَاةَ ۚ وَذَٰلِكَ دِينُ الْقَيِّمَةِ (٥) إِنَّ الَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا مِنْ أَهْلِ الْكِتَابِ وَالْمُشْرِكِينَ فِي نَارِ جَهَنَّمَ خَالِدِينَ فِيهَا ۚ أُولَٰئِكَ هُمْ شَرُّ الْبَرِيَّةِ (٦) إِنَّ الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا وَعَمِلُوا الصَّالِحَاتِ أُولَئِكَ هُمْ خَيْرُ الْبَرِيَّةِ (٧‬) جَزَاؤُهُمْ عِنْدَ رَبِّهِمْ جَنَّاتُ عَدْنٍ تَجْرِي مِنْ تَحْتِهَا الْأَنْهَارُ خَالِدِينَ فِيهَا أَبَدًا ۖ رَضِيَ اللَّهُ عَنْهُمْ وَرَضُوا عَنْهُ ۚ ذَٰلِكَ لِمَنْ خَشِيَ رَبَّهُ (٨‬)

(1) The disbelievers among the People of the Book and the polytheists would not cease from their disbelief until the clear proof came to them. (2) That is, a Messenger from Allah, reciting purified scriptures. (3) Within them are upright writings. (4) And those who were given the Book did not differ except after the clear proof came to them. (5) And they were not commanded except to worship Allah, sincere in devotion to Him, inclining to truth, and to establish prayer and give zakat. And that is the upright religion. (6) Indeed, those who disbelieved among the People of the Book and the polytheists will be in the Fire of Hell, abiding therein eternally. Those are the worst of creatures. (7) Indeed, those who believed and did righteous deeds – those are the best of creatures. (8) Their reward with their Lord will be Gardens of Paradise beneath which rivers flow, wherein they will abide eternally, forever. Allah is pleased with them, and they are pleased with Him. That is for whoever fears his Lord.

Explaining what Al-Bayyinah is

  1. Allah says: (The disbelievers among the People of the Book would not cease) meaning, the Jews and Christians would not cease, (and the polytheists) from other communities would not cease, not only did they not abandon the misguidance they were upon, but they continued in that error, and the passing of time only increased their disbelief. (Until the clear proof came to them) meaning, a manifest and evident proof.
  2. 2,3). It then explains what that Al-Bayyinah (the clear proof) is. (A Messenger from Allah) that Messenger was sent to invite people to the truth. A divine book was revealed to that Messenger to bring them out of darkness into light, to purify them, and to teach them knowledge. Therefore, it continues, (a Messenger reciting purified scriptures). These are scriptures protected from devils, touched only by the pure, because they are exalted words. 'Within them' refers to those scriptures. (Upright writings) meaning, writings containing just commands and truthful information that lead to guidance and truth. When this proof comes, the matter becomes clear to any truth-seeker who has no other interests. Thus, those who are to perish may perish upon a clear proof, and those who are to live may live upon a clear proof.
  3. 4). If the People of the Book do not believe in this Messenger and do not submit, it is not their first act of defiance and misguidance. They have differed, disagreed, and formed factions. All this happened (after the clear proof came to them), and these proofs necessitated their unity and agreement. However, due to their contempt and insignificance, guidance only increased their misguidance, and insight only increased their blindness.
  4. 5). The foundation and essence of all divine books are one. (They were not commanded except to worship Allah, making their devotion sincere to Him) meaning, all acts of worship, both apparent and hidden, should be intended for the pleasure and proximity of Allah. (Inclining to truth) meaning, separating oneself from all ideologies contrary to the principles of this religion based on monotheism. Although establishing prayer and giving zakat are included in the command to 'worship Allah', they are mentioned separately due to their great importance and virtue. Moreover, performing these two is like performing all the duties of the religion. (And that is the upright religion) 'That' refers to monotheism and sincerity. It is called the upright religion because only this true religion leads to the Gardens of bliss. Other paths lead to Hell.
  5. 6). Allah then speaks about the reward for the disbelievers who turned away after the clear proofs came to them. (Indeed, those who disbelieved among the People of the Book and the polytheists will be in the Fire of Hell). The punishment of Hell has intensified upon them and encompassed them. (Abiding therein eternally) it will not be lightened for them. They will be in despair within it. (Those are the worst of creatures) because they understood the truth but rejected it, thereby losing both this world and the Hereafter.
  6. 7). (Indeed, those who believed and did righteous deeds – those are the best of creatures) because they understood Allah and worshipped Him, thus succeeding in both this life and the Hereafter.
  7. 8). (Their reward with their Lord will be Gardens of Paradise for eternal residence) meaning, Gardens of permanent abode, from which they do not depart. They seek no other goal beyond it. (Beneath which rivers flow, wherein they will abide eternally, forever. Allah is pleased with them, and they are pleased with Him). Allah is pleased with them because they performed deeds that earned His pleasure. And they will be pleased with the various honors and great rewards He has prepared for them. (That) that good reward, (is for whoever fears his Lord) for one who fears Allah, keeps away from sins, and fulfills his obligations.
