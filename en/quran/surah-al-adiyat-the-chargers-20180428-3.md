**Chapter: 100**

## بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ

وَالْعَادِيَاتِ ضَبْحًا (١) فَالْمُورِيَاتِ قَدْحًا (٢) فَالْمُغِيرَاتِ صُبْحًا (٣) فَأَثَرْنَ بِهِ نَقْعًا (٤) فَوَسَطْنَ بِهِ جَمْعًا (٥) إِنَّ الْإِنْسَانَ لِرَبِّهِ لَكَنُودٌ (٦) وَإِنَّهُ عَلَىٰ ذَٰلِكَ لَشَهِيدٌ (٧) وَإِنَّهُ لِحُبِّ الْخَيْرِ لَشَدِيدٌ (٨) أَفَلَا يَعْلَمُ إِذَا بُعْثِرَ مَا فِي الْقُبُورِ (٩) وَحُصِّلَ مَا فِي الصُّدُورِ (١٠) إِنَّ رَبَّهُمْ بِهِمْ يَوْمَئِذٍ لَخَبِيرٌ (١١)

> (1) By the chargers that pant, (2) and the strikers of sparks, (3) and the raiders at dawn, (4) stirring up clouds of dust, (5) and penetrating into the midst of the enemy gathering. (6) Indeed, mankind is ungrateful to their Lord. (7) And indeed, they are witnesses to that. (8) And indeed, they are intense in their love for wealth. (9) But do they not know when the contents of the graves are scattered, (10) and that which is in the hearts is made manifest? (11) Indeed, their Lord is, that day, fully acquainted with them.

## Allah swears by the horse

Allah swears by the horse because of the clear signs and manifest blessings that humans recognize in this animal. The oath is also taken because horses possess certain qualities not found in other animals.

1. "By the chargers that pant": When running at great speed and intensity, the horse pants. This is the sound produced from its chest due to the exertion of the run.
2. "The strikers of sparks": This happens when their hooves strike hard against rocks. Due to the friction while running, the hardness of the hooves causes sparks to fly.
3. "And the raiders at dawn": Raids were mostly conducted at dawn.
4. "Stirring up clouds of dust": Through their running and raiding, they stir up dust. "And penetrating into the midst of the enemy gathering": They reach the center of the enemy group that has come to attack, carrying the rider with them.

## Moving to the subject of the oath

6. "Indeed, mankind is ungrateful to their Lord": One who neglects the good deeds that should be done in gratitude to their Lord. It is human nature to have a mind that does not allow for the full fulfillment of obligations. Moreover, there is laziness. It is also their nature to neglect the physical and financial responsibilities they are required to perform. Only those whom Allah has guided and who have fully fulfilled their obligations are exempt from this.
7. "And indeed, they are witnesses to that": Humans know their own ingratitude and neglect. They bear witness to it themselves in a way that they cannot deny or refute. Here, "they are witnesses to that" could also refer to Allah. That is, humans are extremely ungrateful to their Lord, and Allah is a witness to that. When it is understood that Allah is the witness, there is a strong warning and caution for those who show ingratitude to Him.
8. "Indeed, they" (mankind) "are intense in their love for wealth": They love wealth so much that they end up abandoning their mandatory obligations. Their own interests are more important to them than the pleasure of their Lord. All this happens because their focus is limited to this world and they are heedless of the Hereafter.

## The following verses urge us to fear the Day of Reckoning (the Hereafter)

9. "But do they not know": That is, does this person not understand? "When the contents of the graves are scattered": When Allah brings the dead out of their graves to be resurrected and gathered.
10. "And that which is in the hearts is made manifest": What humans have hidden in their hearts, whether good or evil, will be revealed; secrets will be made public, everything hidden will be exposed, and the results of their actions will appear on their faces.
11. "Indeed, their Lord is, that day, fully acquainted with them": He knows all the actions of humans—the visible and the hidden, the clear and the obscure—and will reward them accordingly. The specific mention of "that day" for Allah, who knows everything at all times, is to emphasize that Allah rewards based on His perfect knowledge of their deeds.
