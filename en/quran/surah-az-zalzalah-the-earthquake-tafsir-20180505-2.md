# Chapter: 99

## بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ

إِذَا زُلْزِلَتِ الْأَرْضُ زِلْزَالَهَا (١) وَأَخْرَجَتِ الْأَرْضُ أَثْقَالَهَا (٢) وَقَالَ الْإِنْسَانُ مَا لَهَا (٣) يَوْمَئِذٍ تُحَدِّثُ أَخْبَارَهَا (٤) بِأَنَّ رَبَّكَ أَوْحَىٰ لَهَا (٥) يَوْمَئِذٍ يَصْدُرُ النَّاسُ أَشْتَاتًا لِيُرَوْا أَعْمَالَهُمْ (٦) فَمَنْ يَعْمَلْ مِثْقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ خَيْرًا يَرَهُ (7) وَمَنْ يَعْمَلْ مِثْقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ شَرًّا يَرَهُ (8)

(1) When the earth is shaken with its final, violent shaking. (2) And the earth brings forth its burdens. (3) And man says, "What is the matter with it?" (4) That Day, it will report its news, (5) because your Lord has inspired it. (6) That Day, the people will depart in separate groups to be shown their deeds. (7) So whoever does an atom's weight of good will see it. (8) And whoever does an atom's weight of evil will see it.

## Events of the Day of Resurrection

**1, 2)** Allah describes here the events that will take place on the Day of Resurrection. The earth will tremble and shake so violently that the buildings and mountains upon it will collapse. The mountains will be pulverized, the hills will be leveled, and the earth will become a flat, smooth plain without any pits or mounds. "And the earth brings forth its burdens" means it will cast out the dead and the treasures hidden within it.

**3)** "And man says"—upon seeing the terrifying events that have befallen the earth, he will ask, "What is the matter with it?"

**4, 5)** "That Day, it will report its news"—the earth will speak. "Its news" refers to the good and evil deeds that creatures performed upon its surface. It means that the earth will be among those who bear witness to the actions of the servants. It is able to do this because "your Lord has inspired it." Allah will command the earth to reveal the deeds that took place upon it, and it will not disobey that command.

**6)** "That Day, the people will depart"—at the time of resurrection, "in separate groups," so that "they may be shown their deeds"—the good and evil they performed, along with their full recompense.

**7, 8)** "So whoever does an atom's weight of good will see it. And whoever does an atom's weight of evil will see it"—this encompasses all good and evil deeds. An atom's weight is the most insignificant measure of things; if even that is rewarded, then the state of anything greater than that will surely be even more significant. Allah says:

> "The Day every soul will find what it has done of good present [before it] and what it has done of evil, it will wish that between itself and that [evil] was a great distance." (Quran 3:30).

Elsewhere, Allah says:

> "And they will find what they did present [before them]." (18:49)
