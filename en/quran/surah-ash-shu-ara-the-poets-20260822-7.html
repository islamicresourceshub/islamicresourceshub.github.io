Surah Ash-Shu'ara: The Poets

Surah Ash-Shu'ara

The Poets

(Revealed in Makkah)

Abdur Rahman ibn Nasir as-Sa'di Translation: Haris bin Salim Phone: 95444 92089

  1. Indeed, I am to you a trustworthy messenger.
  2. So fear Allah and obey me.

إِنِّي لَكُمْ رَسُولٌ أَمِينٌ (143) فَاتَّقُوا اللَّهَ وَأَطِيعُونِ (144)

143, 144) «Indeed, I am a messenger to you» sent by Allah, your Lord, for a purpose. Accept His mercy and submit to it. «Trustworthy»—because you know that I am trustworthy, you can believe in me and in the message I have brought.

  1. And I do not ask you for it any payment. My payment is only from the Lord of the worlds.

وَمَا أَسْأَلُكُمْ عَلَيْهِ مِنْ أَجْرٍ إِنْ أَجْرِيَ إِلَّا عَلَى رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ (145)

145) «And I do not ask you for any payment for this»—if I did, you could say that the only reason you are stopping us from following you is because you desire to take our wealth. «My payment is only from the Lord of the worlds»—I do not seek any reward from anyone other than Him.

  1. Will you be left in what is here, secure?
  2. Within gardens and springs?
  3. And crops and palm trees with soft, layered fruit?

أَتُتْرَكُونَ فِي مَا هَاهُنَا آمِنِينَ (146) فِي جَنَّاتٍ وَعُيُونٍ (147) وَزُرُوعٍ وَنَخْلٍ طَلْعُهَا هَضِيمٌ (148)

146-148) «Will you be left in what is here, secure? Within gardens and springs, and crops and palm trees with soft, layered fruit?» Do you think you will be left alone? Do you think these blessings were given to you for no reason, and that you have no purpose other than to enjoy physical pleasures like animals? Do you think you do not need to follow commands and prohibitions? Furthermore, you are using all these blessings to act against Allah.

Quranic Insight - Tafsir as-Sa'di

  1. And you carve out of the mountains, homes, with skill. وَتَنْحِتُونَ مِنَ الْجِبَالِ بُيُوتًا فَارِهِينَ ۝١٤٩

149) «And you carve out of the mountains, homes, with skill»—carving homes into the mountains with great expertise. This means your cleverness and skill have reached the point where you build homes in hard, solid mountains.

  1. So fear Allah and obey me. فَاتَّقُوا اللَّهَ وَأَطِيعُونِ ۝١٥٠

  2. And do not obey the order of the transgressors. وَلَا تُطِيعُوا أَمْرَ الْمُسْرِفِينَ ۝١٥١

150-151) «So fear Allah and obey me. And do not obey the order of the transgressors»—those who have exceeded the limits.

  1. Who cause corruption in the land and do not reform. الَّذِينَ يُفْسِدُونَ فِي الْأَرْضِ وَلَا يُصْلِحُونَ ۝١٥٢

152) «Who cause corruption in the land and do not reform»—those who commit sins, cause corruption on earth, and keep inviting people toward it. They bring no good, only corruption. This is the worst attitude, for it is pure evil.

Prophet Salih (peace be upon him) told them not to be deceived by those who had already decided to oppose their prophet and were busy inviting people to the path of misguidance. It is regarding them that Allah said: وَكَانَ فِي الْمَدِينَةِ تِسْعَةُ رَهْطٍ يُفْسِدُونَ فِي الْأَرْضِ وَلَا يُصْلِحُونَ 'And there were nine people in the city who caused corruption in the land and did not reform' (27:48)

However, this prohibition and advice were of no use. They said to Prophet Salih (peace be upon him):

  1. They said, "You are only one of those affected by magic." قَالُونَ إِنَّمَا أَنتَ مِنَ الْمُسَحَّرِينَ ۝١٥٣

153) «They said, "You are only one of those affected by magic"»—you have been bewitched and are saying things that have no meaning.
