Universal Calls in the Quran 19

Truth, Falsehood, and Human Free Will

Ijas Bin Ismail, Muscat Contact: 00968-98242217

One of the most profound questions humanity has ever faced is why one should believe in God. The Lord of the universe did not create humans and leave them to wander aimlessly; rather, He has shown them the right path through prophets and divine scriptures. Whether or not to choose that path is a matter of human freedom. In the final part of Surah Yunus, Allah instructs the Prophet to address the people as follows:

قُلْ يَا أَيُّهَا النَّاسُ قَدْ جَاءَكُمُ الْحَقُّ مِن رَّبِّكُمْ فَمَنِ اهْتَدَىٰ فَإِنَّمَا يَهْتَدِي لِنَفْسِهِ وَمَن ضَلَّ فَإِنَّمَا يَضِلُّ عَلَيْهَا وَمَا أَنَا عَلَيْكُم بِوَكِيلٍ

"Say: O mankind, the truth has come to you from your Lord. So whoever is guided is only guided for the benefit of his soul, and whoever goes astray only goes astray against it. And I am not a manager over you" (Quran 10:108).

This verse presents four key lessons regarding human freedom and personal responsibility:

The Truth Has Been Revealed This is a declaration that «Al-Haqq»—the Truth—has arrived. Through the Quran, Allah has clearly laid out the true purpose of a sacred life before humanity. On the Day of Judgment, no human will be able to offer excuses such as "we did not know what the truth was" or "no one told us." The Creator has already provided clear evidence that distinguishes truth from falsehood to all of mankind.

The Benefit of Guidance is Personal This line corrects a major human misconception. Many feel that we worship God to provide Him with some benefit. However, Allah states that if you choose the path of guidance, you are the sole beneficiary of that choice. By worshipping the Creator, humans cannot add anything to the majesty of God. By following divine commands such as prayer, charity, and honesty, it is the human who gains peace of mind in this world and eternal success in the hereafter.

The Harm of Going Astray is Personal If a person decides to deny the truth and choose a path of transgression, they are not defeating God or the Prophet; rather, they are harming their own soul. If a patient refuses to take the medicine prescribed by a doctor, the tragedy is suffered by the patient, not the doctor. Similarly, those who reject divine guidance and choose the path of transgression are opening the way to their own destruction.

The Prophet is Not a Guardian The declaration "I am not a manager over you" is highly significant. Allah has not tasked anyone with forcing people into religion or imposing it upon them. The obligation of the Prophet and the believers is merely to convey the truth to the people with love and reason. Whether or not to accept it is the decision of each individual. This verse clarifies that faith is not something to be imposed, but something to be accepted in the heart through true discernment.

Life is the sum of constant choices. Both the right path and the wrong path are before us. If we show the wisdom to acknowledge the Creator and live in submission to Him, it is our own success. Otherwise, the loss will be ours alone.

We have seen how the Quran presents to the world various topics such as the origin of man, his purpose, duties toward the Creator and fellow creations, life's trials, righteousness and wickedness, and resurrection and judgment.

May Allah help us all to reflect upon and incorporate these universal messages, which view all of humanity as one, into our lives.

The address "Ya Ayyuhan-Nas" appears 20 times in the Quran. It also appears in the 16th verse of Surah An-Naml. However, that verse was not included in this series. This is because that call was made by Prophet Sulaiman (peace be upon him) to his people. The subject matter there concerns him telling his people about the special blessings Allah had bestowed upon him. Unlike the universal pieces of advice we discussed, that verse refers to a specific historical context and a miracle granted to a prophet; therefore, it was omitted to maintain the consistency of this series.
