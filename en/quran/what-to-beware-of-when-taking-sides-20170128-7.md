> "Close friends, that Day, will be enemies to each other, except for the righteous." (**Quran** 43:67).

![](/assets/what-to-beware-of-when-taking-sides-20170128-7/28Jan17-13.jpg)

## Mutual Relationships and Taking Sides

The majority of human beings form mutual bonds of love and take sides based on various factors. There are those who build relationships for worldly gains, or simply to support their own personal interests and opinions. Ready to do or spend anything for the sake of such alliances, they often fail to consider or care about truth and justice.

## On the Side of Truth and Justice

A believer, however, is commanded to stand firmly on the side of truth and justice. One must strive to align with the camp of justice and goodness, even if it goes against one's own parents, and constantly pray to the Almighty for the grace to do so. **Allah** says:

> "O you who have believed, be persistently standing firm in justice, witnesses for Allah, even if it be against yourselves or parents and relatives. Whether one is rich or poor, Allah is more worthy of both. So follow not [personal] inclination, lest you not be just. And if you distort [your testimony] or refuse [to give it], then indeed Allah is ever, of what you do, Aware." (4:135).

## Commitment to Truth and Goodness

A believer's primary commitment must be to truth and goodness. It is on this foundation that closeness or distance to individuals and groups should be established. Otherwise, the Quran warns, one will eventually have to regret such relationships.

## Gentle Reminders with Love

Along with having the courage to support truth and goodness, it is also a believer's duty to gently and lovingly correct those who go astray. When the truth and evidence have been made clear, if someone stubbornly rejects them, a believer should not turn a blind eye to the truth just to avoid earning a friend's displeasure. The firm resolve of a believer must be that even if a friend is displeased, they cannot afford to earn the displeasure of Allah, the Most Merciful.

## The Perfection of Faith

Explaining the perfection of *Iman* (faith), the **Prophet** (peace be upon him) said:

> "Whoever loves for the sake of Allah, hates for the sake of Allah, gives for the sake of Allah, and withholds for the sake of Allah has perfected **Iman** (faith)." (**Abu Dawud**).

## Love and Hatred for Allah's Sake

The Prophet (peace be upon him) teaches that loving Allah and what He loves, while detesting everything that Allah dislikes, is a fundamental requirement of faith.

## Hope for an Exemplary Community

If leaders, followers, and friends move forward with this teaching firmly in their hearts, we can hope for the rise of an exemplary community committed to truth and justice. Otherwise, let such people beware of the Quranic warning of disgrace and despair that awaits them before Allah's tribunal in the Hereafter:

> "When those who were followed disown those who followed them, and they see the punishment, and all relations between them are severed. And those who followed will say, 'If only we had another turn [in worldly life] so we could disown them as they have disowned us.' Thus will Allah show them their deeds as regrets for them. And they will never emerge from the Fire." (2:166,167).
