# 'Afkarun Ukhrawiyya' (Thoughts on the Afterlife): 6

![](/assets/a-good-death-20180428-5/28Apr18-08.jpg)

## An individual who died long ago

An individual who died long ago
You claim in your logic that he is a saint
Yet you abandon the Messenger of Allah
And interfere in the authority of the Lord
Do not do this, my dear, do not say such things
No one knows what the Lord has decreed for another

## The Companion known as Uthman ibn Maz'un

**Uthman ibn Maz'un** was a Companion
The first Companion to pass away in Medina
A noble soul who was able to see the Prophet
A brave man who stood in the ranks of the path of Islam

## When the Ansar shared with the Muhajir

When the **Ansar** shared with the **Muhajir**
He arrived at the home of Umm al-Ala
There he embraced death
And the noble Messenger came to visit him

## Umm al-Ala says these words

> 'Allah has honored you
> And I bear witness to that'

She declares Uthman to be successful
The noble Messenger then speaks with displeasure:

> 'Who has given you this information?'

> 'O beloved Prophet, does he not deserve it?
> If not him, then who is worthy of honor?'

Immediately, the **Mustafa Prophet** says this:

> 'I, the Prophet, do not know how it will be,
> I hope for goodness for him
> But only what Allah has decreed will come to pass.'

## Since then, regarding anyone

Since then, regarding anyone
I have not passed judgment, says that noble lady
This is information provided by many books
You can see it in **Bukhari** as well
Paradise and Hell are granted by Allah
We do not know for whom they are intended
Everything will arrive according to the decree
Whether in Paradise or in Hell
Some people die while uttering the Kalima
Yet death is a hope for a beautiful end
Likewise is the martyr
If sincere, Paradise is certain for them

## Some who die other than in battle

- Some who die other than in battle are also martyrs, as the Prophet said
- An individual who dies of cholera, the significance is that he is a martyr
- If one's end is by drowning, the fact is that he too is a martyr
- If one is struck by paralysis and dies from it, that is martyrdom
- One who dies of a stomach ailment is also a martyr, according to the Messenger's words
- One who dies buried under a collapsed building is also among them
- One who dies in the pain of childbirth is also included in the ranks of martyrs
- Those who die from fire burns are also among this group

This prophetic tradition is found in the **Muwatta**
And **Malik** is its author

- One who dies while doing good deeds, that death is also blessed, according to the words
- One who fought to protect the honor of their family
- One who fought to protect their wealth
- One who fought in the path of the faith

All of them have reached the rank of martyr

## Therefore, do not judge either of these in anyone

Therefore, all these deaths are beautiful
We can always think of this as the reason
But these are not evidence
Do not judge that they are definitely destined for Paradise
And if none of these are seen
It is not proof that they are destined for Hell!
Therefore, do not judge either of these in anyone
That knowledge belongs to the Lord, the Owner

## Must die in the path of the religion

I must die in the path of the religion, O Allah
You alone are the support for that, O Allah
Grant light in the grave, O Allah
Grant me Jannatul Firdaus, O Allah
