## Abdullah: The Skilled Carpenter
Once upon a time, in a village, there lived a skilled carpenter named **Abdullah**. He worked under a wealthy employer. Most people in the village believed that a house construction was only truly complete if Abdullah had worked on it. He performed his duties with such precision, sincerity, and meticulousness that all the villagers held him in high regard.

## The Decision to Retire
Years passed by. Abdullah's agility and health began to decline slowly. One holiday, while eating with his wife and children, he revealed something he had been contemplating for days: he had decided to retire from his job. His family agreed with his decision. After all, having worked hard for so long while balancing various responsibilities, Abdullah truly deserved a rest. Therefore, his family had no objections.

## The Employer's Request
The next day, Abdullah spoke to his employer about his intention to retire. However, the employer did not agree. He said, "You must not retire until you have completed the construction of one last house that I intend to build."

Reluctantly, Abdullah agreed to take on this final project, provided that the employer guaranteed it would be the last task of his career.

## Negligence in the Final Job
Distracted by thoughts of his upcoming retirement, Abdullah lacked the motivation to show his usual focus and sincerity toward the large house his employer had assigned him. He adopted an attitude of, "The others will have to work without me from now on; they can pay attention if they want to, this is all I can manage."

## An Ordinary House
Abdullah had only one thought: to finish the assigned work as quickly as possible. Consequently, he neglected many important details. In any case, the house was completed within the scheduled days. Abdullah made no effort to give the house any special character, resulting in a very ordinary structure. Usually, every new house he built would have some unique feature or charm.

## The Employer's Gift
The next day, the employer arrived to see the house. He took the keys from Abdullah, called together all the workers and others present, and with great respect, handed the keys of the new house to Abdullah. He said, "I would like to give this house as a gift to Abdullah, who has worked with me so sincerely for all these years."

## Abdullah's Realization
Everyone was overjoyed. It was then that Abdullah realized the loss caused by his lack of sincerity in his final job. He understood the necessity of maintaining sincerity, focus, and precision in every task one performs.

## The Moral
My friends, if we want to receive the due reward for all the good deeds we perform in this world in the **Hereafter**, we must do them with good intentions, sincerity, and a desire for the reward from the Almighty. That is the lesson we must learn from Abdullah's story.
