Grown-up Children

ഇബ്‌നു അലി എടത്തനാട്ടുകര · 2017-01-21 · Stories & Literature

When the Officer Father Returned Home

When the officer father returned home, he found his son ready to leave the house. The cause was a disagreement with his mother. The father sought ways to mediate, but the son was in no mood to relent. The boy is no ordinary child; he is only in the second grade! Although the first attempt failed, the father did not stop his efforts at reconciliation. He called his wife and questioned her. The serious matter at hand was that the mother, a teacher, had taken the television remote away from the son!

By sitting both parties down together, the teacher and the officer compromised in front of the grown-up school student, and somehow managed to avert the plan to run away from home for the time being.

A Senior Officer's Memory

I am reminded of something another senior officer once said. He had to reprimand his daughter, who was studying for her degree, for some reason. As soon as she heard it, she cried and wailed, went into her room, and bolted the door from the inside. After a long time, the mother, not seeing her daughter outside, knocked and called out. There was no sound from inside. Panic and distress set in... the mother's sobbing grew into wailing and screaming. Even after other family members joined in calling out and crying aloud, there was no response or movement from within. Just as they were about to break down the door after the collective weeping, the daughter opened it and stepped out. The whole family stood frozen, not knowing what to say or do. A display of affection took place then that could not be described as crying or laughing, joy or sorrow. The father begged for forgiveness as if he were kneeling at his own daughter's feet.

Even now, I carry the helplessness of that officer who said that no matter what nonsense his children say or do, he will not utter a single word in response.

What Has Happened to Our Children?

What has happened to our children? Is it that they lack the self-confidence we gained in the past, living in joint families under the loving shade of grandmothers and grandfathers, among a troop of siblings and cousins, giving and taking, fighting and getting beaten? Or is it that they have been confined to nuclear families and never learned what self-confidence is? Could it be that they have started to prefer the prison life within the high walls we built to keep out the outside world and the gaze of neighbors? Or is it that they have failed to realize that there is a living life beyond video games, Facebook, WhatsApp, Twitter, and chatting?!

Have they realized that they can get everything they want from us in an instant? Have they failed to realize that there is a world beyond message sharing, trolling, short stories, movies, and serials? Is it because they think the idea that an eternal life awaits beyond this one is just a fairy tale?

All of this serves as a reminder—and should trouble us—that we need to think deeply and awaken to the causes and the remedies.

"And the worldly life is not but amusement and diversion; but the home of the Hereafter is best for those who fear Allah, so will you not then reason?" (Quran 6:32).

"Then did you think that We created you uselessly and that to Us you would not be returned?" (Quran 23:115).
