## A friend's call amidst the hustle of work

I received a call from a friend while I was caught up in the middle of work. He asked if I had read the newspaper, and when I replied that I hadn't, he said he had sent it to my WhatsApp. He sounded deeply distressed. A while later, he came to see me in person. I still hadn't read it. He shared a news report from that day’s paper about an asthmatic mother and her five-year-old heart patient daughter living a life of misery in a makeshift house covered with plastic sheets. In that child, he saw his own daughter. He spoke in a low, firm voice, insisting that we must do something immediately and that he had already set aside a four-digit sum for the cause.

He was an accountant by profession, and he insisted that since there were two other friends willing to **help**, we should find that family and deliver the aid that very day. I suggested that we shouldn't just divert the money he had set aside, as small, one-time contributions would only provide temporary relief. I proposed that we think of a permanent solution for the family's suffering, and he left to reflect on this.

That night, I called my friend to inform him that I had discussed the matter with another friend who is a social worker, and that we would plan accordingly. He kept pestering me, frequently asking for updates on the progress.

## A Sunday visit to that thatched hut with more friends

On a free Sunday, we reached that thatched hut with a larger group of friends. The reality was even more painful than what we had read. The walls and roof were made of thin, fragile plastic sheets that could be torn by even a small lizard and blown away by a light breeze. Right there, our friend who is a **social worker** announced that he would cover the daughter's education expenses and the family's living costs. We held a consultation on the spot. We met with the local community leaders and mosque officials, discussed the situation, and gave them confidence. Drawing from past experiences, we explained that if we work together, we can achieve our goal. We discussed in detail how to begin and other logistics. We reminded everyone that if we turn away or close our eyes to this, we would have to answer to the Creator. When we returned, our hearts were filled with a sense of relief and hope. The accountant friend, in particular, felt a profound sense of peace.

## After discussions at the local mosque

Following the discussions at the local mosque, we received a call. The villagers had called for a meeting and requested that we attend and facilitate it. We arrived with even more friends. Everyone spoke, and a committee was formed. A friend who is an engineer prepared and presented an estimate in no time. Pledges of money, stones, cement, sand, and physical labor poured in from the audience. We also pledged what we could. The accountant friend increased his contribution from the initial four-digit sum to a six-digit one. It was an indescribable joy for everyone!

## A bank account was opened

A **bank account** was opened. The house construction is nearing completion. We ask for your prayers.

## If we set our minds to it, we can achieve much

If we set our minds to it, we can achieve many things. There are many good hearts waiting to cooperate in goodness, regardless of the boundaries of religion or political affiliation. Those who walk the path of goodness will surely receive the mercy of the Creator. It only takes one heart to take the initiative.

> "But seek, through that which Allah has given you, the home of the Hereafter; and [yet], do not forget your share of the world. And do good as Allah has done good to you. And desire not corruption in the land. Indeed, Allah does not like corrupters." (**Quran**: 28:77)
