## Hassan in Qura Village

Once upon a time, in a village called Qura, lived a farmer named **Hassan**. Hassan was very dear to the villagers and his family. This was because Hassan was always ready to help anyone who approached him without any hesitation. He never hesitated to offer whatever help he could to others. Hassan had become such a person because he had deeply understood the teachings of **Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him)** about the characteristics of a true Muslim.

## Parenting Style

Hassan had three sons: Qasim, Farooq, and Abdullah. Qasim was the eldest. Farooq and Abdullah were twins. Hassan was determined that his sons should grow up to be very good individuals, so he would often tell them stories from the **Quran** and tales of the prophets and their companions. The three boys listened with great interest, understanding everything, and excelled in their character compared to other children in the village.

## The Father's Test

One day, Hassan called his three sons and gave each of them a candy. He then said:

> "My sons, you must all eat this candy where no one can see you. Whoever eats this without anyone seeing them will receive a gift from me."

## The Sons' Actions

As soon as he received the candy, Abdullah quietly went inside the house and then into the hall. Oh, his mother was there! He slowly hid behind the door. When his mother went into the kitchen, he quietly climbed the stairs to the upper floor. He then crawled under his father's desk and settled there. Assured that no one was paying attention to him and that no one had seen him come there, he couldn't contain his joy. He was certain that today, he would be the winner in front of his father. He confirmed this to himself. He slowly unwrapped the candy and ate it.

Farooq, on the other hand, stood holding the candy in his hand without moving from his spot. He remained there until both Qasim and his father went inside talking. Once he was sure they had both gone inside, he quickly ran to the auto-rickshaw parked in the courtyard of their neighbor, Abdulla. Making sure no one was watching him, he ate the candy.

However, Qasim held the candy in his hand and walked around inside and in the courtyard. But he couldn't be sure that no one was watching him. No matter where he hid or how secluded a spot he found, even if he saw no one around, he thought about how Allah was watching from above. He became certain that he could not eat it without Allah seeing, no matter which corner of the world he went to. His father had also firmly stated that the gift would only be given to those who ate it unseen. He did not eat the candy and kept it in his hand.

## Qasim's Victory

After a while, Hassan called his sons and asked them what they had done. Abdullah excitedly narrated his adventure. When he mentioned hiding behind the door when he saw his mother, his mother exclaimed, "Wow... you're amazing!"

Farooq also described what he had done. Qasim remained silent. His father asked Qasim, "What did you do, Qasim?"

Qasim showed his empty hand. The candy was still in his hand! Seeing this, Farooq burst out laughing. "This elder brother couldn't even eat one candy unseen! How unfortunate!"

His father asked, "What happened, Qasim?"

He replied, "No matter where I hid to eat it, Allah would see me. Therefore, I could not eat it unseen."

His father and mother were very happy. Farooq and Abdullah stood stunned, saddened by the fact that they had lost the prize.

Children, even if we don't see, Allah sees us. Nothing in this universe happens without His knowledge or sight. Therefore, let us live our lives doing only good deeds.
