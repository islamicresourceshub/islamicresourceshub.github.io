If you pray five times a day,
There are countless benefits to gain.
If you keep Allah in your heart,
You can walk through life without stumbling.

To attain purity of the heart,
Prayer is the finest medicine.
If devotion resides within,
You will surely perform many good deeds.

If you perform many good deeds,
The Lord will grant you Paradise.
If you refrain from all evil,
You can earn the pleasure of the Rabb.

When performing righteous deeds,
What a sense of enthusiasm fills the soul!
To love our fellow beings,
We must be kind and compassionate.

If you remember all that you have learned,
You can climb the steps of success one by one.
If you possess humility,
Success is surely guaranteed.
