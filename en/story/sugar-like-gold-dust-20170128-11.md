Just as one would pour gold dust into a bowl, with such care were those sugar grains poured into a container. Their son had brought the sugar, wrapped in paper, from the nearby shop, to offer black tea to their guest.

## The Special Guest, a Social Worker Friend

The organization he led was covering the educational expenses of hundreds of children. The friend had come to inquire about the family details of one such child. The sight that greeted him upon arrival angered him. The child stood near the hut, clad only in a single towel, not attending class. The friend reprimanded the child for not going to class.

The mother, hearing the noise, came out and tearfully explained that the child wasn't going to class because they had been expelled for not paying the fees. She added that she would earn money by husking areca nuts at the neighboring landlord's house to pay the fees, and that her son had gone to the next house to husk areca nuts. The fees for the government school were a very small amount.

The friend, who had reprimanded the child, felt saddened. At that moment, the household couldn't even afford a glass of tea for the guest. The child ran off with one of the notes given by the friend, to buy sugar from the nearby shop. It was these very sugar grains that were now being prepared to be stored in a jar with more care than gold dust. Such extreme meticulousness – not a single grain of sugar should be lost, not wasted!

## The Food Items Wasted by Many of Us

How much food is wasted in many of our homes! And how much is destroyed without even being cooked! How many dishes are needlessly ruined every single day. If it's a wedding or a feast, it's even more so.

In our journey through life, there are many around us who struggle for a single meal. One only needs to open their inner eye to see them.

When you waste food, when you buy more than you need, when you throw unfinished or unused leftovers into the waste bin, remember that there are countless others around us waiting to partake in even a small portion of it.

## Those Who **Waste** Should Heed These Verses from the Holy **Quran**

> "...And do not squander [your wealth] wastefully. Indeed, the wasteful are brothers of the devils, and ever has Satan been to his Lord ungrateful." (Al-Isra: 26,27).

> "Eat and drink, but do not be wasteful. Indeed, **Allah** does not like the wasteful." (Al-A'raf: 31).
