The Story of the Dug Treasure

Long ago, in a village, a young man began digging in a spot, believing there was treasure buried there. On the first day, a large crowd gathered to witness him digging, and many even came to help. Two days passed, and there was no sign of treasure. The number of people helping began to dwindle. Three days, four days passed—still no treasure! Almost everyone who had joined him left. Even his wife, whom he thought would stay with him until the end, said, "You are mad. I am going my own way."

On the sixth day, in a final attempt, he dug. Before him lay only a pit. No treasure! Frustrated, he ended his endeavor. The villagers saw him as nothing more than a madman. Months later, a traveler passing through saw this massive pit and inquired about it. Upon learning the story, he conducted some checks and dug for one more day—the seventh day. Nothing. But when he dug on the eighth day, he found the great treasure.

Persist Until the Goal is Achieved

This story teaches us that more important than how much we have done is the commitment to keep working until the goal is achieved.

The Holy Quran teaches us that you must continue to worship your Lord until death comes to you.

Our children must be those who reach their goals through this consistency in action—both in this world and the hereafter.

Discouraging Words Children Hear

But often, are they not hearing only words of discouragement? Let us think sincerely.

"You will never amount to anything..." is the curse they hear from home.

Family members and neighbors label them a "lost generation." Visual media, audio media, newspapers, teachers... everyone sees children and the new generation only as culprits who are unwilling to improve.

Can You Get Sweet Fritters from Chapati Dough?

Have we seriously considered the other side of this? If you take dough for chapati, knead it, roll it out, and put it on the stove, and then say, "Oh! I intended to make sweet fritters (neyyappam)," will you get sweet fritters from chapati dough? No, certainly not! Knowing this, if we beat that chapati from all sides and pour jaggery and oil over it, will it become a sweet fritter? No!

The Crisis of Parents in Kerala

In truth, this is the crisis many parents in Kerala face. They are baking chapatis but expecting sweet fritters. They desire for their children to walk the path of goodness, but they do not prepare the way for it. Islam provides the methods for the wholesome growth of children.

It Must Start from Conception

When Prophet Ibrahim (AS) was distressed for many years without children, his prayer was:

"My Lord! Grant me a righteous child."

When desiring children, prayer and hope are essential. Modern science suggests that a mother's thoughts during pregnancy are transferred to the child. A mother's movements and behavior should be considered with this in mind. Will the recitation of the Quran and good thoughts not influence the child growing in the womb? After birth, the child learns much of what they see and hear from their first school: their mother and father. Are parents able to be role models for them?

The Prophet's Model of Love

Are we taking lessons from the life of the Prophet (PBUH), who showed us the sublime model of expressing love to young children?

  • A father who stands up to kiss his daughter Fatimah when she enters the house and leads her inside.
  • A grandfather who plays the role of a camel for his grandsons Hasan (RA) and Husayn (RA).
  • An Imam who carries a child while performing prayer.
  • A preacher who caresses a child while delivering the sermon.
  • A compassionate man who publicly kisses his grandchildren and prays for them, expressing his love for them without words.

He taught us lessons that are beneficial.

Misguidance Before Teenagers

We must reflect. There are many paths before teenagers that lead them astray:

  • Alcohol, drugs, movies, bad company.
  • Mobile phones, the internet, clubs, music...
  • And so on.

It is the Duty of Guardians to Guide

Do not let them run wild, but do not imprison them either. Impart religious consciousness. Show them the paths to it. We can trust children who are aware that the ultimate goal of life is not just to get a high-paying job, but to achieve success after death, and that it is impossible to say, see, hear, or do anything without the Creator's knowledge. There is no doubt that through them, we will find peace and joy for our eyes.

The Mission of the Wisdom Students Wing

Wisdom Students Wing is performing the noble task of guiding the student world toward the shores of goodness without casting them aside through criticism, and molding them into a generation with a sense of values.

For the Foam of Evil and the Essence of Truth

Amidst the flash floods of evil, when the world of vices—buoyed by the accompaniment of noisy advertisements—beckons our children, let us hold our children close to help them discover the truth that lies deep beneath the water, realizing that the foam and bubbles will burst in a split second.
