# My Pen

**Nada Jabeen C. P.**

![An image of a pen and an inkpot on a table](/assets/pen-ink.jpg)

For the words left unsaid,
silence is never the end,
they are reborn somewhere
as letters.
Along the paths traveled,
the people I met,
the faces etched in my heart,
and the words that remained as wounds,
all return one day
to the tip of my pen.
The silence of certain nights
turns into poetry at dawn!
Some losses
return as characters
in a story.
When pain finds words,
its burden lightens.
When moth-eaten memories
find letters,
they cease to be wounds.
Slowly, life experiences
take the form of lines.
The lines gently
give color to dreams.
In the end, though it is only
letters that remain
on the paper,
I know that hidden between them
is the journey
of a silent life.
