അബൂ ഫായിദ · 2018-05-19 · Stories & Literature

The Crescent Moon of Ramadan in the Sky

The crescent moon of Ramadan has appeared on the horizon of the sky.

The days of arrival are near, and joy rises like waves.

It is the month where virtues blossom, the month where good deeds multiply.

It is the month filled with devotion, the month in which the Quran was revealed.

The world of believers welcomes it, saying 'Ahlan Ramadan' — welcome Ramadan.

Hearts seeking solace say, 'Ahlan Ramadan' — welcome Ramadan.

Prayer to the Lord

O Rabb — the Ocean of Mercy, with weeping eyes,

We stand before You, seeking to attain the blessings of Ramadan.

We see the gate called Rayyan before our eyes.

O Dayyan — the Supreme Judge, will You not open it for us?

O Khallaq — the Creator, grant us the reward of fasting; that is our plea.

There is none other than You to grant us help for this.

May You illuminate our hearts with Taqwa — God-consciousness.

Grant us support to understand the essence of the Quran.

O Allah, grant us a place in Jannatul Firdaus.

O Allah, You alone are our protection from the fire of Hell.
