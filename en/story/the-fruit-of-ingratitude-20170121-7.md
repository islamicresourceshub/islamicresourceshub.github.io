## A Generous Farmer

Long ago, in a village, there lived a generous farmer. He was always ready to help the poor who came to him in any way he could. The Almighty **Allah** had blessed him with a vast, fertile, and abundant garden. It was a beautiful sight to behold, filled with various vegetables and fruits like grapes, apples, oranges, and mangoes, each with its own unique color and taste. Every harvest season, he would set aside a portion for the poor. The needy in the area, knowing this, would come to his garden to collect fruits and vegetables.

Years passed in great happiness. The people were very grateful for his kindness and loved him dearly. Eventually, the farmer passed away.

## The Farmer's Children Reach the Garden

Days after the funeral, the farmer's children arrived at the garden. They were not as generous or charitable as their father. They were unwilling to allow the share that was regularly given to the poor. So, one day, they gathered in the garden to make a decision. Each expressed their opinion. Some said that from now on, not a single fruit should be given to anyone, arguing that their father had no regard for the difficulties of life.

One of the children suggested that if they stopped giving what their father used to give, it would bring a bad name to the family. "When they see the harvest, they will come running. We will be forced to give them fruits and vegetables. It is better to take the produce secretly," he said.

## While the Villagers Were Asleep

Everyone agreed with that decision. However, the Merciful Lord, who generously bestows all blessings upon His servants, had made another decision.

Under the cover of night, while the villagers were asleep, the farmer's children arrived at the garden one by one. Their goal was to pick all the fruits and vegetables secretly and take them to the market.

But when they reached the garden, they could not believe their eyes. They stood there, stunned and bewildered. Not a single fruit remained. The garden was completely empty, as if everything had been stripped away!

It was then that they realized their decision was wrong. They asked Allah for forgiveness for their mistake.

## The Blessings of the Lord of the Worlds

Human beings cannot permanently possess the knowledge, health, wealth, prosperity, and peace that we receive as blessings from the Lord of the Worlds. Everything is solely by the grace of the Lord. Therefore, be grateful to Him. He will give abundantly to those who are grateful.

> "Indeed, We have tried them as We tried the companions of the garden, when they swore to cut its fruit in the morning, without making any exception. Then there came upon it a visitation from your Lord while they were asleep. And it became as if it were reaped. Then they called to one another at morning, 'Go early to your crop if you would cut the fruit.' So they set out, whispering to one another, 'There shall not enter it today upon you any poor person.' And they went early in determination, capable of preventing. But when they saw it, they said, 'Indeed, we are lost; rather, we have been deprived.' The most moderate of them said, 'Did I not say to you, why do you not exalt Allah?' They said, 'Exalted is our Lord! Indeed, we were wrongdoers.' Then they approached one another, blaming each other. They said, 'O woe to us! Indeed, we were transgressors. Perhaps our Lord will substitute for us [one] better than it; indeed, we are toward our Lord desirous.'" (Al-Qalam 17-32).

This story is cited by Allah in the Holy **Surah Al-Qalam**, verses 17 to 32.
