Xukunka Babri

Labaatan iyo shan sano ayaa laga joogaa markii Masjidka Babri, oo ahaa astaan u taagan cilmaaniyadda Hindiya, ay burburiyeen koox xagjir ah oo ku gabanaya magaca 'karseva' ama adeegga diinta. Iyadoo Maxkamadda Sare ay qorsheynayso inay dib u dhageysato dacwadda shirqoolka ee ka dhanka ah hoggaamiyeyaasha maskaxda ka ahaa burburinta sida L. K. Advani, Murli Manohar Joshi, Kalyan Singh, iyo Uma Bharti, waxaan halkan ku soo bandhigaynaa baaritaan ku saabsan arrintan.

Marka laga soo tago dadkaas, waxaa dacwadda ku jiray 13 hoggaamiye oo kale oo ka tirsan xisbiga BJP. Maxkamadda hoose ee Raebareli ayaa horey u sii daysay dadkan, go’aankaas oo ay markii dambe ansixisay Maxkamadda Sare ee Lucknow. Maxkamadda Sare ee dalka ayaa hadda dib u eegis ku samaynaysa dacwaddan ka dib markii CBI ay gudbisay rafcaan sanadkii 2011, iyo codsi kale oo uu gudbiyay hawl-wadeen bulsho oo lagu magacaabo Haji Mehboob Ahmed sanadkii 2015.

Waxaa jiray laba dacwadood oo la xiriira burburintii Masjidka Babri. Dacwadda koowaad (197/1992) waxay si toos ah u khusaysay burburinta masjidka, waxaana eedaysanayaal ka ahaa ilaa laba boqol oo kun oo qof oo aan magacyadooda la aqoon. Dacwaddan oo lagu xukumi karo ugu badnaan toban sano oo xabsi ah ayaa markii hore lagu dhageysanayay maxkamadda Lucknow. Dacwadda labaad (198/1992) waxay la xiriirtay shirqoolkii lagu burburiyay masjidka. Waxay si gaar ah u khusaysay dadkii goobta joogay ee shirqoolka ka qaybqaatay sida Advani, Kalyan Singh, Joshi, Uma Bharti, Vinay Katiyar, iyo Ashok Singhal. Eedeynta ugu culus ee dacwaddan, oo lagu xukumi karo ilaa shan sano oo xabsi ah, ayaa ahayd in ay isku dayeen inay kiciyaan nacayb u dhexeeya qowmiyadaha diimeed, taas oo khatar gelinaysa midnimada qaranka. Dacwaddan waxaa lagu dhageysanayay maxkamadda Raebareli. 5-tii Oktoobar 1993, CBI ayaa la wareegtay labada dacwadood waxayna u gudbisay hal warqad eedaysanayaal ah. CBI waxay maxkamadda u sheegtay in dadka lagu magacaabay warqadda ay ku kulmeen si qarsoodi ah guriga Vinay Katiyar si ay u go'aansadaan burburinta masjidka. Si kastaba ha ahaatee, maxkamadda Lucknow ee Maxkamadda Sare ee Allahabad ayaa dacwaddan dib u dhigaysay iyadoo aan loo baahnayn. Bishii Febraayo 2001, maxkamaddu waxay hoos u dhigtay xawaaraha dacwadda iyadoo ku andacoonaysa cilad farsamo, iyadoo sheegtay in CBI aysan la tashan Maxkamadda Sare ee Allahabad markii ay la wareegaysay dacwadda shirqoolka ee Raebareli. Inkasta oo aysan maxkamaddu si cad u difaacin eedaysanayaasha, haddana CBI iyo dowladaha gobolka ee isbeddelayay midkoodna ma qaadin tallaabooyin lagu saxayo ciladdaas farsamo. Natiijadii, dacwaddii shirqoolka ee lambarkeedu ahaa 1980 ayaa dib loogu celiyay maxkamadda Raebareli.

Sawir astaan u ah dhismaha macbadka Ram ee Ayodhya.

Maxkamadda Lucknow waxay markii dambe dhageysatay oo kaliya dacwadda lambar 1970, taas oo khusaysay oo kaliya dadkii ka qaybqaatay burburinta masjidka, iyadoo aan lagu darin hoggaamiyeyaasha BJP. Iyadoo adeegsanaya sababtan, garsoore Srikant Shukla ayaa 24-kii Maajo 2001 ka noqday dacwaddii ka dhanka ahayd Advani iyo kuwa kale. In kasta oo CBI ay gudbisay rafcaan, haddana 20-kii Maajo 2010, Maxkamadda Sare ee Allahabad waxay ansixisay go’aankii Shukla. Tani waxay keentay in 11-kii Febraayo 2011, CBI ay tallaabo adag ka qaaddo Maxkamadda Sare. CBI waxay ku dooday in Srikant Shukla uu sameeyay kala qaybin macmal ah oo u dhexeysa dacwadaha la xiriira burburinta masjidka. Waxay ku doodeen inuu isku dayay inuu hoggaamiyeyaasha BJP ka badbaadiyo dacwadda shirqoolka isagoo ku andacoonaya inaysan si toos ah uga qaybqaadan burburinta. Waxay ka codsadeen Maxkamadda Sare inay abuurto jawi labada dacwadoodba loo wada dhageysto. CBI waxay maxkamadda u sheegtay: 'Maadaama kicinta dambiga, fulinta dambiga, burburinta masjidka, iyo weerarrada joogtada ah ee ka dhanka ah saxafiyiinta ay yihiin arrimo isku xiran, ma jirto wax caqabad ah oo ka hor taagan in dacwad loo wada furo sida uu dhigayo qodobka 120 B ee Xeerka Ciqaabta Hindiya. Dacwadaha waa in la wada dhageystaa maadaama ay jiraan dambiyo isdaba joog ah. Suurtagal maaha in caddaymaha loo kala qaybiyo laba maxkamadood oo kala duwan.'

6-dii Maarso ee la soo dhaafay, Maxkamadda Sare ayaa ka hadashay rafcaankii CBI. Maxkamaddu waxay sheegtay in go’aankii maxkamadda hoose ee ku saabsanaa baabi’inta dacwadda shirqoolka ee ku salaysan sababo farsamo uusan ahayn mid sharci ah. Garsoorayaasha R. F. Nariman iyo P. C. Ghose ayaa amar ku bixiyay in CBI ay gudbiso warqad eedaysanayaal cusub oo ka dhan ah dhammaan dadka ku lugta leh. In kasta oo dacwadda loo qorsheeyay 22-kii Maarso, haddana waxaa loo beddelay 6-da Abriil sababo la xiriira maqnaanshaha garsoore Nariman.

Qareenka hoggaamiyeyaasha BJP, K. K. Venugopal, ayaa si adag uga dooday in aan la keenin warqad eedaysanayaal cusub, laakiin garsoore Nariman ma aqbalin. 'Ma aqbali karno in dadka lagu eedeeyay dambiga la sii daayo iyadoo aan la eegin caddaymaha. Maxaa lagu sii daayay? Sababaha farsamo marnaba ma noqon karaan marmarsiiyo lagu sii daayo dadka dambiga galay,' ayuu yiri garsoore Nariman. Neeraj Kishan Kaul, oo ah qareenka guud ee dheeraadka ah, ayaa matalayay CBI.

Maanta, Uma Bharti waa wasiir federaal ah, Kalyan Singhna waa guddoomiyaha gobolka Rajasthan. Murli Manohar Joshi wuxuu isu diyaarinayaa inuu noqdo madaxweynaha dalka ka dib Pranab Mukherjee. Joshi waa qofka ugu horreeya liiska madaxweynaha ee uu diyaariyay BJP. Haddii xukunku noqdo mid ka dhan ah, riyada Joshi ee ah inuu noqdo madaxweyne ma rumoobi doonto. Uma Bharti iyo Kalyan Singh ayaa sidoo kale laga yaabaa inay iska casilaan xilalkooda.

Iyadoo doodahani socdaan, hoggaamiyaha BJP Subramanian Swamy ayaa u tagay Maxkamadda Sare isagoo dalbanaya in la oggolaado dhismaha macbadka Ram. Garsoore J. S. Khehar ayaa soo jeediyay in arrinta lagu xalliyo dhexdhexaadin ka baxsan maxkamadda. 'Ha u arkin amarka maxkamadda, u arag talo. Maadaama arrintu ay tahay mid xasaasi ah oo dareen leh, waxaan wada fariisan karnaa dhammaan dhinacyada si aan u helno xal macquul ah,' ayuu yiri garsoore Khehar.

Ururada cilmaaniyiinta ayaa guud ahaan diiday fikradda Khehar. Hogaamiyeyaasha sida Dr. S.Q.R. Ilyas, xoghayaha guud ee Muslim League E. T. Mohammed Basheer, xubin ka tirsan guddiga sharciga Muslimiinta Maulana Khalid Rashid, iyo Sunni Waqf Board ayaa diiday talada maxkamadda. Hogaamiyeyaashu waxay sheegeen in maxkamaddu ay tahay inay xukunka ku riddo caddaymaha horyaalla ee la xiriira lahaanshaha dhulka. Xoghayaha guud ee CPI(M) Sitaram Yechury ayaa sidoo kale sheegay in dacwadda sharciga ah ay u baahan tahay xukun maxkamadeed.

30-kii Sebtembar 2010, Maxkamadda Sare ee Allahabad ayaa soo saartay xukun muhiim ah oo ku saabsan lahaanshaha dhulka uu masjidka Babri ku yaallay. Maxkamaddu waxay xukuntay in dhulka lagu kala qaybiyo saddex qaybood oo loo siman yahay oo la siiyo Hindu Mahasabha, Sunni Waqf Board, iyo Nirmohi Akhara. Si kastaba ha ahaatee, xukunkan laguma qancin Hindu Mahasabha iyo Sunni Waqf Board. Waxay u gudbiyeen Maxkamadda Sare. 9-kii Maajo 2011, Maxkamadda Sare ayaa joojisay xukunka Maxkamadda Allahabad waxayna amartay in xaaladdu ay ahaato sidii ay ahayd. Maxkamadda Sare ayaa xustay in xukunka Maxkamadda Allahabad uu ahaa mid layaab leh maadaama aysan jirin cid codsatay qaybinta dhulka.

Socdaalkii gaadhiga ee Advani wuxuu dhex maray wadnaha dalka. In kasta oo Lalu Prasad iyo Mulayam Singh ay isku dayeen inay joojiyaan socdaalkaas, haddana Advani wuxuu ku guuleystay inuu kiciyo dareenka Hinduuga. Wareysi uu siiyay India Today ka hor 'karseva', Advani wuxuu sheegay in karseva aysan ahayn oo kaliya heeso iyo cibaado, balse ay noqon doonto mid lagu isticmaalo leben iyo qalab kale. Vinay Katiyar, oo ah hoggaamiyaha Bajrang Dal oo sugaya xukunka, ayaa markaas yiri: 'Xitaa haddii sharcigu joojiyo ama maxkamaddu xukunto, karseva way dhici doontaa.' Hadal ka sii daran ayaa ka soo yeeray hoggaamiyaha VHP Ashok Singhal: 'Haddii la joojiyo karsevayaasha, halkan waxay noqon doontaa cadaab.'

Toddobaadyada soo socda ayaa goob joog u noqon doona dacwado iyo xukunno muhiim ah oo la xiriira Masjidka Babri. Xaaladda ay dowladdu si joogto ah u beegsanayso dadka laga tirada badan yahay, caddaaladda maxkamadda ayaa ah rajada kaliya ee dadka laga tirada badan yahay. 6-dii Diseembar waa maalin madow oo taariikhda adduunka ah. Waa maalin Hindiya ay ku qasbanaatay inay madaxa hoos u dhigto adduunka hortiisa. Tani waa fursad lagu soo celin karo wejiga cilmaaniyadda ee dalkeena. In la sheegto goob cibaado oo aan lahayn wax caddayn taariikhi ah, in la dhigo sanamyo habeenkii, in la burburiyo goobta cibaadada, iyo in la kiciyo rabshado dalka oo dhan waa wax aan la aqbali karin. Waxaan u baahanahay inaan ka hortagno xaaladda fowdada ah ee beddeshay sharciga, iyadoo aan loo eegin qowmiyad, diin, ama siyaasad. Waa inaan soo celinnaa sharaftii dalka si aan madaxa kor ugu qaadno adduunka hortiisa.

Dadka dambiga galay waa in la ciqaabaa.
