## Dayaxa bisha Ramadaan oo cirka ka muuqda

Dayaxa bisha Ramadaan oo cirka ka muuqda

Maalmihii soo dhowaaday, farxaddu waa sida hirarka badda

Bishii fadliga ubaxay, bishii camalka wanaagsan kordhay

Bishii cibaadada ka buuxday, bishii **Quraanka** la soo dejiyay

Caalamka mu'miniinta ayaa soo dhoweynaya 'Ahlan Ramadaan'

Quluubta raadinaysa nasteexada ayaa leh 'Ahlan Ramadaan'

## Ducada aan u jeedinayno Rabbiga

Rabbi, adiga oo ah badda naxariista, anagoo ilmaynayna

Waxaan hortaada ka raadinaynaa inaan helno fadliga Ramadaan

Waxaan indhaheenna ku haynaa iridda loo yaqaan Rayyaan

Rabbi, miyaadan noo furayn iriddaas?

Rabbi, fadlan na sii ajarka soonka, taasi waa baryadayada

Ma jiro cid aan adiga ahayn oo noo gargaari karta

Qalbiyadeenna noogu qurxi **Taqwa** ama cabsida Alle

Na sii gargaar aan ku fahanno macnaha Quraanka

Alle, na sii meel aan ku nagaano Jannatul Firdows

Adiga mooyee cid kale kama magan galno naarta
