# Terrorism Hunt and Religious Freedom

The article begins with the idea that terrorism is a mental perversion that disrupts the peace of any society. It analyzes the relationship between terrorism and religious freedom.

![A person holding a sign that says "Stop Terrorism"](/assets/terrorism-and-religious-freedom-20170114-1/14Jan17-12.jpg)

## Terrorism is a mental perversion that disrupts the peace of any society

Terrorism is a mental perversion that disrupts the peace of any society. Its hallmark is the production of hatred, wholesale and retail. Terrorist activities are carried out in the world in the name of religion, regionalism, and ethnicity. It is in the context of global efforts against terrorism that discussions about the hunt for terrorists are heating up in our country.

## Intelligence Systems and Wrongful Detentions

We, the residents of this country, who possess an intelligence system comparable to global standards, have much to be proud of. Those responsible can indeed put handcuffs on anyone who stands as an obstacle to peace and national security, if they make sincere efforts. However, what we see today is the detention of individuals who have not even come close to terrorist activities, branded as terrorists.

## Sangh Parivar Agenda Behind the Hunt for Terrorists

Amidst this, there have been attempts by law enforcement officials to single out and attack the Muslim community under the guise of hunting terrorists. Perhaps because the central government is in the hands of **Narendra Modi**, under whose shadow the Gujarat genocide attempt looms, his followers are spewing venom against Muslims. It is also to be suspected that some officials, at government expense, have taken it upon themselves to implement the **Sangh Parivar** agenda under the name of hunting terrorists.

## Islamophobia and Dr. Zakir Naik

Under the guise of hunting terrorists, some scholars are making insidious attempts to curtail religious freedom, driven by the existence of Islamophobia. The increasing acceptance of Islam, which is natural, is disturbing the sleep of many. This might be why **Dr. Zakir Naik**, who has brought India's pride to the pinnacle of the world, has been targeted. For over two decades, he has been traveling the world spreading the message of Islam, and even his enemies have not accused him of terrorism or extremism during this time. It is noteworthy that the spiritual guru **Sri Sri Ravi Shankar** himself engaged in a friendly dialogue with Dr. Zakir and praised his abilities on several occasions. This is not to argue that this Marathi doctor is free from human flaws or slips of the tongue. The challenge remains that not a single call for extremist or terrorist activities can be found by searching his hundreds of speeches.

## From Chhota Bachchan's Propaganda to Actions

How quickly did the Sangh's inquisition and the accompanying state terror unfold! If the fabricated propaganda initiated by the **Chhota Bachchan** leaders has escalated to police action and the closure of channels and other institutions run by Dr. Naik, then it is time for India's secular conscience to awaken.

## Attempt to Suppress Islamic Preaching

It is becoming increasingly clear that the Sangh Parivar, at government expense, is implementing a hidden agenda to prevent Islamic preaching and propagation under the pretext of hunting terrorists. The latest example of this is **M.M. Akbar**. It should not be forgotten that many Malayalis understood the danger of extremism through the book *The Way Fascism Comes* written by M.M. Akbar. There are planned efforts to chain influential Islamic speakers and preachers, who intellectually attack communalism and terrorism, one by one. The fact is that the enemies did not start trying to suppress the growth of Islam by silencing preachers yesterday or today. However, history tells us that all such attempts have turned out to be mere delusions.

## Time to Stand Together

The situation where those who preach and practice religion may be branded as terrorists sooner or later must never be allowed. It is high time for those active in religious, political, and cultural spheres, and all Muslim organizations, to come forward unitedly with strong protests against this double standard. It is the duty of every citizen to protect the pillars of democracy and secularism from being shaken.
